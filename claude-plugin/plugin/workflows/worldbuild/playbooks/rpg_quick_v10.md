## Interview And Owners

Settle the play language first (router, Play Language). The short premise
collected before depth belongs to row 1; inherit it without asking again.
Show progress through the router's player-facing sections, not
internal row labels. Ask at most one unresolved consequential decision per
response. Accept volunteered answers across rows; keep pending suggestions,
uncertainty, rejection, and corrections distinct in the existing decision log.

For semantic guidance and owners, read only the corresponding part of
`rpg_quick.md`: v10 rows 1/2/3/4 map to its slots 1/4/2/3; rows 5–8 map directly.
Its fixed ten-slot accounting, early-character inference, and split preparation
approval do not apply. This playbook owns the new order and completion contract.

Set content limits (`content_boundaries.md`), Player authorship, consequence
ceiling, and world creation authority before proposing protected character
truth. They are independent choices even inside one completion row. Safety
remains a separate question; a bundle never grants safety, source, or
protected-character permission. Row 4 also settles the NPC power scale before
any face is named, and the thin world's truths include the price and bounty
anchor table (router).

Ask for the character's useful seed and volunteered surface, not a mandatory
height/weight inventory. Unspecified bodily facts remain unspecified and create
no invented advantage, impediment, or NPC judgment. Ask only when the Player
cares or an actual upcoming dependency needs the answer. Establish what they
want tested and what must be protected; never infer that permission from tone,
stats, or campaign promise. An explicit "discover later" records no directed
personal test yet: that bounded permission stance can resolve the area while
the character detail stays open. Mere uncertainty is not that acceptance and
never authorizes imposed personal pressure.

Build a thin causal world from the accepted premise/research before offering
world-dependent competence. Record protected character decisions without
inventing social history before the world supports it. System choices then
inherit competence, limits, and grounding. Explain tracking through its effect
on choices and only enable explicitly approved modules. If resolution remains
unclear, show one short optional out-of-play example of routine competence and
genuine resistance before expanding ecology: no actual roll, resource spend,
character action, or continuity write. It stays inside the owning decision.

Optional Dashboard, images, World Voices, and Agent Seats remain independent.
State what is available and what it costs; absent consent leaves each off.
Do not interview disabled features' subordinate settings. Offer the Turn
Protocol and its timing/freshness tradeoff explicitly.

The relationship decision includes the necessary starting access/travel
contract: distance cost, known versus discovered routes, and whether movement
leaves an actionable trace, only where relevant. The Player chooses those
consequences; the coordinator supplies edges, routines, and causal detail.
Neither a rejected experience nor a deferred proposal becomes a dormant hook
by default. Realize only enough world/cast detail to support meaningful choices;
there is no minimum roster, side-condition, or counter-current count.

Persist accepted semantic owners, the readable decision summary, and applicable
row status on the acceptance turn. Each accepted decision/revision advances
`setup_revision` and synchronizes the active profile's `source_setup_revision`.
Do not run a full check after each answer.

## Review And Readiness

Row 8 shows the coherent two-way design, consequential defaults/deferrals,
protected choices, and why the opening belongs to this character while the
world remains independent. Acceptance sets `design_direction_approved_revision`
to the resulting revision. Materialize the approved opening scale while not
ready, using the shared Required Reciprocity Core without its detail quotas.

Cross-read actual owners before the final display. Use
`preparation_review_v9.md` for the same combined factual review/readiness
acceptance, recorded in v10 row 9. Show a few relevant accepted choices and
their actual player-safe affordances; do not promise outcomes or reveal secrets.
Internally check that refusal, ordinary activity, and leaving the opening remain
causally playable, without authoring mandatory branches.

Readiness requires all nine rows locked, real required owners, reviewed
defaults, earlier design approval, and preparation approval at the current
revision; it never depends on a numeric decision total. Use `finalization.md`
for preflight, profile locks, approved projections, snapshot, and aggregate
check. A design correction invalidates both approvals; preparation-only changes
invalidate the latter. Preserve unaffected accepted answers and never recount
revisions as new decisions. No unchanged preparation receives a second approval.
