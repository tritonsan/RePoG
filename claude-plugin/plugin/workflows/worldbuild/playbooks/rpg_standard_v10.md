## Dependency-Guided Interview

Read only the corresponding semantic module and owner mapping in
`rpg_standard_deep.md` for rows 1–19. Its quotas, Deep pack procedure, mandatory
physical questionnaire, and separate module-20/21 acceptance do not apply.
Independent questions remain independent; there is no reason to bundle play
mix and pace to meet a count. Rows express coverage, not rigid question order.

Settle the play language first (router, Play Language). Row 3's content
boundary follows `content_boundaries.md`. Ask the NPC power scale before row 14
names any face, and write the price and bounty anchor table with row 10's world
truths (router).

Inherit the pre-depth premise and all early answers. Resolve source permission
before source-dependent options and authorship before protected character
proposals. Establish a world-independent character seed, then enough accepted
world operating truth to ground competence and social access before detailed
ecology. Thus a needed portion of row 10 can precede world-dependent work in
row 5; finish its remaining detail later without asking an accepted choice again.
Do not materialize dependent truth while its inputs remain unresolved.

Appearance begins with what the Player wants established. Height, weight, and
complete physical cards are optional until requested or genuinely needed by an
upcoming dependency. Unknown facts remain unknown and justify no invented
advantage, impediment, or NPC reaction. Ask what the Player wants tested and
protected explicitly; tone and numerical weakness cannot supply that permission.

Cards describe effects on play: for example, counted provisions constrain what
the character can do on a journey. Translate the accepted effects into the
approved modules and required tracking, without adding unapproved mechanics.
If the Player is unsure, an optional short out-of-play resolution example can
show routine competence, actual resistance, and the role of a recorded roll
before ecology expands. No roll, resource, character choice, continuity change,
or additional approval occurs in that example.

Row 11 owns the Standard travel choices: whether distance costs anything,
whether routes begin known or are discovered, and whether movement leaves a
trace others can act on, when relevant to accepted play. It does not delegate
them to an inactive Deep pack. Explain preparation beyond the starting aperture
without promising verified content that does not exist. The coordinator derives
individual route edges from the accepted travel and source contracts.

Distinguish rejected content, unselected alternatives, and deliberate deferrals.
Preserve each in its existing owner with its scope; do not revive rejected
experience as a renamed hook. Build only causally needed actors, movements,
places, and exceptions. No roster, side-condition, counter-current, or act-size
quota is a readiness requirement. A faction row may resolve as no relevant
coordinated faction; a small act may be valid when its question is playable.

Optional layers receive independent consent and capability/cost disclosure;
disabled features receive no subordinate interview. Turn Protocol remains an
explicit choice. Apply the router's early-answer, uncertainty, conflict,
fatigue, pause/resume, and local-versus-persistent correction rules.

Persist accepted semantic owners and the selected row status in the acceptance
turn, advance `setup_revision` per accepted decision/revision, and synchronize
the active profile. Do not add a full check after every answer.

## Approval And Finalization

Row 19 reviews the coherent design and consolidated consequential defaults and
deferrals. Acceptance sets `design_direction_approved_revision` at its resulting
revision. Materialize the opening scale while not ready, then cross-read owners.
Internally check meaningful engagement, refusal, ordinary activity, and leaving
the opening without scripting four future branches or forcing the same hook.

Row 20 follows `preparation_review_v9.md` using the v10 status heading. Show
actual player-safe preparation and the concrete affordances created by relevant
accepted choices, never guaranteed success or hidden truth. The same acceptance
records factual review and preparation approval at the current revision.

All twenty rows must be locked, with required owners and reviewed defaults;
design approval must precede current preparation approval. No minimum or maximum
decision count substitutes for these conditions. Use `finalization.md` after
acceptance; no module 21 or second acceptance of unchanged preparation exists.
Design changes invalidate both approvals, preparation-only changes the latter.
Revise affected owners/dependencies while preserving unaffected answers.
