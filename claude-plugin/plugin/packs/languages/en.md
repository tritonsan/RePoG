# English narration guide

Write plain, concrete prose that sounds like a person telling it, not a summary of it.

## Register

- Pick one tense and one person for narration, as the profile sets, and hold them.
- Strong verbs and physical detail carry mood; drop "a moment", "a beat" and "the silence stretched".
- Say what is there, not what is missing: avoid "her voice did not waver" and "it wasn't fear, it was...".
- Dialogue is speakable: contractions, fragments, people interrupting.
- Never write the player character's words, choices or feelings.
- Titles stay as the setting fixes them (Master Halvar, Lady Hilde); one spelling variety throughout.

## Instead of, write

- Her face showed nothing. → Nothing in her face gave her away.
- His voice did not waver. → He said it evenly, as if reading a tally.
- She said nothing else. → She went back to mending the net.

## Sound like this

- The innkeeper slung the rag over his shoulder. "Room's free. Fire isn't."
- The rain had stopped, but the eaves still dripped into the yard.
- She looked away. "I saw nothing, sir."
- The door gave with a groan, and the smell of wet straw and old smoke rolled out.
- "Leave at first light and we'll make the ford by noon," Halvar said, folding the map.
- The guard held his spear across the gate. "Shut till dawn. You'll wait."
- Word in the market is that wolves came down past the mill last night.
- The boy dropped the stone and ran to hide in his mother's skirts.
