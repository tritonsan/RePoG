# Player Character

## Name, Alias, And Concept

Mira is a boat repairer familiar with the public workshop.

## Player Experience Contract

- Desired player fantasy: useful craft with independent people.
- Activities the Player wants spotlighted: patient practical teaching and mutual learning in the next part of play. Quiet inquiry remains welcome; it has not been retired.
- Accepted basis: an explicit ongoing OOC direction after the first repair shift, supplied as synthetic fixture truth.
- Decisions the Player wants to own: whether to teach, investigate, talk, wait, or leave; all conclusions and commitments.
- Experiences to avoid: mandatory guild or committee politics.

## Change Appetite

- Parts the Player wants tested: explaining skilled work to someone with another perspective.
- Parts that must not be touched: family bonds must not become betrayal tests.
- Identity elements that must not be overwritten: the character's ordinary self-chosen trade.

## Authorship Boundaries

Do not invent family facts, trauma, hidden patrons, political memberships, inner
feelings, conclusions, or voluntary actions. Existing workshop access does not
mean Mira accepts today's invitation.

## Competence–Limitation–Cost–Counterplay

Ordinary boat repair and tool care are reliable. Teaching is an opportunity to
demonstrate a known skill, not guaranteed rapport or obedience. Mira has no
automatic power to persuade Ece to abandon paid work.

## Capability Anchors For Play

- Routine: recognize and shape an ordinary removable wooden guide safely.
- Contested: winning another person's time when obligations conflict.
- Out of reach: knowing hidden motives or unfamiliar causes without evidence.
