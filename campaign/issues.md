# Issues

Campaign id: `new_campaign`

Issues are active pressures, not plot scripts. They tell Codex what is moving
in the world if the player does nothing.

A Player choosing which issues matter sets salience, not exclusivity. Candidates
they did not choose stay as dormant domains in `world_dynamics.md`, recorded with
the trigger that would surface them, so nothing is deleted and nothing is forced.

Every current issue carries three or four side conditions: secondary effects,
second-order consequences, and the kinds of change that would shift its direction.
These are material to improvise from, not a plot. Each also carries one
counter-current—something that looks like it contradicts the issue yet fits this
world, such as a decent officer inside a corrupt branch, or a predator who happens
to be shielding someone from something worse. A world whose every force points the
same way leaves nothing to play against.

Never record a predetermined resolution. An issue's direction stays open until play
decides it.

This file owns the durable definition of a systemic problem: what is wrong,
who is affected, and the broad escalation implied by inaction. It does not own
the live scene, the player's dramatic question, a faction's stable identity,
or the latest evaluated offscreen move. Those belong respectively to
`current_state.yaml`, `threads.md`, faction notes, and `world_dynamics.md`.
