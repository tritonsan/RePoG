# Glossary

Optional. How recurring terms are rendered in the play language: titles and
honorifics, units of time, distance, weight, and money, place names, and forms
of address. Narration uses the preferred form. A listed variant that appears
instead is reported to the GM after the reply, and the address and title notes
below are shown to the GM with the language guidance.

This file governs wording only. Who holds a title, where a place lies, and what
a character knows stay with their owners. Leave the file unfilled when the
campaign has no recurring rendering choices.

Seed it at the first translated name: the first time a place name, title, rank,
or recurring term is rendered into the play language, add its row in that same
commit, so later replies keep one form. A writer warning `glossary_suggest`
offers a row for a new note whose name is not in the play language; fill its
`Preferred` with the play-language form before committing it.

## Rendering Policy

Fill only the lines the campaign has decided, with a sentence or two kept on
the field's own line. `Place names` says whether names keep their original
form, are translated by meaning, or are adapted to the play language.
`Titles and honorifics` and `Address forms` say which titles exist and how
characters address the player's character and one another, including when a
formal or familiar form applies.
`Units` lists the setting's units of time, distance, weight, and money, so
narration does not slip into units the setting would not use.

## Terms

One row per term. `Term` names the concept or its original form. `Preferred`
is the form narration uses, in the play language. `Variants` lists spellings or
renderings to avoid, separated by commas. `Notes` says when the term applies,
what it must not be confused with, or why a variant is wrong, such as an
anachronism. Keep `|` out of every cell; it would split the row.
