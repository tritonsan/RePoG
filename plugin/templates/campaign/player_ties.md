# PC Integration / Player Ties

Use this file for the two-way relationship between the player character and the
initial world. It owns neither generic character identity (`player.md`) nor the
world's independent truth (`world.md`, `issues.md`, and `world_dynamics.md`);
it records how those authorities meet.

Keep accepted tie origins, personal meaning, integration permissions, and
do-not-use conditions here. Changing NPC identity/routine belongs in its note,
present location/activity in `active_cast.md`, knowledge in
`knowledge_boundaries.md`, current relationships in `relationship_map.md`, and
shared motion in `world_dynamics.md`. Use precise owner references below rather
than parallel live accounts. Existing setup descriptions are historical evidence,
not substitutes for reading those owners; do not invent history while converting
a duplicate to a reference. The GM reads relevant premises at scene/act preparation.
