# Session 0 Content Step

Load for the content-boundary decision in every route: Deep v8
`01_content_boundaries`, schema-v10 Quick row 2 and Standard row 3, the
content part of every earlier slot or module, and Companion's relationship and
boundaries decision. The answers go to `boundaries.md` / Content Boundaries.

## 1. Show The Notice

Before asking, show the notice below once, in the play language, as its own
short block. Use the Turkish or English text as written; for another play
language, translate the English faithfully without softening it. It is shown
here and summarized in two sentences when the campaign is created; it is not
repeated at later openings.

**Türkçe:**

> **Oynamadan önce.** RePoG yalnızca 18 yaş üstü içindir ve kullandığın yapay zekâ aracındaki kendi hesabınla çalışır. O sağlayıcının kullanım politikası oturumdaki her şeye uygulanır: yalnızca GM'in yazdıklarına değil, senin isteklerine ve OOC notlarına da. Session 0'daki içerik ayarları birer tercihtir. "Açık" seçmek, açık cinsel anlatımın serbest olduğu anlamına gelmez. Yakınlık sahneleri ima yoluyla yazılır ve eylemin kendisinde sahne kesilir. 18 yaşından küçük biriyle ilgili cinsel içerik, geçmiş hikâyede bile olsa, hiçbir koşulda yazılmaz. Karanlık temalar (şiddet, ölüm, suç) yazılabilir ama ölçülü anlatılır. Bir yanıt engellenirse bu tek başına ihlal sayılmaz. Aynı isteği zorlamak yerine aracının geri alma komutuyla geri dön ya da yeni bir oturum aç. Açık cinsel içerik için tekrar tekrar ısrar etmek uyarıya, geçici olarak sıkılaştırılmış filtrelere ve sonunda hesabın askıya alınmasına yol açabilir. Kesin eşikler yayımlanmış değildir. Başka araçlarla ürettiğin görseller o aracın sağlayıcısının kurallarına ve oradaki hesabına tabidir.

**English:**

> **Before you play.** RePoG is for adults 18+ only and runs on your own account with the AI tool you use. That provider's usage policy applies to everything in the session, including your own requests and OOC notes, not only the GM's replies. The content settings in Session 0 are preferences. Choosing "open" does not unlock explicit sexual writing: intimate scenes are written by implication and cut away at the act itself. Sexual content involving anyone under 18 is never written, not even in a backstory. Dark themes (violence, death, crime) can be written, with restraint. If a reply is blocked, that alone is not a violation; undo it with your tool's rewind command or start a new session instead of pushing the same request. Repeatedly pushing for explicit sexual content can lead to warnings, temporarily stricter filters and, eventually, suspension of your account. No exact thresholds are published. Images you make with other tools follow that tool's provider rules and your account there.

## 2. Ask The Boundary On Its Own

Ask it alone, never inside a style, bundle, or profile card, and never let
accepting a card set it. Show the table in the Player's language, in this order:

| Row | Options |
| --- | --- |
| Anyone under 18 in a sexual or romantic context, including backstory | fixed: never |
| Graphic violence against children on the page | fixed: never |
| Sexual content | avoid / fade before it starts / implied, no explicit description |
| Harm to children | mentioned or offscreen / avoid |
| Torture and graphic violence | open / fade / avoid |
| Harm to animals | open / fade / avoid |
| Suicide and self-harm | open / fade / avoid |
| Severe humiliation or discrimination | open / fade / avoid |
| Addiction | open / fade / avoid |
| Illness, dying, and medical detail | open / fade / avoid |
| Captivity and slavery | open / fade / avoid |

- The two fixed rows are shown as fixed, not offered as choices. The Player
  cannot change them, and no later answer, directive, or OOC request does.
- There is no `open` or "described directly" option for sexual content.
  "Everything at its most open" is a real answer: it sets each changeable row
  to its most open option and leaves the fixed rows as they are.
- State the GM's own limits in the same message, at least: non-consensual acts
  are never written as scenes. Record what you stated under
  `Limits the GM stated`; a limit said in chat and not recorded does not exist
  next session.
- Offer an optional in-fiction stop word: a word the Player can say inside the
  story to end the moment at once. Record it, or `none`.
- Say both guarantees out loud: a limit may be added at any time, including
  mid-play, and takes effect immediately; adding one is never a cost or a
  penalty. An open-ended "anything else?" is not enough on its own, because a
  blank answer would read as consent.

## 3. Record It

Write the answers to `boundaries.md` / Content Boundaries in the acceptance
turn. Keep the Category words and the Setting option words exactly as the
template writes them, so the Setting cell holds one listed option (for example
`fade before it starts`); write the Notes cells in the records language. Keep
both `never (fixed)` rows. Additional Player limits go in the list below the
table.

On the Deep ledger, `complete-stage 01_north_star_authority` refuses while the
fixed rows are missing or the Sexual content or Harm to children setting is not
one of its listed options. Other routes check the same rows by hand before
their review.
