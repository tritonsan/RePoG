## Approval And Finalization

Module 19 approves the complete design at its resulting setup revision after
review of consequential defaults, before actual preparation. Materialize the
opening scale using the shared modules' authority and cast/space responsibilities,
while setup remains not ready. Do not add a hidden script or mandatory quest.

Module 20 uses `preparation_review_v9.md`: show the audited actual preparation
and complete default/deferral record, then ask for corrections or lock/start.
The same acceptance records the factual review and current-revision preparation
approval. There is no module 21 and no extra go/no-go over unchanged material.

Load `finalization.md` only after this combined acceptance. Its substantive
content freeze, preflight, profile locks, approved projections, snapshot,
aggregate check, and failure behavior remain mandatory. Design changes clear
both approvals; preparation changes clear the latter, without recounting an
already completed decision.
