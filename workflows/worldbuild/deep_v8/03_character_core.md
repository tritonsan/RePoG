# 03 — Character Core

## Purpose

Establish the person the world must answer before building the living ecology.
Keep this a playable seed, not a complete biography.

## Decision Order

1. `03_identity_seed` — name or naming stance, concept, self-definition, and
   the premise-level identity that does not require a finished society or
   ruleset.
2. `03_current_desire_and_why_now` — immediate desire and why action matters at
   the opening scale now.
3. `03_tested_direction_and_protected_growth` — what aspect of the character
   the Player wants play to test, and what traits or outcomes must not be used
   as imposed pressure.
4. `03_protected_past_and_relationship_authorship` — apply Stage 1 authorship;
   record what is protected and what may be connected later.

Ask about character truth, never how every NPC should react. The coordinator may
derive bounded public assumptions, but records them as derived rather than
Player-authored interior truth.

## Controlled Extensions

Record `character.deep_personal_integration`, `character.long_horizon`, or
`character.dense_originated_anchors` only when the accepted answer directly
supports it. These tags may activate `character_embedding` later; they do not
authorize hidden backstory.

Activate `character_interior` only from its own manifest tags when inner life,
formative history, or a deep change arc is explicitly load-bearing. It may add
contradiction, fear of loss, private routine, chosen formative pressure, and a
change line. Under Player-owned authorship the Player supplies interior truth;
the coordinator only structures and persists it.

## Materialization Boundary

Critical character, authorship, desire, and protected-fact owners are written in
their acceptance turns. Before stage completion, materialize the compact,
world-independent seed, current motivation, desired test line, and open
load-bearing questions in `character_foundation.md`. Do not materialize the
playable `player.md` card yet. Appearance as read by this society, social
access, and world-specific role wait for Stage 5. Complete or explicitly
default `character_interior` before closing the stage when it is `active`.

## Do Not

- invent an origin tie merely to make the world feel connected;
- decide competence or mechanics before Stage 5;
- create the opening, future arc, or required relationship.
