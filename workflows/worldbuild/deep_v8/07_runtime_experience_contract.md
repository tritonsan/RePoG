# 07 — Runtime Experience Contract

## Purpose

Lock how accepted material feels and operates at the table. This stage owns
runtime and presentation behavior, not new world truth.

## Decision Order

1. `07_play_mix_and_failure_stance` — setting-specific recurring activities,
   challenge rhythm, and partial/redirection/hard-loss failure shape within the
   Stage 1 consequence ceiling.
2. `07_narration_voice` — compare short samples from already accepted material;
   derive selectors and exactly three usable signature anchors.
3. `07_wanted_and_unwanted_gm_behavior` — record concrete behaviors in the
   Player's language, including habits rejected in the sample comparison.
4. `07_optional_layers` — Dashboard, image generation, and World Voices receive
   independent explicit on/off decisions and cost/capability disclosure.
5. `07_turn_protocol_and_performance` — Fast, Balanced, Maximum Continuity, or
   Custom; validation/distill policies, semantic parallelism, worker cap, and
   estimate acknowledgement.

Ask one decision per message. Interiority and hard boundaries inherit Stage 1;
mechanics and progression inherit Stage 5. Do not ask them again.

## Materialization Boundary

Every accepted runtime value is written immediately to `play_profile` at the
current setup revision. Explanations go to `system_fit` or `storytelling` and
must not become competing selector authorities. At stage end, validate profile
couplings, optional-layer independence, meaningful signature anchors, concrete
GM behavior lines, and acknowledged performance costs.

## Do Not

- bundle narration voice with optional feature consent;
- enable mechanics, visuals, Dashboard, or World Voices by implication;
- add world entities, opening facts, or first-act events here.
