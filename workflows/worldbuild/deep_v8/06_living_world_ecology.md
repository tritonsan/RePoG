# 06 — Living World Ecology

## Purpose

Turn the thin kernel into a small living ecology whose places, pressures,
institutions, and people have independent causality. Use the accepted topology;
do not build every domain to equal depth.

## Decision Order

1. `06_topology_activation` — confirm the Stage 1 topology against the now-known
   character, world, and play contract. A change revises Stage 1.
2. `06_salient_ecology` — choose or mix the few places, forces, roles, and
   relationships that should be live at opening scale.
3. `06_relationship_pattern` — choose only consequential known relationship
   positions; the coordinator authors independent work, obligation, routine,
   availability, and knowledge limits.

## Topology Variants

- `exploration_survival`: places/routes → pressures → institutions → NPCs.
- `political_intrigue`: pressures → institutions → reach/places →
  representatives.
- `personal_drama`: everyday places → institutions → NPC relations → pressures.
- `mixed`: complete the Stage 1 primary topology first. Add at most one secondary
  topology only after its inputs and the primary owner writes are complete.

The arrows are dependency order, not a questionnaire or story sequence.

The Stage 1 creation-authority contract remains binding. In approval-first
campaigns, the Player chooses every load-bearing institution, relationship
position, or salient place role before it is materialized; the coordinator may
still supply non-consequential connective fields. Other authority settings may
infer only within their explicitly accepted boundary and must surface ordinary
defaults at review.

## Controlled Extensions

Evaluate every listed trigger tag and resolve only manifest-authorized work:

- `location_network` owns meaningful route cost, access, visibility, knowledge,
  asymmetry, and travel availability.
- `faction_information` owns contested knowledge, reach, representatives,
  methods, mistaken beliefs, and withheld information.
- `group` owns a persistent collective's intent, cohesion, join/departure model,
  and independent member will; it never pre-fills a required roster.
- `character_embedding` owns deeper personal places, routines, obligations,
  independent relationship positions, and character-originated anchors. It may
  not invent protected past or force the world to orbit the character.

An absent trigger produces `not_applicable`. Active extension output is
materialized before stage completion; a `defaulted` extension still needs
displayed, accepted defaults and its owner writes.

## Materialization Boundary

Write critical ecology choices immediately. At stage end, materialize stable ids,
creation tiers, only needed notes, compact agency/routine cards, current
relationships, knowledge classification, plausible routes/presence, and links to
independent pressures. Named T2/T3 actors must be playable before they speak.

## Do Not

- create NPCs only to deliver hooks or factions only because the genre has them;
- let all relationships orbit the Player character;
- schedule future events or prescribe how any pressure resolves.
