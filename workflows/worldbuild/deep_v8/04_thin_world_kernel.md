# 04 — Thin World Kernel

## Purpose

Create the smallest independent causal world that can answer the character.
This stage defines how consequences arise, not an encyclopedia or cast roster.

## Decision Order

1. `04_world_operating_model` — governing cause/effect truths, beneficiaries,
   costs, exceptions, and hard limits.
2. `04_macro_frame_and_local_channels` — large-scale power and the channels by
   which it reaches the starting slice.
3. `04_starting_aperture` — tight or wide live scope and what happens when the
   Player reaches beyond it.
4. `04_world_palette` — accept, change, or defer the elements, motifs, powers,
   social textures, and genre habits that belong, stay out, or require renewed
   approval inside the already locked Stage 1 limits.
5. `04_independent_pressures` — problems or domains that move without the
   character, with visible signs and counter-currents.
6. `04_everyday_life_and_open_unknowns` — ordinary routines and uncertainty
   worth preserving, not filling.

Present coherent causal alternatives for Player choice. The coordinator
realizes subordinate fields; do not interview through society/economy/law schema
headings.

## Creation Authority Gate

Read the locked `01_creation_authority` decision before proposing or writing
world truth. Under `approval-first`, no model-authored load-bearing ontology,
institution, pressure, or social rule becomes truth until the Player selects or
accepts it. Under bounded inference, derive only connective detail inside the
accepted campaign promise, research boundary, and authorship limits and mark
ordinary alternatives as visible defaults. Broad world-side preparation still
cannot cross safety, canon, research, or protected-character boundaries.

## Controlled Extension

Activate `world_fabric` only from its manifest tags when an original society,
operating system, or load-bearing metaphysics needs more than the thin kernel.
Deepen consequence-producing law/custom, obligation/economy, knowledge flow,
power transfer, and metaphysical limits. Do not expand into general lore.

## Materialization Boundary

Critical operating model, aperture, and independent pressure owners are written
immediately. Materialize the accepted `04_world_palette` in `palette.md`; it is
a creative inclusion stance, not a second content-safety contract, and cannot
relax `boundaries.md`. At stage end, materialize a bounded macro frame,
current-scale truths, two-way local channels, independent movement,
ordinary-life effects, and explicit unknowns. Every source-sensitive truth must
cite the locked Stage 2 scope. Complete or explicitly default `world_fabric`
before closing the stage.

## Do Not

- create a sequence of events, climax, required antagonist, or quest;
- make the character the cause of every pressure;
- populate detailed factions, routes, places, or NPCs before Stage 6.
