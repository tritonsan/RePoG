# 08 — Reciprocity And Campaign Horizon

## Purpose

Join the character and independently moving world in both directions, then set a
campaign horizon without designing the first act or fixing a plot.

## Decision Order

1. `08_two_way_reciprocity` — accept, mix, or change patterns showing how the
   character can affect the world and how the world creates opportunity,
   resistance, and clean-success space.
2. `08_anchor_or_explicit_isolation` — materialize at least one
   character-originated anchor, or record explicit isolation and its playable
   consequences without inventing a hidden tie.
3. `08_advancement_and_reward_rhythm` — choose growth cadence, reward rhythm,
   closure presentation, and whether progress is fictional, banded, numeric,
   or deliberately absent within the Stage 5 mechanics boundary.
4. `08_campaign_horizon` — choose what the campaign may explore, what it should
   test, and what closure would mean; preserve competing answers and optional
   futures.
5. `08_continuity_and_preparation_contract` — confirm source precedence,
   knowledge/reveal ownership, creation threshold, review scope, and what must be
   materialized before play.

## Controlled Extensions

- `advancement_detail` deepens multi-track growth, allied development, reward
  budgets, closure review, and power-creep limits only when its manifest tags
  were produced by accepted choices.
- `campaign_architecture` begins here for long-journey, episodic, setup/payoff,
  or ending-horizon triggers. Its Stage 8 portion records multi-arc questions,
  available setups, reach conditions, and possible endings without scheduling
  outcomes. Its separate first-act-dependent portion belongs to Stage 9.

Stage 6 `character_embedding` output is an input here, not a second extension
activation or permission to invent personal truth.

## Materialization Boundary

Write critical reciprocity and authority choices immediately. Before completing
the stage, materialize the anchor/isolation record, independent pressure,
intersection, competence affordance, limitation counterplay, relationship
positions, horizon questions, and closure grammar. When `advancement_detail` is
`active`, make it `complete` or explicitly `defaulted`, and evaluate every
trigger tag.
Resolve the Stage 8 portion of `campaign_architecture` as `complete` or
explicitly `defaulted` before closing this stage. The aggregate extension may
still display `active`; that means only its separately tracked Stage 9 portion
remains unresolved. `stages_1_8_complete` evaluates the Stage 8 portion, not the
aggregate label.

Passing `stages_1_8_complete` means these owners exist and agree; it does not
approve design direction and does not authorize preparation.

## Do Not

- name the first act's event sequence, climax, solution, or required quest;
- turn a dramatic question into a promised answer;
- make an NPC relationship, faction, or world process exist only for the Player.
