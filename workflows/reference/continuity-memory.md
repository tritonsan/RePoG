# Continuity Memory

Load only the relevant section for a difficult lookup, consequential knowledge
transfer, compression, reply handoff, or offscreen ownership question. This is
a cold reference, not another ordinary-turn checklist. The active workflow
still owns routing and persistence. The model decides meaning; helpers may
check declared references and shapes, never infer a fact or judge narration.

The optional details below extend existing owners. Missing new fields do not
invalidate an older campaign. Add them when a real continuity need occurs;
never invent past evidence or require a whole-campaign migration.

## Discover The Owner

Start with the live scene and its small context references. For an entity,
check the canonical name, established aliases, ledger path, and relevant
`session_brief.md` Triggered Lookups before creating another entity or assuming
the topic is new. Search only the indicated owner or narrowly relevant ledger
section when no pointer exists. Do not read broad history for every turn.

An optional expanded lookup has `Lookup id`, `Signals`, `Owner ref`, `Why now`,
and `Verified at revision`. `Owner ref` is a campaign-relative file plus an
existing heading or explicit fact/thread/element id. A heading's normal anchor
form is allowed; fuzzy resemblance is not proof of identity. `Signals` uses
established names, aliases, topics, or causal triggers. `Why now` describes the
condition for reading the owner, never its current answer or a scripted event.

Read the linked owner before using its facts. A brief's older revision does not
prove the owner changed, but its applicability remains unverified until that
source is read. When the relevant owner changed, recheck the pointer and its
condition; refresh or remove that entry at the scene/act preparation boundary.
Do not discard all useful links or rewrite the entire index for an unrelated
revision. A replaced heading/id needs a repaired pointer, not a guessed match.

If a reference is missing or several identities fit, search narrowly for the
same explicit id and established alias. An unresolved lookup is not proof that
the event never happened. Ask one consequential clarification or use Designer
recovery for a required broken owner; do not assert absence, duplicate an
entity, or substitute a historical summary as current truth.

When an important old promise, limit, debt, or dormant element will matter on a
known return trigger, leave a pointer to its sole owner in the existing brief.
Persist any newly established identity/alias or fact in its owner first.
Updating this secondary index may wait for normal propagation; the immediate
scene/owner references must still support the intervening turns. Indexing is
GM-only: a hidden alias or fact id is not permission to reveal it in dialogue.

## Keep Accounts Specific To Their Holders

`knowledge_boundaries.md` already separates known, suspected, unknown, protected,
and disclosed information. Use its optional Holder Accounts only when a
particular actor's channel, timing, or mistaken version matters. Keep one
current account per holder–fact pair, with these fields:

- `Account id`, `Holder ref`, and `Fact id` identify the account and actor.
- `Account` preserves the proposition actually received/believed, including
  qualifiers, or references a stable account version in its owning record.
- `Stance` is `confirmed`, `suspected`, `refuted`, or `unknown` from the
  holder's evidence. It is not a numeric confidence score or objective truth.
- `Source ref` identifies the observation, report, or inference evidence;
  `Learned at` records fictional time, or `unknown` if not established.
- `Recorded revision` identifies persistence; `Supersedes` and `Correction ref`
  identify a replaced account and the evidence for changing it, or `none`.

A credible letter can make its recipient confident in a false report. Keep
that account distinct from world truth and from what other actors heard. A
correction updates only actors who received it; an uninformed actor keeps
their earlier belief. Do not retarget an old account to a mutable source whose
new text the holder has never learned. An institution's knowledge is not
automatically every member's knowledge.

When replacing an account, preserve the former account/source in append-only
history and commit the changed knowledge owner and causal correction together.
For RPG, include this before/after evidence in the semantic capture used for the
writer's receipt, never a manually appended event. Companion uses its existing
atomic semantic receipt and allowed log-marker contract.
Existing holder lists are references to this same knowledge authority and must
agree; they are not independent versions to reconcile by recency. The primary
Companion's Disclosure Ledger remains its exact private/user-facing account
owner, with its existing consent and deception rules. No new account may evade
user-memory consent or an active forgetting instruction.

## Compress Without Changing Consequences

Before shortening an affected owner, identify the details that could change a
later action or interpretation. Preserve them in the same owner, with a source
reference; this is a reasoning step, not a new scorecard or form for every note:

- actor, topic, and scope of the change;
- causal event and the evidence for accepting it;
- condition, exception, deadline, and remaining obligation;
- uncertainty, unresolved alternatives, and whose belief is being described;
- what changed and what explicitly did not;
- the trigger for revisiting it and the history reference needed for detail.

For example, accepting an apology about a missed appointment does not restore
access to a borrowed key. Keep that limit with the current relationship or
permission owner; a historical log link alone cannot replace an active limit.
A conditional promise stays conditional. A declined offer does not become a
commitment, and an unseen preparation does not become remembered experience.

Compare the proposed shorter text with those consequences before writing. If
shortening changes a consequence, restore the missing qualification; compression
is not permission to change truth. A separately established change based on new
evidence requires the normal durable owner transaction, not secondary
enrichment. If evidence is insufficient, retain the unresolved wording and
source rather than choosing the cleaner story. Preserve
meaningful append-only history. Structural validation cannot prove semantic
equivalence; sampled clean-context replays must test that separately.

## Resume An Unfinished Reply

The restart-loss test already makes any new fact needed later durable,
including a real relationship change, promise, permission, or disclosure. Do
not rename those changes as checkpoints to avoid a fictional revision.

When no durable truth changed but the next reply would be ambiguous without
the just-finished exchange, use a pure scene checkpoint under the persistence
workflow. In the existing `scene_frame.resume_anchor`, retain only the last
speaker, the unresolved question or choice referent, and where control rests.
Reference an existing offer/owner when appropriate; do not accept it for the
Player or store a transcript. A coincident durable result carries this handoff
in the same commit, never a second write.

A greeting, joke, atmosphere, or answered line with no material reply dependency
remains soft ordinary play with no write. The approximate five-soft-turn
checkpoint remains a fallback, not a guarantee of verbatim conversation after
an abrupt stop. On resumption, use the anchor to orient without replaying a
resolved action or advancing fiction. If it cannot identify a consequential
reply, clarify briefly instead of inventing the missing question. Companion
exchanges keep their own state/attention/thread contract, not RPG checkpoints.

## One Offscreen Process, One Owner

A personal NPC trajectory belongs in that character's note. A shared process
affecting a tracked faction or world domain belongs in `world_dynamics.md`.
Separate personal and domain activity may coexist, but the same movement must
not have two current next decisions or two evaluation identities.

When transferring a personal process to a shared domain, commit the receiving
domain and the character's handoff together. Preserve the former evaluation
in history; set the personal `Offscreen trajectory status` to `inactive` and
leave a `Domain trajectory ref` to the domain's heading/id. Do not retain a
second evolving copy in the personal fields. If a separate personal trajectory
remains `active`, keep its distinct trigger and evaluation identity; the domain
reference must not make that personal process run again. Existing `inactive`,
`active`, and `needs_review` statuses retain their meanings. Legacy uncertainty
stays `needs_review` until evidence or an explicit decision resolves it.

Evaluate only when the owning process's causal trigger is due, and preserve
its evaluation identity across return visits. A reference is neither another
event nor permission to continuously simulate the character.
