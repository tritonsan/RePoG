# RePoG plugin 0.4.2: fixes from the 93-turn review, and directory readiness

0.4.2 fixes three faults found in a 93-turn review of a live campaign on 0.4.1,
and rewords the plugin's description ahead of a directory submission.

## Roll lines in the play language

`roll_dice.BAND_PHRASES` had no entry for `critical_success` or
`clean_success`, so a Turkish roll line ended in "critical success" or "clean
success". Four reviewers flagged it independently. Both bands now have phrases
in Turkish, German, Spanish, and French.

## The first reply after a compaction

Every compaction in the review landed when the Player's prompt arrived. That
prompt's capsule had been built just before, as a delta whose unchanged sections
were "as last shown", and the summary had just dropped them. The first reply
after each compaction therefore ran without the language card, the call shapes,
and, twice, without the directives. The full capsule arrived only with the next
prompt.

The `SessionStart` hook now prints, after a compaction or a resume:

- the accepted style samples, cut to 800 characters;
- the locked order;
- a full capsule, recorded so that the next prompt gets a delta.

## Years under 18 in a cast member's history

An English test run linked a character's canon age 15 to men's desire by
allusion. Neither `minor_romance` nor `_minor_sexual_proximity` caught it,
because the sentence used no listed term.

Two changes:

- **`[limits]` line:** the capsule adds this line for any cast member in scope
  whose note states an age under 18 (`lang_pack.states_minor_age`). Those years
  are told only as a childhood, never tied to desire, attraction, or a sexual
  look, touch, or offer, stated or implied. On the live campaign the line names
  three characters.
- **Romance terms:** the English and Turkish lists gain a few terms for leering
  and harassment. The full check on the live campaign reports the same
  findings as before.

The play skill and the README now say "backstory and hints included".

## Description and privacy

- **Manifest and README:** both say RePoG is a storytelling and game tool, not
  an adult-content service, and that the 18+ limit is there for dark themes
  such as violence and crime, while sexual content is never explicit.
- **Install line:** the README now names `tritonsan/RePoG`.
- **Privacy section:** a Companion remembers the Player only through local files
  that the Player can read, edit, or delete.
