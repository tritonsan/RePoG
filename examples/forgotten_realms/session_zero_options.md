# Forgotten Realms Session 0 Options

## Player-Facing Contract

This file is a spoiler-safe option bank. It proposes defaults but accepts nothing. The worldbuild workflow must ask exactly one unresolved decision per content turn, explain why it matters, offer neutral options, write the accepted answer to the appropriate campaign-owned file, and stop.

Do not present GM-only truths from [Campaign Frames](campaign_frames.md). Do not infer character identity or personal history from a selected region.

## Quick-Start Proposal

Offer this only as a proposal:

- World: Forgotten Realms, focused on Faerûn
- Rules assumption: 5.5e
- Canon mode: current tabletop canon with unresolved published-adventure outcomes
- Era: current 2025 sourcebook era; exact year left date-neutral unless needed
- Region: Dalelands
- Frame: The Bell the Forest Silenced
- Opening scale: one settlement, one road, one neighboring community, and one forest edge
- Tone: hopeful local heroism with meaningful danger and faction pressure
- Starting stature: capable but not yet famous
- Famous-character use: distant reputation only unless requested

The user must confirm or revise every value that affects play. A single acceptance of `quick start` may accept the bundle only if the workflow first shows all included defaults plainly.

## Decision 1 — Canon Closeness

Why it matters: this controls whether older lore, games, novels, and published-adventure outcomes can become durable truth.

- Current tabletop canon: use the package hierarchy and leave unselected outcomes open. Recommended.
- Current canon plus selected legacy lore: add named older elements after checking conflicts.
- Alternate continuity: choose a game, novel line, adventure result, or homebrew divergence as an explicit branch.

Do not ask the player to enumerate all past media. Ask only which general posture they want; investigate specifics when they affect the selected locale.

## Decision 2 — Regional Experience

Why it matters: the region changes the core activity, pressure, everyday life, and lore burden.

- Dalelands: local heroic fantasy, forest mystery, independent communities, and ancient ruins. Recommended.
- Phandalin and nearby Sword Coast: the most familiar beginner frontier.
- Baldur's Gate: crime, class, urban investigation, and political compromise.
- Calimshan: high magic, trade, resource politics, and genie agreements.
- Icewind Dale: isolation, community survival, cold, and buried danger.
- Moonshae Isles: sea travel, fey promises, sacred nature, and island politics.
- Another Faerûn region: name the desired fantasy; targeted research is required before lock.

## Decision 3 — Campaign Frame

Present only frames compatible with the selected region.

- The Bell the Forest Silenced: restore a failed road and decide how communities, displaced people, old magic, and outside interests share the future.
- The Ledger of Ash: find a missing record before city power decides who disappears with it.
- The Well of Four Courts: settle water access while mortal and elemental claimants invoke incompatible obligations.
- The Last Warm Road: keep communities supplied and decide how survival costs are distributed.
- Salt in the Moonwell: repair or replace old boundaries as land, sea, and fey claims collide.
- Custom local frame: build a new cause-and-effect pressure from the selected region card.

A frame is a starting pressure, not a required plot or ending.

## Decision 4 — Heroic Scale

Why it matters: this sets initial authority, danger, and how much setting knowledge characters are expected to possess.

- Local beginnings: known to a household, street, crew, shrine, or village. Recommended.
- Established troubleshooters: recognized within one institution or district.
- Regional agents: trusted by a faction, ruler, temple, or major community.
- Already renowned: appropriate only when the group wants immediate high-power politics and accepts a larger lore burden.

## Decision 5 — Tone Balance

Ask for the desired balance rather than assuming all Forgotten Realms play is light heroic fantasy.

- Hopeful: harm is real, but solidarity and repair can change systems.
- Adventurous: wonder, momentum, discovery, and risk dominate.
- Grounded: law, supply, travel, and social consequence receive more attention.
- Dark: institutions may be predatory and victories costly; boundaries require explicit review.
- Mythic: gods, high magic, prophecy, and planar forces become visible earlier.

Optional intensity sliders:

- Political complexity: low | medium | high
- Survival pressure: low | medium | high
- Horror: none | atmospheric | substantial
- Moral ambiguity: low | medium | high
- Lore density: introductory | standard | deep
- Famous-character presence: none | distant references | occasional cameo | requested involvement

## Decision 6 — Party Glue

Why it matters: a shared reason to cooperate should exist before individual secrets pull the party apart.

Neutral options:

- Shared home or neighborhood
- Shared missing person or endangered contact
- Same employer, patron, temple, guild, or faction cell
- Survivors or witnesses of the same incident
- Members of a caravan, vessel, patrol, expedition, or diplomatic mission
- Different stakeholders appointed to solve one crisis
- A debt or promise the party holds collectively

The party glue must not determine each PC's feelings about it.

## Character Fantasy Menu

Use these as inspiration, not assignments:

- Local protector rising into legend
- Scholar of dangerous ruins
- Secret defender against concentrated power
- Knight divided between order and conscience
- Urban survivor, investigator, or heist specialist
- Pilgrim interpreting a divine sign
- Rare bearer of spellfire
- Elemental oath keeper or genie-linked negotiator
- Northern guide and survival expert
- Fey-touched guardian or mediator
- Sailor, privateer, or island envoy
- Monster hunter who learns the threat has a history
- Underdark exile or emissary
- Merchant, caravan specialist, or diplomat
- Survivor escaping a dangerous cult

Mechanical background, class, species, feat, and subclass choices follow the selected rules implementation. Narrative identity comes first.

## Player-Authored Character Questions

Ask only those relevant to the selected frame, one decision at a time.

### Place And Belonging

- Which place do you call home, and what makes leaving difficult?
- Who there can still ask something of you?
- Which local custom do you defend or reject?

### Ties

- Who trusts you for a reason unrelated to adventuring skill?
- Who believes you owe them?
- Which faction helped you once, and what did it misunderstand about you?
- What person or community would complicate the obvious solution?

### Belief And Faith

- Do you honor one deity, several, none, or remain uncertain?
- What sign would challenge your current belief?
- Which institution claims moral authority over you without your consent?

### Ambition And Change

- What would being remembered mean to you?
- What kind of power do you fear becoming dependent on?
- Which compromise have you promised never to make?

### Secrets

- What truth would change how one ally sees you?
- What part of your history belongs to the player, to the GM, or to both?
- May the campaign challenge the secret, reveal it, or leave timing entirely to the player?

## Boundaries And Safety

The package never supplies accepted boundaries. Session 0 must establish them before finalization.

Review at least:

- Harm to children and families
- Coercion, captivity, slavery, and loss of agency
- Religious extremism and corruption
- Body horror, transformation, undead, and infernal imagery
- Starvation, exposure, disease, and survival detail
- Discrimination, cultural conflict, and species-based prejudice
- Crime, policing, incarceration, and class violence
- Romance, sexuality, and interpersonal betrayal
- Character death, resurrection, and permanent injury
- Phobias relevant to the selected region

Use the repository's accepted boundary format and visibility rules. Do not store a player's private explanation when the operational boundary is sufficient.

## Materialization Gate

This option bank is complete enough to support Session 0, but it cannot make a campaign ready. Finalization may begin only after:

- Required research for the selected scale is complete or accepted as partial with explicit permission.
- Every player-facing choice needed for the opening is accepted.
- GM-only frame truth is separated from player knowledge.
- PC identity and ties are authored or approved by the player.
- Boundaries are accepted.
- Only the selected opening scale is written into campaign-owned files.
