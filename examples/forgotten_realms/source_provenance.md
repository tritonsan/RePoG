# Forgotten Realms Source Provenance

## Research Snapshot

- Retrieval cutoff: 2026-07-23
- Primary focus: current official tabletop presentation
- Rules focus: revised 2024 rules, currently labeled 5.5e by D&D Beyond
- Setting focus: Faerûn, especially the 2025 setting books
- Attribution mode: links and paraphrased claim summaries
- Verbatim source text: not stored
- Fan-wiki authority: none
- Primary-book access limitation: public product and article text was available; full commercial book text was not independently inspected in this package pass

## Unresolved Research

Before a strict, immutable current-canon lock, verify:

1. The direct primary-book statement of the current in-world year
2. The exact ten-region list in *Heroes of Faerûn*
3. The exact eight-faction list in *Heroes of Faerûn*
4. Current named officeholders only if the selected opening needs them
5. Current outcomes of prior adventures only if the selected locale depends on them
6. Cultural detail for Chult, Calimshan, Reghed communities, Moonshae peoples, Underdark societies, or any other selected identity beyond this package's broad treatment

No reliable current official universal percentage for spellcasters was found. Do not invent one.
