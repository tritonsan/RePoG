# Forgotten Realms Factions

## Use Contract

These cards are stable operating models, not active campaign actors. Materialize only factions that can affect the opening scale. Give each materialized faction a local representative, place, current objective, relationship posture, observable method, and inaction clock.

The 2025 *Heroes of Faerûn* product description confirms eight featured factions, but accessible product copy does not enumerate the complete eight. The first seven cards below are visible in current character-option reporting; do not label this package's broader list as the book's exact official eight until the primary book is checked.

## Cross-Regional Factions

### `fr-faction-harpers` — Harpers

- Source status: visible 2025 character-option faction
- Tier: cross-regional network
- Public mask: loosely connected defenders of freedom, knowledge, and resistance to tyranny
- Stable desire: prevent concentrated power and dangerous knowledge from being used to dominate others
- Methods: information gathering, discreet aid, exposure, sabotage, mentorship, and temporary alliances
- Capabilities: agents, informants, lore, safe contacts, and reputation among sympathetic communities
- Limits: decentralization, uneven information, secrecy, internal disagreement, and reluctance to become a ruling power
- Related pressures: censorship, cult infiltration, authoritarian expansion, dangerous artifacts, and faction capture
- Representative face: instantiate a local contact with a profession and an obligation unrelated to the faction
- Key place: instantiate a plausible meeting or message route rather than a conspicuous headquarters
- Knowledge habits: compartmentalized, coded, need-to-know
- Relationship posture: potential ally, suspicious recruiter, or well-meaning manipulator
- Player hooks: compromised contact, disputed mission, protected witness, hidden archive, or a choice between disclosure and safety
- Verification method: corroborated reports, coded introductions, and deeds rather than symbols alone
- Source claims: `claim-factions-visible-2025`

### `fr-faction-zhentarim` — Zhentarim

- Source status: visible 2025 character-option faction
- Tier: cross-regional commercial, mercenary, and influence network
- Public mask: capable fixers, guards, traders, and people who can make difficult arrangements happen
- Stable desire: increase profit, leverage, access, and dependency
- Methods: contracts, intimidation, market pressure, protection, smuggling, recruitment, and strategic generosity
- Capabilities: money, routes, mercenaries, local partners, deniable coercion, and rapid exploitation of instability
- Limits: internal ambition, transactional loyalty, reputational risk, and resistance from communities that recognize dependency traps
- Related pressures: monopolies, debt, land acquisition, privatized security, and political capture
- Representative face: instantiate a locally useful person whose offer solves a real problem at a future price
- Key place: warehouse, caravan office, guard company, inn, or legitimate business
- Knowledge habits: transactional records, compartmentalized operations, selective truth
- Relationship posture: patron, rival, employer, creditor, or temporary ally
- Player hooks: attractive contract, family debt, seized route, threatened competitor, or faction schism
- Source claims: `claim-factions-visible-2025`, `claim-dalelands-2025`

### `fr-faction-emerald-enclave` — Emerald Enclave

- Source status: visible 2025 character-option faction
- Tier: cross-regional nature-focused network
- Public mask: guardians who oppose destructive imbalance between civilization and the living world
- Stable desire: preserve resilient natural systems and prevent catastrophic exploitation or supernatural corruption
- Methods: observation, warning, mediation, direct defense, restoration, and selective destruction
- Capabilities: local ecological knowledge, scouts, druids, animal ties, remote refuges, and regional warning networks
- Limits: differing definitions of balance, sparse coverage, distrust of institutions, and tension between people and ecosystems
- Related pressures: blight, predation, reckless extraction, magical contamination, invasive supernatural forces, and settlement expansion
- Representative face: instantiate a warden rooted in a specific community rather than an abstract nature oracle
- Key place: threatened watershed, grove, trail network, farm boundary, or restoration site
- Knowledge habits: observation over archives; seasonal and ecological signals
- Relationship posture: ally, critic, mediator, or uncompromising protector
- Player hooks: disputed logging, poisoned water, displaced creatures, corrupted ward, or a necessary but damaging public project
- Source claims: `claim-factions-visible-2025`

### `fr-faction-lords-alliance` — Lords' Alliance

- Source status: visible 2025 character-option faction
- Tier: alliance of political powers
- Public mask: cooperation among rulers and cities for security, stability, and trade
- Stable desire: protect member interests and prevent threats from overwhelming individual jurisdictions
- Methods: diplomacy, intelligence sharing, coordinated force, sanctions, sponsorship, and negotiation
- Capabilities: law, soldiers, money, official access, infrastructure, and public legitimacy
- Limits: competing member priorities, slow consensus, unequal influence, and tension between common security and local advantage
- Related pressures: war, piracy, trade disruption, cross-border threats, refugee movement, and rival state influence
- Representative face: instantiate an envoy or official with both alliance instructions and a local political constituency
- Key place: council chamber, embassy, customs house, military post, or diplomatic residence
- Knowledge habits: reports, formal channels, confidential negotiation, and selective public statements
- Relationship posture: sponsor, regulator, rival jurisdiction, or reluctant coalition
- Player hooks: conflicting mandates, denied aid, disputed border, diplomatic escort, or evidence one member is exploiting the crisis
- Source claims: `claim-factions-visible-2025`

### `fr-faction-order-gauntlet` — Order of the Gauntlet

- Source status: visible 2025 character-option faction
- Tier: cross-regional faith-and-arms organization
- Public mask: disciplined champions who confront supernatural evil and protect communities
- Stable desire: identify and decisively stop forces they regard as intolerable threats
- Methods: investigation, mobilization, defense, exorcism, direct assault, and public moral witness
- Capabilities: trained combatants, divine support, temples, public trust in some regions, and rapid response
- Limits: zeal, incomplete intelligence, doctrinal disagreement, and difficulty addressing structural or morally ambiguous harm
- Related pressures: undead, cults, fiends, corruption, possession, and institutional complicity
- Representative face: instantiate a committed protector whose certainty has a cost
- Key place: temple chapter, hospice, watch post, shrine, or threatened community
- Knowledge habits: testimony, doctrine, signs of corruption, and field reports
- Relationship posture: staunch ally, demanding sponsor, or dangerous simplifier
- Player hooks: wrongful accusation, compromised commander, impossible defense, disputed evil, or a threat protected by law
- Source claims: `claim-factions-visible-2025`

### `fr-faction-purple-dragons` — Purple Dragon Knights

- Source status: visible 2025 character-option faction and current Banneret context
- Tier: Cormyrean state and military institution
- Public mask: defenders and representatives of Cormyr's order
- Stable desire: preserve the realm, its people, lawful authority, and institutional legitimacy
- Methods: patrol, command, investigation, diplomacy, defense, and military action
- Capabilities: training, rank, legal authority, logistics, fortifications, and state intelligence
- Limits: jurisdiction, chain of command, court politics, public scrutiny, and conflict between law and conscience
- Related pressures: border security, noble interference, succession, infiltration, and contested orders
- Representative face: instantiate an officer whose oath, orders, and local knowledge do not perfectly align
- Key place: border post, garrison, court, road station, or protected settlement
- Knowledge habits: reports, command channels, patrol intelligence, and formal records
- Relationship posture: authority, patron, rival command, or institution in need of reform
- Player hooks: unlawful order, missing patrol, political appointment, disputed jurisdiction, or defense with unacceptable collateral cost
- Source claims: `claim-factions-visible-2025`, `claim-banneret-context`

### `fr-faction-cult-dragon` — Cult of the Dragon

- Source status: visible 2025 character-option faction
- Tier: secretive cross-regional cult network
- Public mask: varies; scholarship, trade, private devotion, or no public identity
- Stable desire: advance dragon-centered prophecy, power, patronage, or transformation according to the active cell's doctrine
- Methods: recruitment, relic theft, ritual, bribery, infiltration, manipulation of dragons, and secret scholarship
- Capabilities: hidden cells, esoteric lore, wealth, draconic contacts, fanatics, and expendable proxies
- Limits: doctrinal schisms, dangerous patrons, secrecy, ambition, and unreliable control over dragons
- Related pressures: missing artifacts, manipulated settlements, dragon tribute, prophetic panic, and cult competition
- Representative face: instantiate a cell leader with a specific interpretation rather than a generic zealot
- Key place: archive, merchant concern, isolated shrine, dig site, or dragon-haunted route
- Knowledge habits: coded doctrine, stolen records, prophecy, and compartmentalized ritual knowledge
- Relationship posture: hidden antagonist, splinter ally, infiltrator, or dangerous source of lore
- Player hooks: false prophecy, coerced recruit, stolen egg or relic, divided cell, or dragon exploiting its worshipers
- Source claims: `claim-factions-visible-2025`

## Additional Durable Powers

### `fr-faction-red-wizards` — Red Wizards Of Thay

- Source status: durable Forgotten Realms power; verify current local presence before lock
- Tier: state-linked magical network
- Public mask: traders, envoys, scholars, magical service providers, or openly feared agents
- Stable desire: acquire leverage, knowledge, wealth, and influence useful to Thayan power
- Methods: trade, magical expertise, proxies, coercion, espionage, artifact acquisition, and necromancy where advantageous
- Capabilities: advanced magic, resources, disciplined networks, and access to dangerous knowledge
- Limits: suspicion, internal competition, political exposure, local law, and dependence on intermediaries abroad
- Related pressures: illicit magic markets, missing experts, compromised officials, artifact races, and undead labor or warfare
- Player hooks: useful cure with a political price, defecting specialist, contested artifact, or local partner concealing the true buyer
- Source claims: `claim-legacy-gapfill`

### `fr-faction-flaming-fist` — Flaming Fist

- Source status: primary 2025 Baldur's Gate institution
- Tier: city and regional mercenary enforcement power
- Public mask: defenders and enforcers responsible for security
- Stable desire: maintain order, institutional authority, revenue, and operational control
- Methods: patrols, checkpoints, investigation, arrest, force, contracts, and political influence
- Capabilities: troops, fortifications, legal standing, equipment, and control of movement at key points
- Limits: corruption, divided officers, public resentment, political oversight, and reliance on coercive legitimacy
- Related pressures: selective enforcement, bribery, emergency powers, faction infiltration, and class conflict
- Player hooks: honest officer isolated by corruption, wrongful detention, paid protection, missing evidence, or competing orders
- Source claims: `claim-baldurs-gate-2025`

### `fr-faction-guild-baldurs-gate` — The Guild Of Baldur's Gate

- Source status: primary 2025 Baldur's Gate institution
- Tier: city criminal network
- Public mask: officially hidden; informally understood as an organizer of illicit markets and protection
- Stable desire: keep profitable crime predictable, preserve influence, and prevent rivals from destabilizing its arrangements
- Methods: protection, smuggling, bribery, information trade, theft, intimidation, and controlled violence
- Capabilities: street intelligence, corrupt contacts, hidden routes, crews, and access to illegal goods
- Limits: secrecy, factional ambition, enforcement pressure, community backlash, and dependence on functioning commerce
- Related pressures: turf conflict, coerced neighborhoods, contraband, political blackmail, and vigilante response
- Player hooks: protection bargain, stolen ledger, internal succession, threatened neighborhood, or a worse rival the Guild wants removed
- Source claims: `claim-baldurs-gate-2025`

### `fr-faction-dead-three` — Cults Of The Dead Three

- Source status: primary 2025 Baldur's Gate threat family and current subclass context
- Tier: independent or loosely connected cult cells
- Public mask: hidden within civic, criminal, funerary, or coercive institutions
- Stable desire: express and expand the power of Bane, Bhaal, or Myrkul through tyranny, violence, murder, death, or fear
- Methods: infiltration, terror, ritual killing, coercion, necromancy, and exploitation of institutional weakness
- Capabilities: fanaticism, divine or occult power, secret cells, compromised officials, and fear
- Limits: rivalry among doctrines, exposure, self-destructive violence, and dependence on vulnerable institutions
- Related pressures: unexplained murders, authoritarian cells, desecration, disappearances, and public panic
- Player hooks: survivor pursued for return, conflicting cells, false-flag crime, compromised mortuary, or official using cult methods without formal membership
- Source claims: `claim-baldurs-gate-2025`, `claim-subclasses-2025`

## Regional Power Families

### `fr-power-genie-courts` — Genie Courts

- Source status: primary 2025 Calimshan power family
- Tier: regional and planar powers
- Public mask: courts, patrons, claimants, sovereigns, creditors, or elemental protectors
- Stable desire: uphold advantage, status, territory, old bargains, and elemental interests
- Methods: contracts, patronage, wonders, proxies, tests, gifts, threats, and enforcement of remembered obligations
- Capabilities: elemental magic, longevity, wealth, mobility, followers, and unusual domains
- Limits: rival courts, wording of bargains, pride, old duties, mortal coalitions, and incompatible elemental interests
- Player hooks: inherited contract, disputed translation, missing party to an accord, water right, or mortal client playing courts against each other
- Source claims: `claim-calimshan-2025`

### `fr-power-fey-courts` — Fey Courts And Kin

- Source status: primary 2025 Moonshae power family
- Tier: local, regional, and planar powers
- Public mask: neighbors, patrons, terrors, guardians, guests, or stories made present
- Stable desire: preserve a value, place, season, emotion, promise, or hierarchy meaningful to the specific fey actor
- Methods: bargains, hospitality, transformation, omen, glamour, curse, gift, and indirect pressure
- Capabilities: old magic, crossings, altered perception, place-bound influence, and unconventional allies
- Limits: promises, symbols, rival values, boundaries, names, hospitality, and dependence on a place or condition
- Player hooks: broken oath, disputed festival, altered boundary, missing guest, ecological symptom, or bargain inherited by descendants
- Source claims: `claim-moonshae-2025`

## Materialization Rule

At opening scale, prefer:

- One institution the community relies upon
- One faction exploiting or worsening the pressure
- One faction or power with a credible competing solution

Add more only when their actions become observable. A faction card without a local face, method, and consequence is not yet active campaign content.
