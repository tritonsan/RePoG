# Forgotten Realms Canon Policy

## Research Status

- Classification: `canon_existing_world`
- Coverage: sufficient for a regional Session 0 after the user selects a locale
- Global-setting completeness: intentionally not claimed
- Current-scale lock permitted: no, until locale and continuity choices are accepted
- Risk accepted: pending user decision
- Primary uncertainty: the exact in-world year of the 2025 sourcebook present
- Source register: [Source Provenance](source_provenance.md)

## Source Scope

This package models current tabletop Faerûn rather than the entire D&D multiverse or every continuity ever published under the Forgotten Realms name.

Included by default:

- The 2025 tabletop setting presentation in *Forgotten Realms: Heroes of Faerûn* and *Forgotten Realms: Adventures in Faerûn*
- Revised 2024 core-rule assumptions where mechanics or general cosmology matter
- Post-2014 fifth-edition tabletop material when it does not conflict with the 2025 setting books
- Older-edition history only as conservative gap-filling material

Excluded by default:

- Unselected outcomes from published adventures
- Unselected endings from video games
- Novel, comic, film, or game events that current tabletop sources have not reaffirmed
- Fan-wiki claims used as sole evidence
- Exact demographics not supplied by reliable current sources
- A complete model of every continent, plane, deity, settlement, and named character

## Canon Source Hierarchy

1. Current 2025 Forgotten Realms tabletop setting books
2. Revised 2024 core rules and current errata
3. Official post-2014 fifth-edition tabletop setting and adventure material, where compatible
4. Earlier tabletop editions as historical gap-fillers
5. Novels, video games, comics, and other media only when reaffirmed or explicitly selected as optional continuity
6. Secondary reporting as a discovery and cross-checking aid, never as a replacement for conflicting primary text

When two sources conflict, the higher source wins for template-fixed truth. Record the displaced version as an optional continuity only if it offers clear play value.

## Ruleset Boundary

- Default label: 5.5e
- Meaning: revised 2024 D&D core rules under D&D Beyond's current naming convention
- Legacy support: 2014 5e material can inform a campaign when compatible
- Setting dependency: world truths do not require a specific character build
- Mechanical migration: preserve fiction first; replace classes, spells, difficulty, and item mechanics in the selected system adapter

## Timeline Boundary

- Stable anchor: post-Second Sundering, at the present described by the 2025 Forgotten Realms sourcebooks
- Provisional year: 1501 DR
- Confidence: medium
- Lock rule: do not call 1501 DR immutable until checked directly in the primary 2025 book text
- Date-neutral fallback: use `the current 2025 sourcebook era` if an exact date is unnecessary

Past events may be used at three confidence levels:

- High: restated or relied upon by current tabletop material
- Medium: long-established tabletop history with no known current contradiction
- Low: edition-specific detail that has not been reaffirmed

Low-confidence history should appear as disputed scholarship, legend, or a Session 0 continuity option rather than invisible objective truth.

## Continuity Modes

### Current Tabletop Canon

- Uses the hierarchy above
- Does not assume a specific published-adventure or video-game ending
- Recommended default

### Current Canon With Selected Legacy Lore

- Adds explicitly named older-edition or novel details
- Records each addition and any contradiction it creates
- Appropriate for players attached to a particular historical presentation

### Alternate Realms Continuity

- Allows a selected game, novel series, published-adventure result, or homebrew divergence to define history
- Must identify the divergence point
- Must never be mislabeled as the package default

## Player Action Versus Canon

Once materialized, player action outranks future franchise continuity inside that campaign. A later official publication may update the reusable package, but it must not retroactively rewrite accepted campaign history.

- Before materialization: current package canon governs proposals.
- After materialization: accepted campaign files govern that campaign.
- After a player-caused divergence: store the divergence and its consequences as campaign truth.
- On package update: offer migration; never silently overwrite.

## Famous Characters

Named canonical heroes and villains may establish reputation, history, or distant pressure. They should not become required patrons, rescue the party, or resolve a player-owned conflict by default.

A famous-character appearance requires one of these:

- The user explicitly requests it.
- The selected frame inherently depends on that character.
- The appearance creates a meaningful player decision without taking control of the outcome.

Any uncertain present location, office, relationship, or survival status must be checked before durable use.

## Published Adventure Outcomes

Do not assume that any official adventure happened exactly as printed. During Session 0, choose one of:

- Uncommitted: relevant outcomes remain undefined until needed.
- Broad strokes: the crisis existed, but details and victors remain open.
- Selected result: the user chooses a particular published outcome.
- Replaced: the campaign begins from an explicit alternate result.

## Homebrew Authority

User-supplied homebrew is authoritative for the new campaign when clearly accepted. It does not silently change the reusable package. Record it as:

- Campaign-only addition
- Campaign-only contradiction
- Proposed package revision

Only the third category should be considered for a later package update.

## Conflict Resolution Procedure

1. Identify the smallest conflicting claim.
2. Assign every source a hierarchy level and date.
3. Prefer the current primary tabletop source.
4. Preserve useful alternatives as labeled options, not merged facts.
5. Mark remaining uncertainty and its play impact.
6. Ask the user only if the conflict affects the selected opening scale.
7. Keep readiness false while a required claim remains `needed_pending`.

## Known Uncertainties

| Claim | Current position | Confidence | Required action |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Exact current year | 1501 DR | Medium | Verify in a primary 2025 book before immutable lock |
| Complete list of ten regions summarized in *Heroes of Faerûn* | Product page confirms ten but public copy does not enumerate all | Medium | Verify against the book before claiming the exact official ten |
| Complete list of eight featured factions | Product page confirms eight; accessible summaries expose only part of the list | Medium | Verify against the book before labeling an exact eight |
| Faerûn-wide percentage of spellcasters | No reliable current universal percentage found | High confidence that no number should be fixed | Use regional access bands instead |
| Outcomes of recent adventures and *Baldur's Gate 3* | Not assumed | High | Ask only when relevant to the opening scale |

## Hard Boundaries

- Do not merge every D&D setting into Forgotten Realms.
- Do not treat all old lore as simultaneously current.
- Do not turn a fan summary into primary authority.
- Do not infer a universal culture, law, economy, or magic density for all Faerûn.
- Do not make a canonical celebrity necessary for party success.
- Do not preselect PC identity, allegiance, relationships, or interiority.
- Do not expose a frame's GM-only truth in player-facing Session 0 material.
- Do not copy this package wholesale into an active campaign.
