# Dune Campaign Frames

## Use Contract

Every frame below is RePoG-original campaign design, not Dune canon and not a summary of a published adventure. A frame is selectable reference content until Session 0 accepts it. Player-facing sections may be presented without the GM-only hidden truth. Famous characters are neither patrons nor solutions by default.

## `dune-frame-dry-ledger` — The Dry Ledger

- Status: package-original proposal
- Compatible era: `late-corrino-pre-transfer`
- Opening theater: `dune-region-hagga`
- Party mandate: mixed field cell of an original Minor House with a temporary logistics sub-concession
- Opening scale: one settlement, one water office, one harvester zone, one contested route

### Player-Facing Promise

Your House's smallest Arrakis concession is meeting its spice quota, yet workers collapse from ration cuts and sealed water accounts list people nobody can find. An off-world audit arrives after the next harvest. You have authority to inspect records and protect production—but not enough power to ignore the governing House, the desert, or the people whose lives are entries in the ledger.

### Visible Situation

- Production reports are improbably stable despite repeated equipment failure.
- Water seals pass official inspection but do not match household testimony.
- A recent crawler loss has no complete casualty list.
- The concession factor blames smugglers and demands immediate arrests.
- A worker committee will stop the next deployment unless rations are restored.
- Wormsign and a closing weather window impose a real deadline.

### Actor Configuration

- Original Minor House field office: needs the concession to survive politically
- Governing-House overseer: wants quota and quiet before superiors notice
- Water steward: protects records and refuses a simple inspection
- Concession factor: controls manifests, wages, and emergency stores
- Worker organization: seeks water, truth about the dead, and safe deployment
- Desert broker or smuggler: can move people or evidence at a price

Each actor receives a name and local face only during materialization.

### Causal Chain

1. An avoidable crawler disaster was underreported to preserve quota and reputation.
2. Displaced survivors and dependents lost legal ration status when the official casualty roll closed.
3. Someone began reusing inactive water seals to keep those households alive.
4. A second actor discovered the ghost accounts and used the same blind spot to hide spice skimming.
5. Stable output concealed both crimes until equipment and bodies absorbed the cost.
6. The approaching audit will collapse the shared concealment and let the most powerful actor define one story for both.

### Neutral Action Space

The PCs can separate the two schemes, protect evidence, bargain for legal status, expose the disaster, redirect the audit, follow the spice, organize a safe harvest, or choose whom to make accountable. No faction owns the correct answer. Saving households does not require excusing theft; exposing theft does not require accepting collective punishment.

### Inaction Trajectory

- Next shift: the worker committee stops deployment.
- One step later: the overseer imposes ration control and arrests.
- Audit threshold: the factor burns records and blames missing workers.
- End state: production fails, dependents are expelled, and the patron House loses the concession while the original disaster remains unacknowledged.

### PC Integration Prompts

- Which duty gives you standing here: logistics, medicine, security, analysis, labor knowledge, desert travel, or negotiation?
- Who asked you not to treat the ledger as proof by itself?
- What does your House lose if the concession closes?
- Which order are you unwilling to obey without evidence?

These questions do not assign loyalty or relationships.

### GM-Only Hidden Truth

Hidden-truth id: `dune-frame-dry-ledger-secret-001`

Two overlapping concealments use the same ghost accounts. The water steward redirects ration water to survivors and dependents erased after the unreported crawler disaster. The concession factor discovered the discrepancy later and launders skimmed spice through the inactive worker identities. Neither created the other's scheme, and exposing them as one conspiracy serves the governing office.

### Reveal Requirements

Reveal only after the PCs establish at least two independent chains:

1. casualty roll → inactive seal → living household
2. spice manifest → impossible shift output → factor-controlled transfer

One confession is not enough. A Mentat may identify incompatible patterns but still needs authentic inputs and custody.

### Safe Foreshadowing

- Water loss and spice loss follow different shift rhythms.
- A supposedly dead worker's tool is maintained, not abandoned.
- The factor knows account numbers but not the households behind them.
- The steward is evasive about people and precise about volume.

### Forbidden Early Wording

Do not say that the water steward is stealing for profit, that smugglers caused the shortage, or that one mastermind controls both ledgers before the reveal gate.

### Boundaries And Review

Discuss dehydration, labor death, coercive policing, and collective punishment. Keep endangered households active in their own survival rather than passive stakes.

### Expansion

Expand to Arrakeen or CHOAM only if the PCs preserve evidence, lose the concession, or deliberately escalate. Do not introduce a canonical protagonist as an appellate authority.

## `dune-frame-ashes-of-kanly` — Ashes Of Kanly

- Status: package-original proposal
- Compatible era: late Corrino Imperium before the first novel's regime change
- Opening theaters: original House homeworld, then a bounded Kaitain petition if earned
- Party mandate: envoys and investigators of an original Minor House
- Opening scale: damaged estate, one rival delegation, one disputed message

### Player-Facing Promise

An attack destroys your House's seed vault and leaves a rival House cipher in the ash. Your ruler claims lawful kanly has become attempted annihilation and orders you to secure Landsraad protection. Before you accuse a peer, you must prove what happened, preserve next season's food supply, and decide what your own House is willing to reveal.

### Visible Situation

- The physical evidence points too cleanly to a long-standing rival.
- A rescue signal was delayed, increasing damage.
- The House heir demands immediate public accusation.
- Farmers need access to the sealed site before evidence review is complete.
- A stronger House offers protection in exchange for an exclusive export compact.

### Actor Configuration

- Patron House ruler: needs survival and legitimacy
- Ambitious heir: wants decisive alignment and personal authority
- Rival House envoy: denies the attack but admits a separate covert operation
- Local agricultural council: prioritizes seed and livelihood over honor
- Prospective protector House: offers aid with permanent strings
- Guild or communication intermediary: controls evidence about the delayed signal

### Causal Chain

1. The House faces debt and a succession split.
2. A faction inside the household stages limited sabotage to force a protective alliance.
3. A rival spy operation happens to be present and removes unrelated records.
4. The sabotage spreads beyond its intended containment and destroys the seed vault.
5. The rescue signal is delayed by an intermediary protecting the rival operation.
6. Every participant now holds a true fragment that can make a false complete story.

### Neutral Action Space

The PCs may reconstruct the sequence, protect the harvest, negotiate restitution, expose their own household, use the petition strategically, or reject both rival and protector. Legal victory, food security, House independence, and personal loyalty may not align.

### Inaction Trajectory

The heir publishes the accusation, the rival invokes kanly, the protector stations troops, and the agricultural council loses control of the next planting. The House survives as a dependent client while the internal cause disappears.

### PC Integration Prompts

- What part of the House is worth preserving even if its rulers are wrong?
- Who trusts your judgment but cannot command you?
- What would make you testify against your own patron?
- Which resource or relationship connects you to the affected farmers?

### GM-Only Hidden Truth

Hidden-truth id: `dune-frame-ashes-secret-001`

The heir authorized a controlled fire to manufacture urgency for the protector alliance. A rival spy cell independently entered the site to steal breeding records, and its withdrawal delayed the rescue signal. The heir did not intend the vault's destruction; the rival did not begin the fire. The protector suspects both and prefers a simplified accusation that produces dependency.

### Reveal Requirements

Require fire-pattern evidence, authenticated signal timing, and one motive-bearing record or witness from inside the patron household. A rival cipher proves presence, not causation.

### Safe Foreshadowing

- The planted cipher survived where nearby storage did not.
- The heir knows the protector's terms before formal contact.
- Missing records concern crops rather than military plans.
- The signal delay begins after the fire starts.

### Forbidden Early Wording

Do not call the rival innocent, the heir a traitor, or the protector the architect before evidence supports those interpretations.

### Boundaries And Review

Discuss food insecurity, betrayal by family or patron, political dependency, and possible execution. Never assign a PC secret complicity.

### Expansion

Kaitain becomes active only if the group chooses public adjudication. Otherwise keep the campaign on the homeworld and explore consequences of a private settlement.

## `dune-frame-silent-manifest` — The Silent Manifest

- Status: package-original proposal
- Compatible eras: most pre-Scattering Imperial eras after targeted transport review
- Opening theater: `dune-region-heighliner`
- Party mandate: a House delegation responsible for one sealed cargo
- Opening scale: one transit hold, three disputed containers, one departure window

### Player-Facing Promise

During Guild passage, three containers appear under the same authenticated seal. One belongs to your House, one is claimed by a commercial rival, and one has no owner willing to speak. The Guild will not resume transfer until liability is assigned. Opening the wrong container may violate contract, expose people in hiding, or give another House the pretext it needs.

### Visible Situation

- The duplicated seal passes ordinary inspection.
- Life-support demand exceeds the declared passenger count.
- A CHOAM factor insists the dispute is only stolen spice.
- A Minor House delegation refuses to open its machinery hold.
- Guild personnel care about safe resolution and schedule, not moral vindication.
- The PCs cannot consult their ruler before the decision deadline.

### Actor Configuration

- PC House delegation and cargo custodian
- Minor House evacuating something it will not declare
- CHOAM factor seeking valuable contraband
- Rival security team demanding search authority
- Guild manifest officer with narrow discretion
- Hidden passengers with their own spokesperson and plan

### Causal Chain

1. A failing Minor House attempts to evacuate skilled families before a rival annexes its domain.
2. A coerced port clerk duplicates a legitimate House seal to place them inside a machinery container.
3. A commercial thief notices the altered manifest and adds stolen spice under the same identity.
4. Life-support imbalance exposes a problem without distinguishing people from contraband.
5. Rival agents force a hold, expecting both political leverage and profit.
6. Guild delay makes every passenger favor a quick scapegoat.

### Neutral Action Space

The PCs can establish custody, negotiate sanctuary, return stolen spice, expose coercion, transfer responsibility, construct a lawful fiction, or accept a political cost. The hidden passengers are decision-makers, not cargo to be awarded.

### Inaction Trajectory

The Guild isolates all three containers, rival security receives limited inspection, the families are returned to a hostile port, and the spice theft is blamed on the failing House.

### PC Integration Prompts

- What did your House trust you to transport without supervision?
- Which contract do you understand better than the rest of the delegation?
- Why might displaced specialists seek your protection?
- What political cost are you authorized to accept—and what cost is yours alone to choose?

### GM-Only Hidden Truth

Hidden-truth id: `dune-frame-manifest-secret-001`

The duplicate seal conceals two independent acts: a rescue organized by the failing House and spice theft added later by a CHOAM subcontractor. The hidden families know the thief's identity because they heard the second loading, but revealing it exposes their location.

### Reveal Requirements

Separate atmosphere telemetry, loading order, and spice residue. The families disclose their witness only after credible protection or another plan they accept.

### Safe Foreshadowing

- Life-support changes before the cargo-mass discrepancy.
- Tool marks on two containers were made by different crews.
- The CHOAM factor asks about spice before any spice is found.
- The undeclared container transmits a deliberate, nonverbal reply.

### Forbidden Early Wording

Do not call the passengers helpless, the Minor House a trafficker, or the CHOAM factor the sole culprit before evidence and consent.

### Boundaries And Review

Discuss confinement, refugees, family separation, inspection of bodies, and coercion. Hidden passengers must be portrayed as people with agency and disagreement.

### Expansion

End the frame on arrival at one selected world. Do not turn the heighliner into unlimited access to Guild secrets.

## `dune-frame-maker-margin` — The Maker's Margin

- Status: package-original proposal
- Compatible era: `late-corrino-pre-transfer`
- Opening theater: boundary between `dune-region-hagga` and `dune-region-deep-desert`
- Party mandate: survey and recovery team for an original concession
- Opening scale: one weather station, one seismic line, one rock refuge

### Player-Facing Promise

A survey line intended to open new spice ground has gone silent. Security reports Fremen sabotage; the surviving technicians report impossible readings and missing charges. Recover the team before a storm closes the route, then decide whether the survey should ever resume.

### Visible Situation

- Instruments show subsurface moisture where official models predict none.
- Several explosive charges were removed rather than detonated.
- A wounded scout refuses to identify who saved them.
- The contractor promises extraordinary yield if blasting continues.
- A Fremen signal warns the party away without offering negotiation.

### Actor Configuration

- Survey contractor under quota pressure
- Technical lead who suppressed anomalous readings
- House security officer seeking a clear enemy
- Worker survivors who want rescue before blame
- Specific Fremen contact group protecting a route and community
- Patron representative deciding whether evidence reaches Arrakeen

### Causal Chain

1. The contractor shifts a survey line beyond its authorized boundary.
2. Readings suggest a water-linked ecological formation and a route used by a nearby community.
3. The technical lead removes charges and falsifies progress to buy time.
4. Fremen scouts disable instruments but rescue workers caught by the first collapse.
5. Security interprets every sign through sabotage doctrine.
6. The storm will erase evidence and strand anyone left at the station.

### Neutral Action Space

The PCs can rescue, negotiate, preserve or destroy readings, relocate the survey, expose the overreach, bargain over access, or protect the route without learning its destination. Understanding the full spice cycle is not required to recognize dangerous intrusion.

### Inaction Trajectory

Security detonates the remaining line, the storm traps the team, the protected route is exposed, and retaliation destroys the concession's ability to operate safely.

### PC Integration Prompts

- What makes you credible to workers, security, or desert travelers?
- What piece of equipment are you personally responsible for recovering?
- Which kind of knowledge do you refuse to take without consent?
- What does your patron believe the desert owes them?

### GM-Only Hidden Truth

Hidden-truth id: `dune-frame-maker-secret-001`

No single sabotage caused the failure. The contractor knowingly crossed the boundary; the technical lead removed charges after recognizing ecological danger; Fremen scouts disabled the line to protect a route and then saved trapped workers. The anomalous readings are real but insufficient to map a sietch or fully explain the spice cycle.

### Reveal Requirements

Require authorization records, charge custody, survivor testimony, and negotiated observation of the site. Do not reveal a sietch location as a reward.

### Safe Foreshadowing

- Removed charges are stored safely, not stolen.
- Rescue knots or water treatment do not match House procedure.
- The technical lead fears the contractor more than the reported attackers.
- Instrument damage prevents mapping but leaves warning data intact.

### Forbidden Early Wording

Do not state that Fremen caused the collapse, that the readings reveal a secret reservoir, or that the PCs have discovered the complete spice mechanism.

### Boundaries And Review

Discuss environmental destruction, extraction, outsider intrusion, and cultural secrecy. A Fremen-centered version needs specific Session 0 consent and internal community voices.

### Expansion

Expansion follows relationship: the selected community may offer a second meeting, not automatic access to its sietch.

## `dune-frame-golden-audience` — The Golden Audience

- Status: package-original proposal
- Compatible era: late Corrino Imperium after targeted court review
- Opening theater: `dune-region-kaitain`
- Party mandate: delegation of an original House seeking a CHOAM advantage
- Opening scale: one court precinct, one directorship hearing, one allied delegation

### Player-Facing Promise

Your House can secure a commercial appointment that would end a generation of debt. The price is a public dossier condemning an allied House for the same off-ledger trade that once kept your own world alive. Win the audience, test the evidence, and decide whether survival requires selective truth.

### Visible Situation

- The dossier's documents authenticate correctly.
- The accused ally asks for one private day before the hearing.
- The appointment sponsor controls access but not the final vote.
- A CHOAM bloc benefits whether the ally falls or submits.
- Servants and dependents from both delegations will bear the first sanctions.

### Actor Configuration

- Patron House ruler participating at distance
- PC delegation with limited authority
- Accused allied House and its divided envoys
- Appointment sponsor seeking a new voting bloc
- CHOAM factor who assembled the dossier
- Court intermediary who can authenticate provenance but not motive

### Causal Chain

1. Several Houses used the same off-ledger network during an earlier supply crisis.
2. The ally continued after the emergency and displaced a sponsor-backed monopoly.
3. The sponsor isolates evidence against the ally and offers the PCs its vacated advantage.
4. The patron House's predecessor benefited from the original network.
5. Full disclosure harms every participant; selective disclosure creates dependency.
6. The hearing's ritual deadline converts ambiguity into public position.

### Neutral Action Space

The PCs can accept, bargain, disclose symmetrically, build a third bloc, protect affected communities, delay with valid procedure, or sacrifice the appointment. No roll can make every consequence disappear.

### Inaction Trajectory

The sponsor releases the selective dossier through another House, the ally falls, the patron gains nothing, and evidence of its own benefit remains available for later coercion.

### PC Integration Prompts

- What does ending the House's debt change for your home?
- Which court skill or relationship did you choose to bring?
- What public statement would violate your character's values?
- Who outside the nobility must not become an invisible cost?

### GM-Only Hidden Truth

Hidden-truth id: `dune-frame-audience-secret-001`

The dossier is genuine but deliberately incomplete. The patron House and sponsor both benefited from the same network during the emergency. The sponsor's goal is not moral enforcement; it is to break an independent trade bloc and bind the patron through shared exposure.

### Reveal Requirements

Require provenance across multiple ledgers, testimony from a non-noble participant, and proof that the sponsor possessed excluded records before making the offer.

### Safe Foreshadowing

- Page numbering shows removed schedules.
- The sponsor discusses loyalty more than the alleged harm.
- An old patron seal appears in a transport appendix.
- A servant recognizes a route name the delegates call obsolete.

### Forbidden Early Wording

Do not call the dossier forged, the ally innocent, or the sponsor omniscient.

### Boundaries And Review

Discuss economic sanctions, inherited debt, coerced testimony, and elite decisions harming dependents.

### Expansion

House-ruler or architect play follows only after the audience resolves and the group accepts broader political scope.

## `dune-frame-bornless-horizon` — The Bornless Horizon

- Status: package-original proposal for an official alternate-continuity premise
- Required continuity: `awakening-alternate`
- Opening theater: alternate Arrakis; do not import default-era outcomes
- Party mandate: independent survival cell with optional Atreides or Harkonnen ties
- Opening scale: one storm-opened canyon, one temporary base, one contested salvage site

### Player-Facing Promise

In the timeline where Paul Atreides was never born, a coriolis storm exposes a sealed desert installation. Both Atreides and Harkonnen agents claim it, while independent survivors need its water machinery before the next storm cycle. Build enough power to reach the site, then decide who controls what you recover.

### Visible Situation

- The exposed structure contains old water-recovery equipment and contradictory House markings.
- A survey team vanished after transmitting a partial map.
- Atreides and Harkonnen recruiters offer protection for exclusive claim.
- Independent settlers cannot survive another equipment failure.
- Worm movement makes permanent occupation uncertain.

### Actor Configuration

- Independent settlement council
- Atreides field representative
- Harkonnen field representative
- Rival salvager with the missing map segment
- School-trained mentor or specialist selected by the players
- Desert survivor group with prior knowledge of the canyon

### Causal Chain

1. An older installation was abandoned after its water machinery destabilized local ground.
2. A recent storm exposes access and scatters records bearing marks from multiple custodians.
3. The first survey team splits over whether to sell, share, or reseal the site.
4. One member falsifies the map to prevent immediate faction seizure.
5. Competing bases consume more water while racing to recover the machinery.
6. Another storm and worm migration will make delay costly.

### Neutral Action Space

The PCs can salvage, repair, share, bargain, misdirect, reseal, choose a faction, remain independent, or move the settlement. Base construction supports decisions; it is not proof of ownership.

### Inaction Trajectory

Both Houses escalate, the independent settlement exhausts reserves, the rival salvager sells the true route, and fighting destroys the recoverable machinery.

### PC Integration Prompts

- Which home planet and mentor did you choose, if using the game's character premise?
- Why are you independent, faction-aligned, or undecided?
- What part of survival work do others rely on you to perform?
- What would make a stronghold a home rather than a claim marker?

### GM-Only Hidden Truth

Hidden-truth id: `dune-frame-bornless-secret-001`

The installation changed hands repeatedly; no present faction has an uncontested ancestral claim. The missing survey team divided voluntarily. Its mapmaker hid the stable route because removing the largest condenser would collapse the remaining underground catchment and destroy the site for everyone.

### Reveal Requirements

Require structural inspection, records from at least two custodial periods, and contact with surviving survey members. Do not tie the answer to the franchise-wide mystery of the missing Fremen.

### Safe Foreshadowing

- House emblems occupy different construction layers.
- The portable condenser is physically tied to older channels.
- The map's false path avoids damaging terrain rather than leading into an ambush.
- Survey messages use plural voices after the reported disappearance.

### Forbidden Early Wording

Do not claim the installation explains the missing Fremen, belongs canonically to a famous House, or contains a destiny-defining artifact.

### Boundaries And Review

Discuss survival extraction, base raiding, faction coercion, body-fluid recovery, and PvP expectations. Define whether player structures can be permanently lost.

### Expansion

Use official *Dune: Awakening* locations, characters, and plot only after a targeted continuity check. This frame remains a local original problem.

## Frame Materialization Rule

Materialize exactly one frame. Copy its visible premise, selected actors, causal chain, and hidden truth into the appropriate campaign owners. Do not copy comparison ratings, alternate frames, or another frame's GM secret. Re-name every placeholder through Session 0; a placeholder is not accepted truth.
