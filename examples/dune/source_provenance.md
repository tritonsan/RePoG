# Dune Source Provenance

- Retrieval cutoff: 2026-07-23
- Primary literary focus: Frank Herbert's six Dune novels
- Default-era focus: the late Corrino Imperium before the opening events of *Dune*
- Playability focus: licensed *Dune: Adventures in the Imperium* product descriptions
- Adaptation focus: explicit separation of expanded novels, Legendary screen works, *Dune: Prophecy*, and *Dune: Awakening*
- Attribution mode: bibliographic references, links, and paraphrased claim summaries
- Verbatim source text: not stored
- Fan-wiki authority: none
- Commercial-text limitation: full book text was not independently re-read during this package pass; broad primary-text claims were kept conservative and cross-checked against public licensed descriptions where possible

## Source Register — Screen And Game Continuities

### `src-dune-prophecy`

- Title: *Dune: Prophecy*
- Publisher: HBO / Warner Bros. Discovery
- URL: https://www.hbo.com/dune-prophecy
- Role: official screen-series statement that the story is ten thousand years before Paul, follows a Sisterhood that becomes the Bene Gesserit, and is inspired by *Sisterhood of Dune*
- Confidence: high for series premise and continuity separation

### `src-dune-awakening`

- Title: *Dune: Awakening*
- Publisher: Funcom
- URL: https://duneawakening.com/
- Role: official open-world survival premise, Arrakis activities, Atreides/Harkonnen paths, character customization, and explicit question “what if Paul Atreides was never born?”
- Confidence: high for alternate-timeline and gameplay premise
