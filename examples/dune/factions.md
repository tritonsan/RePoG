# Dune Factions And Institutions

## Use Contract

These are stable power models, not active factions. A campaign instantiates only two or three at opening scale, each with a local face, place, objective, method, limit, clock, and knowledge boundary. No institution is a hive mind; local members may dissent, misunderstand, or pursue personal aims.

## Power Map

| Power | Primary leverage | Structural dependency | Default opening role |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Imperial House and Sardaukar | legitimacy, appointment, force, fear | Landsraad balance, Guild transport, spice | distant authority or patron's patron |
| Great and Minor Houses | fiefs, retainers, votes, kanly, production | Emperor, Guild, CHOAM, populations | patron, rival, or affected peer |
| CHOAM interests | ownership, contracts, dividends, quotas | House participation, spice flow, transport | auditor, buyer, or pressure source |
| Spacing Guild | interstellar transport and protected knowledge | spice, neutrality, customers | gatekeeper or distant constraint |
| Bene Gesserit | training, observation, networks, long plans | access, secrecy, human relationships | adviser, examiner, or independent agenda |
| Fremen communities | desert knowledge, mobility, solidarity, hidden infrastructure | water, secrecy, internal consent | affected power, ally, rival, or protagonists |
| Arrakis governing apparatus | law, security, concessions, records | ruling House, labor, water, production | immediate authority or antagonist |
| Smuggler and merchant networks | off-ledger movement, buyers, information | routes, trust, bribery, desert competence | broker, competitor, or lifeline |
| Human Schools and specialists | scarce trained capability and reputation | patrons, doctrine, graduates' loyalty | PC origin, adviser, or contested asset |
| Ixian and Tleilaxu interests | specialized knowledge, bodies, or technology | secrecy, patrons, continuity-specific institutions | opt-in complication only |

## Faction Cards

### `dune-faction-imperial` — Imperial House And Sardaukar

- Status: template power; exact ruler and deployment are era-sensitive
- Public mask: guarantor of order, appointments, hierarchy, and inter-House stability
- Desire: preserve the throne's supremacy while preventing any House or alliance from becoming unmanageable
- Methods: fief grants, honors, marriage, court access, intelligence, coercive example, Sardaukar force, and manipulation of rivals
- Capabilities: legitimacy, elite military power, court network, patronage, and the ability to redefine a local dispute as Imperial concern
- Limits: Landsraad resistance, Guild transport, spice dependence, imperfect intelligence, cost of overt tyranny, and internal succession politics
- Knowledge habits: compartmentalized court reporting, tests of loyalty, deniable intermediaries, and deliberate ambiguity
- Local instantiation: an envoy, judge, observer, Sardaukar officer, or sealed instruction—not the Emperor by default
- Hooks: disputed appointment, secret audit, dangerous favor, threatened intervention, or evidence that could embarrass the throne
- Caution: do not use Imperial force as an arbitrary GM reset; establish trigger, logistics, objective, and political cost
- Source claims: `claim-dune-feudal-imperium`, `claim-dune-power-balance`, `claim-dune-kaitain`.

### `dune-faction-houses` — Great And Minor Houses Of The Landsraad

- Status: template ecosystem, not one faction
- Public mask: honorable stewards of fiefs and peers defending ancient rights
- Desire: preserve lineage and holdings, increase influence, prevent rivals from achieving decisive advantage
- Methods: alliance, marriage, patronage, espionage, kanly, production, CHOAM maneuver, military readiness, and reputation
- Capabilities: local law, household specialists, armed retainers, domain resources, votes, and intergenerational memory
- Limits: debt, succession, Guild access, Imperial appointment, dependent populations, rival coalitions, and the need to appear legitimate
- Knowledge habits: household archives, Mentat analysis, spies, coded messages, family secrets, and selective disclosure to allies
- Local instantiation: one original patron House and at most one immediate rival at opening; every House needs a distinct economy, virtue claim, injustice, and internal divide
- Hooks: concession rivalry, heir dispute, insult, disputed evidence, marriage offer, CHOAM vote, or a kanly action whose legality is uncertain
- Caution: noble status is not moral worth; show who produces House wealth and who pays for rivalry
- Source claims: `claim-dune-house-play`, `claim-dune-kanly-convention`, `claim-dune-feudal-imperium`, `claim-dune-play-scales`.

### `dune-faction-choam` — CHOAM Interests

- Status: template institution; exact ownership and voting require targeted research
- Public mask: commercial coordination and lawful distribution across the Imperium
- Desire: maintain profitable flow, reliable obligations, and leverage among shareholders and Houses
- Methods: contracts, directorships, audits, quotas, price pressure, access, preferential terms, and authenticated records
- Capabilities: economic information, cross-House stakes, buyers, inspectors, and the ability to turn local failure into a reputational crisis
- Limits: it does not move independently of powerful participants; it depends on production, Guild transport, enforceable agreements, and political backing
- Knowledge habits: ledgers, forecasts, manifests, compartmentalized ownership, and competing interpretations of the same shortage
- Local instantiation: an auditor, factor, contracting office, or buyer with one measurable demand and one conflict of interest
- Hooks: impossible quota, missing dividend, false manifest, stockpile, emergency purchase, or an audit timed to favor a rival
- Caution: do not invent exact corporate procedure or share structure without source verification
- Source claims: `claim-dune-choam`, `claim-dune-power-balance`, `claim-dune-arrakis-spice`.

### `dune-faction-guild` — Spacing Guild

- Status: template institution with protected internal knowledge
- Public mask: neutral provider of safe interstellar passage
- Desire: preserve transport monopoly, spice access, operational secrecy, and a political order that does not threaten those foundations
- Methods: price, schedule, refusal, routing, manifest control, selective information, and carefully limited representation
- Capabilities: rapid interstellar movement, strategic bottleneck power, protected expertise, and insight unavailable to ordinary travelers
- Limits: profound spice dependence, need for customers to preserve the system, institutional caution, and risks created by open political alignment
- Knowledge habits: reveals only what a transaction or survival requires; separates operational truth from passenger understanding
- Local instantiation: a factor, envoy, security representative, manifest officer, or schedule decision—not a fully explained Navigator
- Hooks: delayed passage, undeclared cargo, price shock, sealed passenger, route anomaly, or a favor that compromises neutrality
- Caution: mystery is not omnipotence; define what the local representative can actually authorize
- Source claims: `claim-dune-guild-travel`, `claim-dune-arrakis-spice`, `claim-dune-power-balance`.

### `dune-faction-bene-gesserit` — Bene Gesserit Sisterhood

- Status: template institution; era-sensitive aims and ranks require research
- Public mask: educators, advisers, companions, observers, and disciplined specialists
- Desire: shape human possibilities and preserve long designs through access, training, lineage, memory, and influence
- Methods: observation, social positioning, education, bodily discipline, selective truth, testing, marriage counsel, networks, and Missionaria Protectiva groundwork
- Capabilities: exceptional trained awareness and control, institutional memory, cross-House access, and long planning horizons
- Limits: incomplete knowledge, disagreement, dependence on relationships and placement, institutional blind spots, and individuals' choices
- Knowledge habits: layered disclosure, coded recognition, tests, need-to-know agendas, and careful separation of personal from institutional speech
- Local instantiation: one Sister, acolyte, adviser, examiner, or contact with a specific assignment and personal interpretation
- Hooks: a test with political consequences, conflicting instructions, protected pupil, planted legend, lineage concern, or request for quiet access
- Caution: never assign a PC hidden conditioning, reproductive duty, allegiance, or pregnancy without explicit player consent
- Source claims: `claim-dune-human-schools`, `claim-dune-missionaria`, `claim-dune-religion`.

### `dune-faction-fremen` — Fremen Communities

- Status: plural template; each sietch is separately instantiated
- Public mask: outsiders often misread Fremen as scattered desert inhabitants or raiders
- Desire: survive with dignity, protect community and water, keep routes and numbers secret, pursue locally held political and ecological futures
- Methods: desert mobility, concealment, mutual obligation, selective trade, intelligence, sabotage, negotiation, ritual, and force
- Capabilities: ecological knowledge, stillsuit discipline, terrain mastery, hidden infrastructure, strong social memory, and the ability to act where Imperial systems fail
- Limits: water, secrecy, internal obligation, cost of exposure, disagreement among communities, and pressure from extraction regimes
- Knowledge habits: access follows relationship and responsibility; outsiders receive only what a specific community chooses to risk
- Local instantiation: one named sietch or mobile group with internal disagreement, non-PC goals, and a decision process
- Hooks: threatened route, water debt, missing person, ecological intrusion, disputed trade, refuge request, or competing responses to off-world contact
- Caution: no monolithic culture, automatic alliance, outsider savior, or instant access to sacred/survival knowledge
- Source claims: `claim-dune-fremen`, `claim-dune-water`, `claim-dune-worm-spice-cycle`, `claim-dune-religion`.

### `dune-faction-arrakis-rule` — Arrakis Governing Apparatus

- Status: campaign-instance; defaults to a Harkonnen administration only in `late-corrino-pre-transfer`
- Public mask: lawful steward of spice production, security, water, and Imperial obligation
- Desire: meet quota, preserve control, conceal weakness, reward loyal offices, and survive scrutiny from House superiors and rivals
- Methods: concessions, ration authority, security, labor control, licensing, records, informants, patrols, and punishment
- Capabilities: immediate jurisdiction, facilities, armed agents, water access, employment, and control of official narratives
- Limits: hostile ecology, labor dependence, smuggling, corruption, Fremen agency, off-world politics, and distance between offices
- Knowledge habits: offices hoard data, alter ledgers, report upward selectively, and compete to define failure
- Local instantiation: one concession overseer, one security face, one water or records official, and one internal rival; never an anonymous total apparatus
- Hooks: impossible order, missing ledger, jurisdiction conflict, compromised carryall, collective punishment, or audit panic
- Caution: if coercion is central, establish boundaries and affected-community agency
- Source claims: `claim-dune-house-play`, `claim-dune-arrakeen-carthag`, `claim-dune-class-daily-life`, `claim-dune-arrakis-spice`.

### `dune-faction-smugglers` — Smuggler And Merchant Networks

- Status: template ecosystem, not one guild
- Public mask: merchants, guides, transport contractors, salvage crews, or people who know whom to ask
- Desire: preserve routes, earn profit, protect crews and communities, avoid monopoly control, and keep options open
- Methods: hidden caches, bribery, barter, off-ledger manifests, local alliances, false identity, salvage, and information brokerage
- Capabilities: flexible movement, buyers, deniable contacts, knowledge across social boundaries, and access where official systems are slow
- Limits: trust, weather, water, betrayal, House retaliation, Guild dependence for off-world scale, and uneven local legitimacy
- Knowledge habits: rumors are priced, routes compartmentalized, and proof disclosed only when payment or obligation is credible
- Local instantiation: one crew or network with a livelihood beyond helping PCs, a protected community, and a line it will not cross
- Hooks: poisoned deal, stolen water, hidden passenger, spice stockpile, disputed salvage, or route endangered by House action
- Caution: neither romantic freedom fighters nor faceless criminals by default
- Source claims: `claim-dune-play-scales`, `claim-dune-arrakeen-carthag`, `claim-dune-class-daily-life`.

### `dune-faction-schools` — Human Schools And Specialists

- Status: institutional family; instantiate the selected School separately
- Public mask: trusted expertise in analysis, medicine, combat, counsel, or disciplined service
- Desire: preserve reputation, training line, access, and influence while graduates navigate patron obligations
- Methods: selection, intensive training, professional norms, placement, certification, secret doctrine, and alumni relationships
- Capabilities: scarce human performance that substitutes for prohibited automation or ordinary training
- Limits: human bias, loyalty, incomplete inputs, conditioning, patron control, and institutional politics
- Knowledge habits: doctrine and technique are layered; a graduate does not automatically know another School's secrets
- Local instantiation: a Mentat, Suk doctor, Ginaz-trained Swordmaster, or another selected specialist with a personal ethic and explicit employer
- Hooks: disputed diagnosis, poisoned analysis, stolen pupil, conflicting oath, succession need, or specialist whose neutrality is doubted
- Caution: a character archetype is not an organization-wide personality; verify exact abilities and conditioning before they decide a plot
- Source claims: `claim-dune-limits-computers`, `claim-dune-human-schools`.

### `dune-faction-ixian` — Ixian Interests

- Status: opt-in and continuity-sensitive
- Public mask: suppliers of sophisticated devices and technical solutions
- Desire: preserve markets, secrecy, autonomy, and advantage near the edge of accepted technology
- Methods: contracts, prototypes, compartmentalized research, intermediaries, espionage, and plausible compliance
- Capabilities: specialized technical knowledge, coveted devices, industrial networks, and clients among powerful institutions
- Limits: political suspicion, prohibition boundaries, sabotage, customer dependence, and era-specific constraints
- Knowledge habits: design knowledge is fragmented and hidden behind commercial secrecy
- Local instantiation: one envoy, engineer, workshop, or procurement contract after targeted research
- Hooks: suspect device, stolen design, failed prototype, Imperial inquiry, or buyer demanding impossible deniability
- Caution: do not call every advanced device illegal or import later-era technology into early play
- Source claims: `claim-dune-limits-computers`, `claim-dune-human-schools`, `claim-dune-kaitain`.

### `dune-faction-tleilaxu` — Tleilaxu Interests

- Status: opt-in, continuity-sensitive, and high body-autonomy review
- Public mask: specialized biological services, guarded expertise, and intermediaries whose full purpose is unclear
- Desire: varies sharply by era and source; must be researched rather than inferred
- Methods: biological expertise, secrecy, bargaining, infiltration, and long projects
- Capabilities: campaign-specific only after source verification
- Limits: mistrust, political hostility, internal doctrine, dependency on access, and continuity-specific constraints
- Knowledge habits: extreme compartmentalization and deceptive presentation
- Local instantiation: one bounded contract or representative; never a generic surprise shapeshifter without setup
- Hooks: identity doubt, forbidden contract, medical bargain, missing specialist, or evidence whose body-source matters
- Caution: requires explicit boundaries around body horror, identity violation, reproductive coercion, and dehumanization
- Source claims: `claim-dune-human-schools`, `claim-dune-kaitain`.

## Opening Faction Budget

For `dune-frame-dry-ledger`, instantiate only:

1. the original Minor House field office as patron and divided institution
2. the local Arrakis governing office as authority and pressure
3. one affected local organization: workers, water keepers, smugglers, or a specific Fremen contact group

CHOAM and the Guild may begin as deadlines or constraints without local faces. Add a face only when the PCs can interact with that institution.

## Faction Clock Contract

Every active faction clock records:

- desired end state
- next action if unopposed
- required capability
- visible sign
- people who pay the cost
- information the faction lacks
- condition that changes its plan

A faction does not advance merely because it is famous in canon.
