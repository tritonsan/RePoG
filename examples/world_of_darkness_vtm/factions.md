# World of Darkness: Vampire: The Masquerade Factions, Sects, And Clans

## Use Contract

These are stable power models and player-facing lineage prompts, not active factions. A campaign instantiates only two or three opening powers, each with a local face, place, objective, method, limit, clock, and knowledge boundary. No sect, clan, hunter force, or supernatural category is a hive mind.

## Power Map

| Power | Primary leverage | Structural dependency | Default opening role |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Camarilla court | domain recognition, status, boons, offices, Masquerade enforcement | mortal infrastructure, compliance, secrecy, contested legitimacy | patron, authority, or rival |
| Anarch movement | local solidarity, direct action, territory, flexible coalitions | neighborhood relationships, defense, internal negotiation | alternative authority or divided ally |
| Autarkis and independents | mobility, specialized ties, refusal of formal allegiance | feeding access, havens, tolerated borders, personal reputation | broker, party posture, or wildcard |
| Thin-blood networks | adaptation, mutual aid, alchemy, overlooked routes | precarious status, blood access, trust, protection | affected community, ally, rival, or party basis |
| Sabbat cells | ideological commitment, terror, infiltration, Ritae, war posture | secrecy, recruitment, fanaticism, hostile environment | antagonist only by default |
| Second Inquisition forces | institutional data, raids, equipment, covert mandate | uncertain intelligence, jurisdiction, secrecy, human personnel | escalating antagonist |
| Independent hunters | local knowledge, conviction, community access, improvisation | limited resources, imperfect evidence, personal cost | antagonist, rival, witness, or uneasy ally |
| Blood-craft scene | rituals, formulae, occult markets, specialized knowledge | ingredients, secrecy, teachers, dangerous experimentation | patron, market, mystery, or temptation |
| Mortal communities and institutions | daylight action, legitimacy, labor, records, memory, refusal | resources, law, trust, internal disagreement | causal stakeholders, never scenery |
| Other supernatural presences | capabilities and motives outside vampire society | source-specific limits and their own conflicts | rare threat, boundary, or unknown |

## Sect And Institution Cards

### `wod-vtm-faction-camarilla` — Camarilla Court

- Status: current V5 template; exact offices and membership are local
- Public-to-Kindred mask: the institution that preserves the Masquerade, order, and recognized domain
- Desire: maintain secrecy and hierarchy while surviving hunter pressure, elder absence, Anarch challenge, and internal decay
- Methods: recognition, boons, status, court ritual, domain grants, Elysium, selective protection, investigation, punishment, and mortal influence
- Capabilities: accumulated havens, retainers, records, wealth, experienced agents, and the power to make a local act socially legible
- Limits: incomplete control, modern evidence, factional courts, dependence on mortals, absent elders, and subjects who can defect or resist
- Knowledge habits: oral obligation, compartmentalized offices, curated history, and punishment for information that threatens legitimacy
- Local instantiation: choose only offices the city needs; a Prince, council, Sheriff, Harpy, Keeper, or Hound is never mandatory merely because the title exists elsewhere
- Hooks: provisional domain, disputed boon, missing officeholder, unauthorized Embrace, exposed feeding route, or evidence the court cannot admit it lost
- Caution: neo-feudal order is not neutral safety; show who pays for enforcement
- Source claims: `claim-wod-vtm-camarilla`, `claim-wod-vtm-kindred-masquerade`, `claim-wod-vtm-domain-boons`, `claim-wod-vtm-digital-risk`.

### `wod-vtm-faction-anarch` — Anarch Movement

- Status: current V5 template; local form may be a barony, council, coalition, crew network, or contested neighborhood settlement
- Public-to-Kindred mask: freedom from elder tyranny and the right to shape one's own nights
- Desire: protect autonomy, territory, and chosen community while resisting Camarilla claims and internal domination
- Methods: direct action, mutual aid, reputation, local patrols, assemblies, personal leadership, sabotage, negotiation, and control of practical access
- Capabilities: flexible networks, current local knowledge, neighborhood ties, rapid mobilization, and fewer inherited procedures
- Limits: uneven resources, personality-driven authority, internal disagreement, hunter pressure, and the gap between anti-tyranny rhetoric and actual practice
- Knowledge habits: relationship-based trust, local channels, rumor, and selective transparency
- Local instantiation: define who can decide, who can refuse, how territory is defended, and what the movement owes mortals living there
- Hooks: contested block, compromised Baron, mutual-aid route, Camarilla offer, thin-blood exclusion, or protection claim residents did not request
- Caution: do not equate Anarch with morally good, leaderless, young, or politically uniform
- Source claims: `claim-wod-vtm-anarch`, `claim-wod-vtm-domain-boons`.

### `wod-vtm-faction-autarkis` — Autarkis And Independents

- Status: campaign-instance ecosystem, not one organization
- Public-to-Kindred mask: self-rule, neutrality, clan obligation, family interest, professional service, or refusal to be counted
- Desire: preserve chosen autonomy, livelihood, haven, and relationships without surrendering to a local sect
- Methods: brokerage, mobility, specialized service, carefully balanced favors, hidden feeding, clan networks, and selective alliances
- Capabilities: cross-boundary contacts, deniability, expertise, and the ability to speak where formal rivals cannot
- Limits: no guaranteed protection, fragile feeding access, suspicion from every side, and dependence on personal credibility
- Knowledge habits: private ledgers, compartmentalized contacts, and truths priced by risk rather than ideology
- Local instantiation: one individual, family, coterie, or service network with a reason neutrality remains possible
- Hooks: safe passage, contested haven, debt inherited from a client, information sale, or a neutrality test
- Caution: independence is a political condition, not freedom from consequences
- Source claims: `claim-wod-vtm-domain-boons`, `claim-wod-vtm-coterie-play`.

### `wod-vtm-faction-thin-blood` — Thin-Blood And Duskborn Networks

- Status: current V5 vampire context; playable only through selected V5 support
- Public-to-Kindred mask: varies from disposable outsiders or feared omen to adaptable neighbors and self-organized survivors
- Desire: survive, secure blood and shelter, develop leverage, protect one another, and define identity beyond full-blooded vampire judgment
- Methods: mutual aid, day-adjacent or overlooked access where supported by the rules, Thin-Blood Alchemy, information exchange, flexible feeding, and mobility
- Capabilities: adaptation, underestimated routes, alchemical practice, mortal proximity, and knowledge of systems established Kindred ignore
- Limits: prejudice, unstable protection, uneven capabilities, exploitation, scarce teachers, and hunter attention
- Knowledge habits: peer teaching, coded exchange, cautious markets, and practical testing
- Local instantiation: define who belongs, what “thin-blood” means socially, what capabilities owned rules permit, and who is allowed to leave
- Hooks: missing formula, threatened shelter, scapegoat accusation, blood shortage, cure rumor, or patron demanding ownership
- Caution: do not treat thin-bloods as lesser people, automatic prophets, or a universal cure plot
- Source claims: `claim-wod-vtm-thin-bloods`, `claim-wod-vtm-blood-craft`, `claim-wod-vtm-clans-current`.

### `wod-vtm-faction-sabbat` — Sabbat Cell

- Status: antagonist-first under inspected V5 source
- Public-to-Kindred mask: crusaders against Antediluvians and the constraints of Humanity, bound through ideology and Ritae
- Desire: pursue the Gehenna War, destroy or subvert enemies, and replace individual human attachment with cultic purpose
- Methods: infiltration, destabilization, terror, recruitment, mass violence, Ritae, Paths, and weaponized breaches
- Capabilities: commitment, supernatural violence, willingness to violate norms, hidden cells, and knowledge framed by war
- Limits: hostile territory, fanatic interpretation, internal doctrine, hunter pressure, logistics, and enemies created by atrocity
- Knowledge habits: revelation through ideology, ritualized secrecy, and information valued for war
- Local instantiation: one bounded cell with a specific target and capability; never “the Sabbat attacks” without route, objective, and evidence
- Hooks: missing fledgling, desecrated haven, false-flag breach, war relic, infiltrated cult, or a target the local court wants hidden
- Caution: set explicit boundaries around cult abuse, dehumanization, coercive bonding, atrocity, and body horror
- Source claims: `claim-wod-vtm-sabbat-antagonists`, `claim-wod-vtm-beckoning-gehenna`, `claim-wod-vtm-noddist-myth`.

### `wod-vtm-faction-second-inquisition` — Second Inquisition Forces

- Status: umbrella antagonist model, not one unified agency
- Public mask: ordinary state, church, intelligence, security, or contracted functions whose supernatural mandate is concealed
- Desire: identify and neutralize vampire threats while preserving secrecy, jurisdiction, budgets, doctrine, or institutional advantage
- Methods: data correlation, surveillance, informants, raids, asset seizure, interrogation, specialist equipment, and exploitation of vampire mistakes
- Capabilities: daylight action, legal or covert access, logistics, trained teams, institutional records, and mortal-scale persistence
- Limits: compartmentalization, false positives, rival jurisdictions, secrecy, political exposure, human conscience, incomplete supernatural understanding, and finite resources
- Knowledge habits: classified fragments, operational labels, pattern analysis, and deliberate separation between collectors and strike teams
- Local instantiation: define the exact cell, sponsor, data source, legal cover, confidence, and response threshold
- Hooks: flagged clip, burned haven, compromised ghoul, seized archive, false identity collision, or operation another agency does not understand
- Caution: never grant unexplained omniscience or turn torture and surveillance into competence aesthetics
- Source claims: `claim-wod-vtm-second-inquisition`, `claim-wod-vtm-digital-risk`, `claim-wod-vtm-camarilla`.

### `wod-vtm-faction-hunters` — Independent Hunters

- Status: H5-informed non-player ecosystem; not H5 player play
- Public mask: neighbors, survivors, researchers, activists, faith communities, conspiracy groups, criminals, or professionals following a pattern
- Desire: protect people, prove an experience, avenge harm, gain power, serve doctrine, or survive what they learned
- Methods: observation, community knowledge, improvised traps, records, infiltration, publicity threats, and alliances of necessity
- Capabilities: daylight mobility, mortal trust, local legitimacy, unpredictability, and the ability to notice consequences vampires normalize
- Limits: danger, trauma, incomplete lore, disagreement, limited equipment, legal exposure, and manipulation by larger organizations
- Knowledge habits: case files, testimony, folklore, experimentation, and errors reinforced by fear
- Local instantiation: one person or cell with a witnessed cause, a line it will not cross, and a theory that is partly wrong
- Hooks: survivor organizing, missing person search, copied evidence, mistaken identity, shared enemy, or hunter targeted by the Second Inquisition
- Caution: hunters are neither automatic heroes nor disposable fanatics; no hunter becomes a PC in this package
- Source claims: `claim-wod-h5-separate`, `claim-wod-vtm-kindred-masquerade`.

### `wod-vtm-faction-blood-trade` — Blood-Craft And Occult Market

- Status: V5 subject layer; exact practices require owned sources
- Public-to-Kindred mask: teachers, collectors, ritualists, alchemists, fixers, desperate clients, and traders in dangerous knowledge
- Desire: acquire ingredients, students, formulae, access, prestige, safety, or a new rule of blood
- Methods: apprenticeship, barter, theft, experiment, ritual, coded market, favors, and control of scarce texts or sites
- Capabilities: Blood Sorcery, Thin-Blood Alchemy, occult contacts, specialized tools, and knowledge that can alter a local balance
- Limits: Hunger, ingredients, source-specific procedures, rival schools, bodily cost, unstable experiments, and evidence left by procurement
- Knowledge habits: guarded recipes, lineage claims, counterfeit texts, demonstrations, and truth mixed with market performance
- Local instantiation: one seller, teacher, buyer, victim, site, and transaction; define what is verified versus claimed
- Hooks: stolen sigil, poisoned batch, impossible formula, missing donor, counterfeit grimoire, or ritual that attracts another presence
- Caution: establish boundaries for blood use, medical harm, body horror, coercion, and cultural/religious appropriation
- Source claims: `claim-wod-vtm-blood-craft`, `claim-wod-vtm-thin-bloods`, `claim-wod-vtm-blood-bonds-ghouls`.

### `wod-vtm-faction-mortals` — Mortal Communities And Institutions

- Status: mandatory causal layer, never one faction
- Public mask: none; these are people living in the city, not a supernatural category
- Desire: work, care, safety, justice, status, livelihood, belonging, truth, privacy, and countless goals unrelated to vampires
- Methods: daylight action, law, labor, refusal, organizing, reporting, care, commerce, technology, faith, art, and memory
- Capabilities: institutional continuity, numbers, public legitimacy, data, relationships, and the ability to change conditions vampires depend on
- Limits: unequal power, incomplete knowledge, internal conflict, mortal vulnerability, and institutions that may fail or exploit them
- Knowledge habits: experience, records, rumor, expertise, community memory, and mundane explanations
- Local instantiation: at least two groups with different interests; every feeding ground must also be a lived place
- Hooks: unsafe venue campaign, tenant action, hospital shortage, missing person, whistleblower, labor dispute, or refusal to host a vampire-backed project
- Caution: never call all mortals “the herd” in neutral package narration
- Source claims: `claim-wod-vtm-personal-political-horror`, `claim-wod-vtm-touchstones-convictions`, `claim-wod-vtm-mature-content`.

### `wod-vtm-faction-other-supernatural` — Other Supernatural Presences

- Status: non-player, source-gated, perspective-limited
- Public mask: unknown until instantiated; may appear entirely mundane or be misidentified by vampires
- Desire: must come from the selected line and local individual, not from vampire stereotype
- Methods: unknown until sourced
- Capabilities: bounded to observed or researched facts
- Limits: source-specific and mandatory; mystery is not omnipotence
- Knowledge habits: their own, not automatically legible to Kindred
- Local instantiation: one presence at most in a beginner opening, with a reason it intersects vampire stakes
- Hooks: territorial warning, impossible witness, contested dead, environmental harm, occult bargain, or hunter theory that names the wrong monster
- Caution: no non-vampire PCs, no generic stat block, no universal cosmology, and no surprise identity without fair evidence
- Source claims: `claim-wod-w5-separate`, `claim-wod-h5-separate`, `claim-wod-legacy-lines`.

## Current V5 Clan Matrix

Clan is not sect. These are broad player-facing affordances, not full lore or mechanics.

| Clan | Broad dramatic affordance | Avoid |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Banu Haqim | judgment, law, blood, migration, duty, and contested belonging | reducing the clan to assassins or one religion/ethnicity |
| Brujah | passion, rebellion, theory versus action, and movements that may reproduce power | compulsory anger, youth, or political uniformity |
| Gangrel | adaptation, territory, animality, travel, and distance from court | “uncivilized” coding or automatic wilderness mastery |
| Hecata | death, family structures, obligation, commerce, and the uses of Oblivion | one uniform family, casual abuse themes, or complete Wraith knowledge |
| Lasombra | ambition, shadow, religious or institutional power, and ruthless adaptation | inherent evil, one faith, or mandatory Camarilla/Sabbat loyalty |
| Malkavian | perception, insight, fractured interpretation, and inherited pressure | mental illness as joke, prophecy machine, or loss of player agency |
| Ministry | temptation, liberation claims, faith, secrets, and testing imposed limits | exoticism, one culture, or assuming every character is a corrupter |
| Nosferatu | concealment, information, community, exclusion, and seeing hidden systems | appearance as moral shorthand or universal sewer dwelling |
| Ravnos | motion, risk, illusion, survival, and rebuilding scattered ties | racist legacy stereotypes, compulsory crime, or rootlessness |
| Salubri | rarity, healing or resistance themes, persecution stories, and disputed reputation | saintliness, guaranteed diablerie bait, or objective clan myth |
| Toreador | creation, attention, desire, patronage, and beauty's power and cost | shallow vanity, compulsory sexuality, or one standard of beauty |
| Tremere | blood craft, institutional rupture, apprenticeship, and knowledge control | every member as loyal academic or magic as consequence-free utility |
| Tzimisce | possession, transformation, hospitality, domain, and body-bound identity | unconsented body horror, ethnic coding, or mandatory Sabbat loyalty |
| Ventrue | leadership claims, stewardship, hierarchy, resources, and accountability | automatic competence, nobility, wealth, or Camarilla loyalty |

Caitiff offer lineage ambiguity or rejection without forming a fifteenth clan. Thin-blooded vampires occupy a liminal V5 condition with supported character options; they are not a clan.

Source claims: `claim-wod-vtm-clans-current`, `claim-wod-vtm-thin-bloods`.

## Opening Faction Budget

For `wod-vtm-frame-borrowed-night`, instantiate only:

1. one local authority: Camarilla office, Anarch coalition, or negotiated independent patron
2. one rival or affected Kindred network: another coterie, thin-blood network, or domain claimant
3. one mortal community or institution with its own goal

A hunter analyst may begin as a clock rather than a face. Sabbat and other supernatural forces remain absent unless the selected frame requires them.

## Faction Clock Contract

Every active power records:

- desired end state
- next action if ignored
- local face and decision authority
- capability available tonight
- hard limit
- internal disagreement
- evidence or rumor it relies on
- information it lacks
- mortal people who pay the cost
- condition that changes its plan

“Clan X,” “the Camarilla,” “the hunters,” or “the werewolves” never advances a clock without a local actor and route of action.
