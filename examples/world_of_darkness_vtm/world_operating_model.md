# World of Darkness: Vampire: The Masquerade World Operating Model

## Use Contract

These are durable causal models for a future V5 chronicle, not active state and not a replacement for owned rules. Materialize only the truths that affect the selected opening. Every city, domain, feeding practice, court, coterie, hunter response, and supernatural presence must be instantiated locally.

## Core Causal Chain

1. Vampires survive by taking blood while attempting to preserve secrecy, agency, and chosen human attachments.
2. Hunger makes power and survival immediate; the Beast turns pressure into risk without erasing responsibility for setup and consequence.
3. Feeding creates witnesses, dependencies, patterns, harmed people, and contested access to places and populations.
4. The Masquerade converts those traces into collective political danger, so vampire authorities regulate behavior while protecting their own power.
5. Domain, boons, status, lineage, information, and control of safe night infrastructure become leverage.
6. Coteries form because no vampire can safely manage every haven, hunt, mortal tie, rival, and threat alone.
7. Camarilla, Anarch, independent, thin-blood, and hostile sect positions compete locally rather than acting as perfect global blocs.
8. Contemporary cameras, accounts, devices, institutions, and human memory can preserve evidence, but every data path has a holder and limit.
9. Mortal hunters range from isolated people to cells and concealed institutions; their uncertainty can make them both dangerous and fallible.
10. Ancient myth, elder absence, Gehenna rumors, and other supernatural beings widen the world without providing a single reliable cosmology.
11. A local choice about one victim, haven, favor, recording, or feeding route can alter coterie trust and propagate into city politics.
12. The chronicle remains about vampire player characters even when other World of Darkness forces shape the consequences.

## Durable Truth Cards

### `wod-vtm-truth-001` — Survival Is Predation

- Status: template-fixed
- Truth: vampires require blood, so survival places another person's body, consent, safety, or resources at stake.
- Table consequence: feeding method, access, cleanup, restraint, and aftermath are story decisions rather than background refueling.
- Must not imply: every feeding is sexual, every mortal is helpless, or harm becomes irrelevant when a roll succeeds.
- Open question: who bears the risk and what boundary does the coterie refuse to cross?
- Source claims: `claim-wod-vtm-vampire-condition`, `claim-wod-vtm-personal-political-horror`.

### `wod-vtm-truth-002` — Hunger Intrudes

- Status: template-fixed; exact procedure belongs to owned rules
- Truth: Hunger and the Beast can distort otherwise competent action, create temptation, and threaten loss of control.
- Table consequence: power has pressure; preparation, feeding, and restraint matter before a crisis.
- Must not imply: the GM may arbitrarily dictate a PC's feelings or use frenzy to cross player boundaries.
- Open question: what visible signs precede danger and who can intervene safely?
- Source claims: `claim-wod-vtm-hunger-humanity`, `claim-wod-vtm-vampire-condition`.

### `wod-vtm-truth-003` — Humanity Is Relational

- Status: template-fixed as theme; character content is player-authored
- Truth: Humanity, Convictions, and Touchstones connect monstrous survival to chosen principles and mortal relationships.
- Table consequence: moral compromise affects people and commitments the players helped define.
- Must not imply: one universal morality, compulsory affection, or permission to endanger a Touchstone without consent and setup.
- Open question: what principle does this vampire choose, and what relationship makes it concrete?
- Source claims: `claim-wod-vtm-hunger-humanity`, `claim-wod-vtm-touchstones-convictions`.

### `wod-vtm-truth-004` — The Masquerade Is Collective Risk

- Status: template-fixed
- Truth: vampire society depends on preventing convincing public proof of its existence.
- Table consequence: a witness, clip, body, transaction, or repeated pattern can matter more than a defeated enemy.
- Must not imply: mortals are oblivious, one memory edit solves every trace, or every authority shares the same enforcement policy.
- Open question: who has the evidence, who can interpret it, and who benefits from calling it a breach?
- Source claims: `claim-wod-vtm-kindred-masquerade`, `claim-wod-vtm-digital-risk`.

### `wod-vtm-truth-005` — Territory Organizes Need

- Status: template-fixed broadly; borders and rights are campaign-instance
- Truth: feeding access, havens, Elysium-like neutrality, routes, and mortal institutions make urban space political.
- Table consequence: crossing a street, hunting at a venue, or sheltering someone may create debt or trespass.
- Must not imply: a Prince or Baron owns mortal people, every city uses identical offices, or domain is legitimate merely because vampires recognize it.
- Open question: what is actually controlled, by whom, and through which mortal or Kindred relationship?
- Source claims: `claim-wod-vtm-domain-boons`, `claim-wod-vtm-camarilla`, `claim-wod-vtm-anarch`.

### `wod-vtm-truth-006` — Favors Preserve Memory

- Status: template-fixed broadly
- Truth: boons, status, reputation, and lineage let vampire society remember obligations beyond mortal legal systems.
- Table consequence: accepting help creates future action; denying a debt tests the institutions and witnesses that sustain it.
- Must not imply: every boon is enforceable, every elder claim is true, or social leverage removes player choice.
- Open question: who witnessed the obligation and what remedy can they actually impose?
- Source claims: `claim-wod-vtm-domain-boons`, `claim-wod-vtm-camarilla`.

### `wod-vtm-truth-007` — Blood Can Create Dependency

- Status: template-fixed broadly; exact mechanics require owned rules
- Truth: vitae may empower, bind, sustain, or exploit mortals and vampires through ghouling, Blood Bonds, and related practices.
- Table consequence: sharing blood is never a consequence-free healing potion; the relationship and consent matter.
- Must not imply: every attachment is a Blood Bond, bound people have no remaining agency, or a PC can be secretly bound without player agreement.
- Open question: what was offered, what was understood, and who controls continued access?
- Source claims: `claim-wod-vtm-blood-bonds-ghouls`, `claim-wod-vtm-vampire-condition`.

### `wod-vtm-truth-008` — A Coterie Is Mutual Exposure

- Status: template-fixed structure; mandate and ties are campaign-instance/player-authored
- Truth: vampires cooperate because shared domain, capability, witness, and danger can outweigh distrust.
- Table consequence: each PC contributes something others cannot safely replace and exposes the group to a distinct risk.
- Must not imply: compulsory loyalty, shared morality, or permission for unbounded PvP.
- Open question: what keeps the coterie together tonight, and what exit remains possible?
- Source claims: `claim-wod-vtm-coterie-play`, `claim-wod-vtm-personal-political-horror`.

### `wod-vtm-truth-009` — Sects Are Coalitions, Not Hive Minds

- Status: template-fixed
- Truth: Camarilla, Anarch, Sabbat, independent, clan, and thin-blood labels contain competing local interests.
- Table consequence: a title or affiliation predicts access and pressure, not every decision.
- Must not imply: Camarilla equals order, Anarch equals freedom, Sabbat equals random violence, or clan equals sect.
- Open question: what does this local member want that their institution does not fully provide?
- Source claims: `claim-wod-vtm-camarilla`, `claim-wod-vtm-anarch`, `claim-wod-vtm-sabbat-antagonists`, `claim-wod-vtm-clans-current`.

### `wod-vtm-truth-010` — Modern Evidence Accumulates

- Status: template-fixed as pressure; technology is campaign-instance
- Truth: networked contemporary life can preserve movement, identity, payment, communication, and violence beyond one witness.
- Table consequence: cleanup requires tracing custody and interpretation, not merely destroying the nearest camera.
- Must not imply: universal facial recognition, effortless hacking, or a perfectly integrated hunter database.
- Open question: which system recorded the event, who can access it, and what mundane explanation competes?
- Source claims: `claim-wod-vtm-digital-risk`, `claim-wod-vtm-second-inquisition`.

### `wod-vtm-truth-011` — Immortality Has Hard Limits

- Status: template-fixed broadly; exact damage and recovery require owned rules
- Truth: vampires may endure extraordinary injury and long spans, but daylight, fire, starvation, torpor, violence, and Final Death remain meaningful limits.
- Table consequence: haven security, time of night, recovery, and escape routes constrain action.
- Must not imply: invulnerability, consequence-free resurrection, or guaranteed survival for a named elder.
- Open question: what can the coterie accomplish before dawn and what must be abandoned?
- Source claims: `claim-wod-vtm-vampire-condition`, `claim-wod-vtm-hunger-humanity`.

### `wod-vtm-truth-012` — The Supernatural Exceeds Vampire Knowledge

- Status: template-fixed epistemic limit
- Truth: Garou, hunters, ghosts, mages, fae, demons, and other forces may exist, but vampires do not receive a complete or unified account of them.
- Table consequence: observation, negotiation, and retreat can matter more than taxonomy; a rumor does not become truth because it uses a familiar franchise term.
- Must not imply: all splat cosmologies are simultaneously literal, every creature is a combat encounter, or another line becomes playable.
- Open question: what was actually observed and which interpretation belongs to whom?
- Source claims: `claim-wod-w5-separate`, `claim-wod-h5-separate`, `claim-wod-legacy-lines`.

## The Vampiric Night

| Pressure | What it creates | What keeps it dramatic |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Blood need | hunting, negotiation, restraint, dependency, pattern | consent, scarcity, witnesses, health, territory, Hunger |
| Day sleep | limited operating window and vulnerable havens | dawn, travel time, intrusion, trusted retainers, fire |
| The Beast | urgency, temptation, frenzy risk | player boundaries, visible setup, accountability, recovery |
| Humanity | principles and relational stakes | player-authored Convictions, Touchstones, compromise, remorse |
| Masquerade | secrecy and collective enforcement | evidence custody, mortal interpretation, sect politics |
| Disciplines | asymmetric supernatural capability | Hunger, witnesses, counters, source-specific limits |
| Vitae | healing, power, ghouls, bonds, trade | dependency, coercion, scarcity, consent, politics |
| Immortality | memory, patience, grief, accumulated obligation | torpor, adaptation, lost ties, changing cities, Final Death |
| Coterie | shared capability and cover | incompatible needs, trust, mandate, negotiated authority |
| Domain | reliable access and standing | mortal agency, borders, rivals, stewardship, abuse of power |

## Feeding Model

For each active feeding practice, record:

- who or what population is approached
- why the vampire has access
- what the person understands and can refuse
- immediate physical and emotional risk
- trace left in bodies, memory, records, or routines
- local domain claim and who contests it
- what happens when the practice fails
- what line or veil governs portrayal

A predator type or successful roll does not answer these questions automatically.

## Mortal Life Must Remain Causal

Every district should include mortal institutions and communities with goals independent of vampire plots. At minimum define:

- one livelihood or night economy
- one care network
- one housing or displacement pressure
- one public authority with limited jurisdiction
- one informal community network
- one data or media custodian
- one place people can refuse vampire access
- one consequence that persists during daylight

Mortals are not collectively “the herd.” That phrase, when used, belongs to a speaker or institution and reveals its ethics.

## Current Clan Affordance

Clan changes inherited pressures, available powers, reputation, and possible lineage ties. It does not decide personality or politics. Use the clan matrix in [Factions](factions.md), then ask the player to define what they accept, reject, misunderstand, or have never been taught.

Caitiff and thin-blooded vampires require explicit local treatment. Thin-blood chronicles may use Players Guide support but still remain vampire-only.

Source claims: `claim-wod-vtm-clans-current`, `claim-wod-vtm-thin-bloods`.

## Other-Supernatural Boundary Matrix

| Presence | Story affordance | Hard boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| W5 Garou and spirits | territorial collision, environmental harm, dangerous negotiation, evidence that the city has non-vampire stakes | no Garou PC, no imported Gift/Rite mechanics, no claim that vampire lore explains spirits |
| H5 hunters | human investigation, desperate opposition, uneasy alignment against worse harm | no Hunter PC, no automatic moral purity, no universal hunter conspiracy |
| V5 Second Inquisition | covert institutional surveillance, raids, compromised data, state/church pressure | no omniscience, no unexplained equipment, no protagonist swap to hunter operations |
| Ghosts and Oblivion | memory, death, haunting, disputed testimony, occult cost | no full Wraith cosmology or ghost PC without a separate package |
| Mages and sorcerers | limits of vampiric certainty, occult bargains, reality that resists blood politics | no generic spell list, no Mage PC, no effortless solution to vampiric limits |
| Changelings and fae | uncanny bargains, dream or story intrusion, misread motives | no Changeling PC or assumed Dreaming cosmology |
| Demons, mummies, and others | rare campaign-specific revelation | identity and capability remain unset until sourced |

## Conflict Model

VTM conflict moves through six linked arenas:

1. **Hunger** — blood, restraint, frenzy risk, and who becomes vulnerable
2. **Humanity** — Convictions, Touchstones, chosen limits, and remorse
3. **Exposure** — witnesses, records, bodies, patterns, and the Masquerade
4. **Position** — domain, boons, status, clan expectation, and sect authority
5. **Relationship** — coterie trust, sire ties, ghouls, mortals, rivals, and dependence
6. **Survival** — haven, daylight, fire, injury, torpor, and Final Death

A consequence should usually move at least two arenas. Erasing a clip may reduce exposure while creating a boon; winning a feeding route may damage a mortal relationship; refusing an elder may preserve a Conviction while losing haven access.

## Default Pressure Cycle

For a bounded opening, define:

- one feeding pattern that can no longer continue safely
- one visible Masquerade anomaly
- one boon or status claim
- one mortal person or group acting for its own reasons
- one local vampire authority with incomplete information
- one rival interpretation that is partly true
- one daylight or three-night deadline
- one choice that cannot satisfy every obligation

Advance pressure through actor capacity and evidence, not arbitrary betrayal.

## Scale Ladder

| Scale | What PCs directly influence | What remains outside default control |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Personal | Hunger, one hunt, one tie, one secret, immediate violence | district policy and sect response |
| Coterie | shared haven, mandate, investigation, small feeding arrangement | citywide domain and institutional hunters |
| Domain | one district or network, several mortal institutions, local status | whole-city obedience and global metaplot |
| Court or movement | offices, coalitions, multiple domains, formal boons | total control of mortals, clans, or hunters |
| City | sect settlement, large breaches, strategic alliances | national systems and global supernatural truth |
| Gehenna War | elder projects, secret societies, mobile violent conflict | suitability for default play; requires a new contract |

Default opening: coterie scale with provisional influence over one district and no guaranteed domain ownership.

## Clock Contract

Every active clock records:

- actor and desired end state
- next action if unopposed
- required capability
- visible sign
- evidence created or destroyed
- mortal people who bear the cost
- information the actor lacks
- condition that changes the plan
- daylight interaction
- Masquerade effect

A clock does not advance because a faction is famous or because “the metaplot happens.”
