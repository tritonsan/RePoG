# World of Darkness: Vampire: The Masquerade Session 0 Options

## Player-Safe Use

This file contains no frame solutions. It proposes decisions but accepts none. Present one unresolved decision at a time, record answers only in campaign-owned files, and keep unselected options as reference.

Every offered campaign in this package has vampire player characters. Other World of Darkness beings may shape the setting but are not character options.

## Recommended Default Pitch

You are a mixed coterie of young or otherwise precarious vampires in an original contemporary city. A local authority gives you three nights of provisional access to one contested district. Overlapping feeding claims, an unpaid boon, and a copied recording threaten the Masquerade. You need one another, but the city does not own your loyalties—and you do not own the mortals who live there.

Default selections proposed, not accepted:

- Continuity: current World of Darkness / V5
- Era: contemporary nights, exact date unset
- Locale: original city, one mixed-use district
- Frame: `wod-vtm-frame-borrowed-night`
- Party: mixed young or precarious vampire coterie
- Sect posture: locally negotiated, not mandatory allegiance
- Scale: coterie with provisional district influence
- Famous characters: absent
- Other supernatural: rumor or distant pressure only at opening
- Rules: owned V5 rules
- Feeding portrayal: selected explicitly at the table

## Decision Order

1. Vampire-only scope confirmation
2. Continuity and edition
3. Era, metaplot intensity, and spoiler ceiling
4. City model and opening district
5. Campaign fantasy and coterie mandate
6. Sect relationship and local authority
7. Clan, Caitiff, or thin-blood options
8. Tone, feeding portrayal, and safety boundaries
9. Player-authored vampires, Touchstones, and coterie ties
10. Rules details and knowledge privacy
11. Opening frame and materialization consent

Do not request a complete encyclopedia of answers at once.

## Playable-Scope Confirmation

Ask first:

> This package supports a coterie in which every player character is a Vampire: The Masquerade vampire. Werewolves, mortal hunters, mages, ghosts, changelings, demons, ghouls, and other supernatural beings may appear as non-player parts of the world. Is that the campaign you want?

If the answer is no, stop materialization and design or research a different package. Do not quietly add a crossover PC.

## Continuity Choice

Choose one:

- `wod5-v5-current`: current V5 with only selected current supplements
- `v5-named-city`: a named published city after targeted research
- `v5-plus-selected-legacy`: current V5 plus individually listed legacy imports
- `v20-legacy`: V20 or another named legacy edition as primary; requires a new operating-model pass
- `adaptation-specific`: name the game, series, or other licensed continuity
- `campaign-divergent`: state the exact changed premise

*Chronicles of Darkness* and *Vampire: The Requiem* are not continuity options inside this package.

## Fifteen Vampire Player Fantasies

| Fantasy | What players do | Best scale | Onboarding | Main caution |
| --- | --- | --- | ---: | --- |
| 1. Fledgling coterie | learn the night while surviving sires, Hunger, and local law | personal → coterie | 5 | mentors must not remove agency |
| 2. Camarilla court retainers | investigate, negotiate, and carry dangerous authority | coterie → court | 4 | hierarchy is not moral legitimacy |
| 3. Anarch neighborhood defenders | protect local autonomy and confront reproduced power | coterie → domain | 4 | protection must not become ownership |
| 4. Provisional domain holders | build feeding agreements, havens, and standing in one district | coterie → domain | 5 | mortals are not domain resources |
| 5. Hounds or investigators | trace breaches, disappearances, and manipulated evidence | coterie | 4 | authority, coercion, and policing need limits |
| 6. Thin-blood survival network | secure shelter, blood, alchemy, recognition, and mutual aid | personal → coterie | 4 | use current owned rules; avoid lesser-person framing |
| 7. Blood-craft researchers | navigate rituals, formulae, teachers, markets, and bodily cost | coterie | 3 | body horror and proprietary knowledge require consent |
| 8. Nightlife or criminal operators | manage venues, movement, cash, influence, and feeding patterns | domain | 4 | workers and patrons need agency; no glamorized coercion |
| 9. Masquerade cleanup cell | contain evidence while deciding whom secrecy protects | coterie | 5 | cleanup is not permission to silence every witness |
| 10. Second Inquisition fugitives | move havens, protect ties, and decode hunter patterns | personal → city | 4 | surveillance and state violence can be intense |
| 11. Boon or elder-legacy inheritors | decide which old obligations survive absent elders | court → city | 3 | metaplot and power imbalance require review |
| 12. Independent or Autarkis coterie | broker access and preserve autonomy among stronger blocs | coterie → domain | 4 | neutrality has material costs |
| 13. Protectors of mortal ties | keep communities and Touchstones alive without controlling them | personal → domain | 4 | avoid saviorism and hostage plots |
| 14. Clan-lineage mission | confront inherited reputation, teaching, and contested history | coterie | 3 | clan never dictates personality, ethnicity, or sect |
| 15. Gehenna War strike team | face elders, secret societies, mobile violence, and temptation | city → mobile | 2 | high lethality, diablerie, and metaplot complexity |

Recommended first campaign: combine fantasies 1, 4, 9, and 13, with optional elements of 8 or 12.

## Coterie Mandate Options

Choose one shared reason to remain together:

- provisional district stewards
- Camarilla investigation or Hound team
- Anarch neighborhood coalition
- all-thin-blood survival network
- mixed coterie sponsored by one reluctant patron
- Masquerade repair and evidence team
- blood-craft market custodians
- nightlife venue or transport operators
- refugees from a burned haven
- independent brokers preserving one neutral route
- inheritors of one disputed boon archive
- custom vampire-only mandate

A mandate gives cooperation a practical basis. It does not assign affection, sect belief, shared morality, or permanent membership.

## City Model Choice

Choose one:

- original city inspired by several places but not mapped one-to-one
- fictionalized version of a real city with explicit changes
- real city with cultural and institutional research
- named published VTM city with source-specific canon review

For an original city, decide collaboratively:

- country or broad legal-cultural context
- climate, season, and length of night
- population pattern and inequality
- primary industries and night economy
- transit and geographic barriers
- housing and displacement pressure
- surveillance and data environment
- one public conflict unrelated to vampires
- one reason the city attracts or repels Kindred
- one reason no sect fully controls it

Do not invent “generic modern America” by omission. If the city remains unnamed temporarily, keep it explicitly unset rather than using a placeholder as fact.

## Opening District Choice

- contested mixed-use district: best default balance
- nightlife corridor: social play and feeding pressure
- civic core: data, institutions, and hunter risk
- old quarter: court, boons, and elder memory
- residential neighborhood: community, Touchstones, and Anarch authority
- medical/university belt: blood logistics and occult research
- waterfront/transit: flight, manifests, and mobile evidence
- cemetery/undercity: occult mystery after source review
- suburban/ecological fringe: isolation and W5-adjacent uncertainty
- digital/media network: connection layer paired with a physical district

Select one physical opening district and at most one connection layer.

## Sect Relationship Choice

- recognized Camarilla members or retainers
- tolerated Camarilla outsiders
- Anarch members under a named local decision process
- independent or Autarkis
- thin-blood network negotiating with full-blooded vampires
- mixed coterie under a temporary compact
- disputed allegiance
- custom local polity

Sabbat player characters are not a default option. Selecting them requires an explicit divergence, owned-source review, and renewed safety design.

Ask:

- Who may give the coterie an order?
- What can the coterie refuse?
- Who recognizes its feeding access?
- What happens if the compact fails?
- Which mortal people are affected but not represented in the agreement?

Offer the current V5 menu only through rules the group owns:

- Banu Haqim
- Brujah
- Gangrel
- Hecata
- Lasombra
- Malkavian
- Ministry
- Nosferatu
- Ravnos
- Salubri
- Toreador
- Tremere
- Tzimisce
- Ventrue
- Caitiff
- thin-blooded vampire

Then ask:

- What does your vampire know about their lineage, and what remains unknown?
- Which stereotype do they reject or exploit?
- Is a sire present, absent, dead, estranged, or deliberately undefined?
- Does clan create a relationship the player wants in play?
- Which exact Bane, Compulsion, Disciplines, and creation options come from the selected owned rules?

Never assign clan from personality, identity, mental health, ethnicity, profession, appearance, or politics.

## Vampire Age And Power

Choose a shared range or explicit mix:

- newly Embraced fledglings
- young vampires with one established tie
- neonates with local standing
- mixed young coterie with negotiated power differences
- ancilla-level or older characters after a power and history review
- elder-scale characters only under a separate campaign contract

Decide how age differences affect authority, knowledge, Blood Potency, generation, and spotlight under the selected rules. Age never grants one player control over another.

## Feeding Contract

For each PC, the player may define or defer:

- preferred feeding context
- what the victim or donor understands
- whether feeding is negotiated, deceptive, coercive, violent, clinical, or off-screen
- forbidden targets or methods
- whether killing through feeding is possible in this campaign
- how Hunger emergencies are handled
- who may intervene in another PC's hunt
- what traces remain
- how prey exclusion or equivalent rules are portrayed
- whether animal, bagged, consensual, or other sources are available under current rules

A mechanical predator choice does not replace informed discussion.

## Tone Dials

Rate each from 0 (absent or background) to 3 (central and explicit):

- personal horror
- political horror
- feeding detail
- Hunger and loss of control
- Humanity and remorse
- court intrigue and boons
- neighborhood/community politics
- romance and sexuality
- blood bonds and ghouls
- body horror and transformation
- occult ritual and blood craft
- surveillance and hunter pressure
- physical violence and Final Death
- elder/metaplot proximity
- other-supernatural mystery
- PvP and betrayal

High setting intensity never waives a player boundary.

## Safety Topics

Discuss lines, veils, opt-ins, and stop tools for:

- blood, wounds, medical procedures, and body fluids
- feeding, intoxication, addiction metaphor, and withdrawal
- sexualized feeding, sexual content, and romance
- coercion, supernatural command, memory alteration, and compromised consent
- Blood Bonds, ghouling, abusive sires, cults, and dependency
- frenzy, loss of control, and harm caused while impaired
- stalking, surveillance, doxxing, raids, interrogation, and state violence
- housing insecurity, poverty, exploitation, and organized crime
- mental illness, disability, disfigurement, and appearance
- religion, blasphemy, ancient myth, and cultural appropriation
- pregnancy, reproductive coercion, family harm, and children
- torture, captivity, diablerie, torpor, fire, and Final Death
- harm to Touchstones, animals, or community members
- PvP, secret allegiance, betrayal, and forced coterie membership

No hidden PC Blood Bond, sire abuse, diablerie, pregnancy, romance, sexual history, mental-health diagnosis, betrayal, or secret supernatural ancestry may be introduced without that player's explicit agreement.

## Player-Authored Vampire Prompts

Each player may answer, decline, or defer:

- What is your name, identity, appearance, and presentation?
- When and where were you Embraced, if you want that fixed now?
- What did you lose, keep, or deliberately leave from mortal life?
- What clan or non-clan condition do you choose?
- What do you know, believe, or reject about your sire?
- Which Convictions are yours, and how do they appear in action?
- Which Touchstones do you want in play, and what protection or consent surrounds them?
- What is your feeding practice and its cost?
- What Ambition or Desire do you want the chronicle to engage?
- What does Hunger look like before control becomes dangerous?
- What haven access or practical resource do you contribute?
- Which sect, clan, or local authority may court you but not own you?
- What tie do you want with another PC?
- What secret do you want in play, and who may know it?
- What part of your history remains private even from other players?

Unanswered prompts remain unknown, not invitations for GM invention.

## Coterie Design Questions

- What practical need keeps the coterie together tonight?
- What shared resource exists: haven, domain access, vehicle, venue, archive, route, or patron?
- Who outside the coterie is affected by that resource?
- What authority can the coterie exercise without asking?
- What decision requires unanimity?
- What behavior can trigger removal from the group?
- What exit remains possible for a PC?
- How are feeding conflicts resolved?
- How are private scenes and secret notes handled?
- What level of intra-coterie coercion or violence is forbidden?

## Other-Supernatural Choice

Choose one opening posture:

- absent and unconfirmed
- rumor only
- one unexplained sign
- one non-player presence after targeted research
- known local boundary the coterie avoids
- central mystery after a separate source and safety pass

Do not reveal the creature's game-line label as common knowledge. Do not offer it as a player character.

## Rules And Resolution Choice

Default: owned V5 rules and current errata.

Then decide:

- which books are in scope
- whether Players Guide clan and coterie options are available
- exact Hunger and frenzy procedures
- Humanity, Convictions, Touchstones, Stains, and remorse treatment
- injury, torpor, healing, fire, sunlight, and Final Death severity
- coterie and domain mechanics
- Blood Bond, ghoul, and diablerie boundaries
- predator types and feeding abstractions
- whether Blood Sorcery, Thin-Blood Alchemy, or Oblivion need extra sources
- whether house rules change the fiction or only procedure

This package supplies no copied mechanics, stat blocks, or character options.

## Knowledge And Privacy Choice

Decide separately:

- what all players know about VTM
- what all PCs know as local Kindred
- which clan or sect facts require a holder
- whether false beliefs are welcome
- how Noddist claims are labeled
- how hunter evidence and confidence are shown
- whether another supernatural being's identity can remain unknown after encounter
- which PC secrets are player-private
- whether private information may create dramatic irony
- how a player can revise or withdraw a private hook

Use [Knowledge Layers](knowledge_layers.md) after acceptance.
