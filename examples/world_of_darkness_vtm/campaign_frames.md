# World of Darkness: Vampire: The Masquerade Campaign Frames

## Use Contract

Every frame below is RePoG-original campaign design, not World of Darkness canon and not a summary of a published chronicle. A frame remains selectable reference content until Session 0 accepts it. Every PC is a VTM vampire. Player-facing sections may be presented without the GM-only hidden truth.

Famous characters, canonical Princes, and other supernatural protagonists are neither patrons nor solutions by default.

## `wod-vtm-frame-borrowed-night` — Borrowed Night

- Status: package-original proposal
- Compatible continuity: `wod5-v5-current`
- Opening theater: `wod-vtm-region-contested-district`
- Party mandate: mixed young or otherwise precarious coterie given provisional access to one district
- Opening scale: one shared haven, two feeding venues, one transit node, one community institution, and one contested meeting place

### Player-Facing Promise

A local vampire authority grants your coterie three nights to stabilize a district whose previous steward vanished. Two coteries hold valid-looking permission to feed at the same venues, a worker was badly injured during an impossible altercation, and a copied security clip is moving through accounts nobody controls. Settle the claim and contain the breach before the authority chooses a scapegoat—or before daylight investigators connect the pattern themselves.

### Visible Situation

- Two independently witnessed grants assign overlapping feeding access.
- A venue worker remembers a person rising after an injury that should have ended the fight.
- The original security drive is gone, but at least two copies exist.
- A thin-blood courier is blamed without evidence and has stopped answering messages.
- The authority expects a clean account before the next court or neighborhood assembly.
- Dawn, weekend crowds, and an automated backup cycle impose separate deadlines.

### Actor Configuration

- Provisional patron: needs the district quiet and the grant accepted
- Rival coterie: believes its permission is older and socially stronger
- Missing domain steward: left records that do not agree
- Venue worker network: wants safety and accountability, not vampire politics
- Thin-blood courier or unaffiliated broker: moved one copy for reasons of their own
- Municipal or contractor analyst: sees anomalies but does not yet know their cause

Each actor receives a name, identity, and local relationship only during materialization.

### Causal Chain

1. The former steward sold or promised the same feeding access twice to satisfy incompatible boons.
2. Members of the two beneficiary groups hunted in the same venue and confronted one another.
3. A mortal worker was injured while intervening and witnessed an unmistakably abnormal recovery.
4. Another worker copied the footage to document unsafe management, not to hunt vampires.
5. A thin-blood courier removed the original drive to protect someone visible in the recording, but did not create the copies.
6. A routine municipal or vendor system will correlate the clip with earlier anomalies if the pattern continues.
7. The local authority knows the grants conflict and prefers one disposable culprit to an admission that its boon system failed.

### Neutral Action Space

The coterie may verify each grant, renegotiate feeding, protect or compensate workers, recover copies, expose the steward, preserve evidence against the authority, ally with the rival coterie, relocate the hunt, or let the institution lose legitimacy. No faction owns the correct answer. Protecting the Masquerade does not require silencing every mortal, and respecting mortals does not require public supernatural disclosure.

### Inaction Trajectory

- First deadline: the clip reaches a larger local audience.
- Second deadline: the patron declares the thin-blood courier responsible.
- Third deadline: the rival coterie retaliates at a feeding venue.
- End state: hunter attention rises, workers bear the immediate harm, and the coterie receives the district only as an enforcement debt.

### PC Integration Prompts

- What capability made the patron include you: negotiation, investigation, occult knowledge, influence, protection, or access?
- What feeding boundary will you not violate merely to keep the grant?
- Which mortal institution in the district can recognize you?
- What does another coterie member know about your first dangerous Hunger?

These prompts do not assign answers or relationships.

### GM-Only Hidden Truth

Hidden-truth id: `wod-vtm-frame-borrowed-night-secret-001`

There is no single breach conspiracy. The missing steward created the overlapping grants to service two boons, the workers copied footage to document violence, and the thin-blood courier stole only the original to protect a visible friend. The analyst has not identified vampires yet. The patron's proposed scapegoat would conceal institutional failure while leaving the copied evidence and unsafe feeding pattern intact.

### Reveal Requirements

Establish at least three independent chains:

1. boon witness → grant date → steward's contradictory promise
2. worker testimony → copy history → current holders
3. original-drive custody → courier's protected person → analyst's actual confidence

A confession, hacked account, or supernatural compulsion alone is insufficient.

### Safe Foreshadowing

- The two grants use different witnesses but the same obsolete phrase.
- Copy timestamps precede the courier's access to the drive.
- The workers discuss safety and management, not monsters.
- The analyst's search terms describe anomalies rather than vampires.

### Forbidden Early Wording

Do not call the courier the breacher, the workers hunters, the analyst Second Inquisition, or the rival grant forged before the evidence supports it.

### Boundaries And Review

Discuss feeding, workplace violence, surveillance, memory alteration, retaliation, scapegoating, and possible harm to a mortal witness. Decide whether feeding is abstract, veiled, or shown.

### Expansion

Expand to the civic core or city court only if the coterie preserves evidence, refuses the patron's settlement, or allows the data correlation to advance. Do not introduce a famous vampire to adjudicate the claim.

## `wod-vtm-frame-gilded-office` — The Gilded Office

- Status: package-original proposal
- Compatible continuity: `wod5-v5-current`
- Opening theaters: `wod-vtm-region-old-quarter` and one court precinct
- Party mandate: Camarilla-aligned retainers, Hounds, investigators, or provisional officeholders
- Opening scale: one vacant office, one boon archive, one Elysium-like gathering, and one contested haven

### Player-Facing Promise

The Keeper of a respected neutral gathering place disappears before announcing a successor. Three sealed copies of the office's boon register disagree, and each version gives a different claimant the power to appoint one of you. The court demands continuity before the next gathering. Determine which obligations are real without letting rivals, hunters, or your own patron decide that truth is merely the version they can enforce.

### Visible Situation

- The Keeper's haven is intact but deliberately emptied of personal effects.
- Three credible witnesses authenticate three incompatible registers.
- A Harpy-like status broker insists that public confidence matters more than archival precision.
- One claimant offers the coterie permanent domain for immediate recognition.
- A mortal conservator has catalogued material no vampire admits exists.
- An unusual access log suggests someone entered during daylight.

### Actor Configuration

- Court authority seeking an orderly appointment
- Status broker who controls public interpretation
- Three claimants with different valid obligations
- Mortal conservator or facilities worker with physical custody
- Missing Keeper's ghoul or former retainer with divided loyalties
- Covert hunter observer using a mundane contractor relationship

### Causal Chain

1. The Keeper maintained separate registers for public obligations, coercive debts, and favors they considered illegitimate.
2. The status broker merged selected entries to make a preferred succession appear inevitable.
3. The Keeper discovered a daylight contractor copying access patterns and chose to disappear rather than expose every debtor.
4. A retainer emptied the haven and placed contradictory registers with different witnesses as a brake on any single claimant.
5. The covert observer knows only traffic patterns, not the content of the boons.
6. The court's demand for rapid continuity will reveal the network more efficiently than the original theft.

### Neutral Action Space

The coterie may separate registers by purpose, find the Keeper, protect the conservator, appoint a temporary Keeper, expose the broker, trade recognition, retire coercive debts, or let the office fail. Preserving the Camarilla does not require accepting every claimed obligation.

### Inaction Trajectory

The preferred claimant takes the office, coerced debts become public obligations, the mortal conservator is blamed for the breach, and the observer maps the next gathering.

### PC Integration Prompts

- Which court obligation gives you standing but not obedience?
- What kind of boon would your vampire refuse to collect?
- Who taught you the difference between status and trust?
- What would make you walk away from the offered domain?

### GM-Only Hidden Truth

Hidden-truth id: `wod-vtm-frame-gilded-office-secret-001`

The Keeper is not dead or Beckoned. They staged a disappearance after detecting daylight surveillance and split the archive to prevent one raid from exposing the court. The status broker independently altered a compilation to control succession. The mortal conservator preserved evidence of both but does not understand the supernatural stakes.

### Reveal Requirements

Require physical document comparison, access-path reconstruction, and contact with either the Keeper or the retainer. Supernatural truth detection cannot determine what category the Keeper intended an obligation to occupy.

### Safe Foreshadowing

- Each register is internally consistent but optimized for a different audience.
- Personal effects were packed, not destroyed.
- The daylight access follows maintenance routes rather than a forced entry.
- The broker quotes obligations absent from the oldest physical pages.

### Forbidden Early Wording

Do not declare the Keeper dead, Beckoned, kidnapped, or betrayed by the conservator before discovery.

### Boundaries And Review

Discuss coercive debt, institutional abuse, surveillance, danger to retainers, memory alteration, and punishment by hierarchy.

### Expansion

Open citywide court politics only after the office's local records and holders are resolved.

## `wod-vtm-frame-last-block` — The Last Block Awake

- Status: package-original proposal
- Compatible continuity: `wod5-v5-current`
- Opening theater: `wod-vtm-region-residential`
- Party mandate: Anarch-aligned or independent vampires who claim responsibility for one neighborhood
- Opening scale: one residential block, one community space, one feeding route, and one redevelopment office

### Player-Facing Promise

A developer backed by a Camarilla-connected fund begins clearing the last affordable block inside an Anarch-held district. At the same time, residents organize against a pattern of night assaults that includes one of your coterie's feeding mistakes. The local Baron demands that you protect the block without revealing Kindred involvement. Decide whether protection means control, alliance, restitution, or leaving the people you claim to defend free to oppose you.

### Visible Situation

- Eviction notices arrive before required public review.
- Residents have mapped night incidents more accurately than local vampires.
- The Baron offers force but wants a permanent feeding tithe afterward.
- A Camarilla intermediary denies ownership while carrying the fund's authority.
- One assault description matches a PC-created or player-approved complication only if that player selects it; otherwise it points to an NPC vampire.
- A tenant organizer has a complete copy of the pattern map.

### Actor Configuration

- Tenant or neighborhood coalition with its own strategy
- Local Baron or council seeking legitimacy
- Camarilla-linked financial intermediary
- Predatory vampire using displacement as cover
- Mortal planner, journalist, or lawyer tracing the fund
- Coterie member's chosen mortal tie, if the player opts in

### Causal Chain

1. The fund uses redevelopment to empty properties needed for a protected Camarilla route and haven network.
2. A local vampire, unaffiliated with the fund, feeds among displaced residents because missing people draw less attention.
3. The Baron knows about the predator but delays action to make the Camarilla project look solely responsible.
4. Residents connect assaults, evictions, and transit changes through ordinary organizing.
5. The Camarilla intermediary expects Anarch violence and has prepared a legal and media narrative around it.
6. Any supernatural battle will accelerate both displacement and hunter interest.

### Neutral Action Space

The coterie may stop the predator, share selected evidence, support the mortal campaign without owning it, expose the fund, negotiate the route, reject the tithe, undermine the Baron, move their own feeding, or accept losing territory. The residents choose whether to cooperate.

### Inaction Trajectory

The predator kills, the Baron retaliates publicly, the fund obtains emergency control, and residents are displaced under a narrative of gang violence.

### PC Integration Prompts

- Why do you stay in this neighborhood when another domain would be safer?
- What support can you offer without demanding blood or obedience?
- Which Anarch promise do you believe, and which have you seen broken?
- What restitution would your vampire accept if they caused harm here?

### GM-Only Hidden Truth

Hidden-truth id: `wod-vtm-frame-last-block-secret-001`

Three actors exploit the same crisis without coordinating: the Camarilla-linked fund wants a secure route, the local predator uses displacement for feeding, and the Baron withholds intervention to strengthen an anti-Camarilla narrative. The residents' map is the most accurate account because it records harms none of the vampire factions considered important.

### Reveal Requirements

Require ownership records, incident comparison, and evidence of the Baron's prior knowledge. Do not reveal a mortal organizer's private sources without their choice or a credible custody path.

### Safe Foreshadowing

- Assaults continue in buildings the fund does not own.
- The Baron knows details absent from public reports.
- The secure route avoids the most profitable redevelopment parcels.
- Resident maps use care visits and shift schedules vampires ignored.

### Forbidden Early Wording

Do not call the mortal coalition a Camarilla front, the Baron a liberator, or redevelopment the sole cause of predation.

### Boundaries And Review

Discuss housing insecurity, policing, predatory feeding, class violence, retaliation, and savior narratives. A PC's responsibility for an incident must be player-selected.

### Expansion

Expand only along the fund's actual ownership chain or the residents' chosen coalition; the neighborhood is not a stepping stone to owning the city.

## `wod-vtm-frame-sigil-market` — The Sigil Market

- Status: package-original proposal
- Compatible continuity: `wod5-v5-current` with owned blood-craft support
- Opening theater: `wod-vtm-region-medical`
- Party mandate: vampire coterie hired to recover a missing blood-craft broker and protect a compromised market
- Opening scale: one night clinic, one underground auction, one sample archive, and one campus service tunnel

### Player-Facing Promise

At an invitation-only exchange for Blood Sorcery and Thin-Blood Alchemy, every lot is withdrawn when the broker discovers that the sample labels contain the names of buyers who never donated. By dawn the broker is missing, one batch has harmed its user, and a hospital inventory audit is scheduled. Find the custody breach before a court, a thin-blood network, or a hunter concludes that your coterie built it.

### Visible Situation

- The labels are genuine products of the market's coding system.
- One formula or ritual component is contaminated; its exact effect comes from owned rules or remains fictional pressure.
- A thin-blood seller demands access to the archive that excluded them.
- A Tremere, Banu Haqim, Hecata, or other blood-craft buyer may be selected, but no clan is mandatory.
- Hospital stock and research samples show small discrepancies over months.
- A mundane compliance officer will begin the audit after sunrise.

### Actor Configuration

- Missing broker who curated the exchange
- Thin-blood alchemist or network excluded from governance
- Established blood sorcerer protecting reputation
- Hospital or laboratory worker tracing inventory
- Rival apprentice who altered one batch
- Hidden buyer seeking names rather than blood craft

### Causal Chain

1. The broker discovers that a hospital contractor has diverted samples into the market.
2. The same contractor sells access metadata to a buyer who may be a hunter cutout but does not understand the full market.
3. The broker relabels the archive with buyer names as a dead-drop warning and disappears voluntarily.
4. An apprentice independently contaminates one rival's batch to expose exploitative practice.
5. Established buyers assume the thin-blood sellers caused both events and prepare retaliation.
6. The hospital audit can reveal the diversion, but a supernatural cleanup could destroy evidence needed by affected patients and workers.

### Neutral Action Space

The coterie may find the broker, separate sample theft from sabotage, protect the audit, close or reform the market, bargain with thin-blood sellers, expose the contractor, identify the hidden buyer, or destroy occult material while preserving mundane accountability.

### Inaction Trajectory

Established buyers attack the thin-blood network, the contractor burns records, the compliance officer is framed, and the hidden buyer gains a clean list of supernatural contacts.

### PC Integration Prompts

- What blood-craft practice, debt, or curiosity gives you standing here?
- What bodily material will you never trade?
- Who deserves access to knowledge your lineage calls proprietary?
- What mundane investigation are you unwilling to sabotage?

### GM-Only Hidden Truth

Hidden-truth id: `wod-vtm-frame-sigil-market-secret-001`

The labels, contamination, and broker's disappearance are three related but non-identical acts. The broker used names to flag compromised custody and went into hiding. The apprentice sabotaged one batch for a personal dispute. The contractor's metadata buyer is building a contact graph and has not yet proved anyone is a vampire.

### Reveal Requirements

Require sample-chain comparison, ingredient or batch analysis under selected rules, and the contractor's data trail. Occult success cannot replace mundane custody evidence.

### Safe Foreshadowing

- Label ink dates from after the samples entered storage.
- Only one batch shares the contaminant.
- The broker removed personal tools but left expensive inventory.
- The hidden buyer asks about connections, not formulae.

### Forbidden Early Wording

Do not call the event a thin-blood attack, ancient curse, Tremere plot, or Second Inquisition operation before evidence.

### Boundaries And Review

Discuss blood and tissue handling, medical harm, experimentation, coercive donation, body horror, addiction metaphor, and institutional retaliation.

### Expansion

A ghost, spirit, or other supernatural explanation may not be added as a twist without a targeted source pass and fair prior evidence.

## `wod-vtm-frame-daylight-list` — The Daylight List

- Status: package-original proposal
- Compatible continuity: `wod5-v5-current`
- Opening theaters: `wod-vtm-region-waterfront`, `wod-vtm-region-civic-core`, and one burned haven
- Party mandate: vampire coterie whose identities or mortal ties appear on a hunter data set
- Opening scale: one night of movement, three safe routes, one seized device, and one dawn deadline

### Player-Facing Promise

After a haven burns, your coterie recovers part of a target list from a departing daylight team. It contains several false identities, one accurate route, and the name of a mortal connection who should be invisible to vampire society. Before dawn, learn what the list actually predicts, move whoever chooses to leave, and decide whether destroying it protects you or blinds you to the next raid.

### Visible Situation

- The list mixes people, addresses, utility anomalies, and unexplained confidence scores.
- One coterie identity is absent even though the haven was shared.
- A local hunter claims to have altered the list and asks for extraction.
- A transit or port checkpoint will change shift before dawn.
- The named mortal connection has their own plan and cannot be moved as cargo.
- A Camarilla or Anarch authority offers shelter in exchange for the device.

### Actor Configuration

- Institutional hunter cell with a narrow raid mandate
- Local independent hunter who supplied or changed data
- Mortal connection named on the list
- Sect authority seeking intelligence and control
- Transport worker or route broker
- Survivor from the burned haven who may know why it was flagged

### Causal Chain

1. Repeated utility and movement anomalies around several havens generated an investigative cluster.
2. A local hunter added names from a missing-person case, believing the institution would rescue victims.
3. After learning the team intended lethal action, the hunter altered confidence values to divert the first raid from a shelter.
4. The alteration exposed a different haven and contributed to the fire.
5. The named mortal connection appears because of ordinary care visits, not supernatural knowledge.
6. The institutional cell possesses a model with dangerous patterns but several false causal assumptions.

### Neutral Action Space

The coterie may evacuate, misdirect, expose the raid, negotiate with the hunter, warn rivals, preserve the model, destroy selected data, change their own behavior, rescue survivors, or accept sect shelter. The mortal connection chooses their own response.

### Inaction Trajectory

The next shift validates the cluster, the shelter becomes a secondary target, sect authorities seize the mortal connection “for protection,” and the coterie loses every route associated with the list.

### PC Integration Prompts

- Which mortal routine may have exposed you?
- What would you abandon to avoid using a person as leverage?
- Which coterie member is trusted to decide when a haven is lost?
- What do you owe the people endangered by your survival pattern?

### GM-Only Hidden Truth

Hidden-truth id: `wod-vtm-frame-daylight-list-secret-001`

The list is neither omniscient nor random. It began as pattern analysis of real anomalies, then a local hunter manually added missing-person contacts and later changed confidence values to prevent one raid. The named mortal is evidence of routine association, not an informant. The coterie can learn from the model without accepting its theory or surrendering it to a sect.

### Reveal Requirements

Require version comparison, utility or movement-source verification, and testimony or records from the local hunter. A captured operative knows only the current raid package.

### Safe Foreshadowing

- Confidence language changes midway through the file.
- Some targets share utilities but no social link.
- The named mortal's visits align with care needs, not meetings.
- The burned haven was promoted in priority shortly before the raid.

### Forbidden Early Wording

Do not call the mortal an informant, the local hunter a trusted ally, or the list a complete Second Inquisition registry.

### Boundaries And Review

Discuss surveillance, raids, fire, displacement, danger to mortal ties, institutional violence, interrogation, and haven loss.

### Expansion

Follow one sponsoring institution only after the local cell, data custody, and player desire for a wider hunter chronicle are established.

## `wod-vtm-frame-empty-throne` — The Empty Throne Below

- Status: package-original high-metaplot proposal
- Compatible continuity: `wod5-v5-current` with `gehenna-war-active`
- Opening theater: `wod-vtm-region-old-quarter` with one sealed underground haven
- Party mandate: vampire coterie inheriting contradictory boons and one sealed instruction from an absent elder
- Opening scale: one succession gathering, one sealed haven, three claimants, and one approaching hostile team

### Player-Facing Promise

An elder long assumed Beckoned sends a fresh instruction bearing a blood-authenticated seal: open the haven beneath their former domain and honor no claimant who asks first. Three factions insist the message proves opposite things, while a Gehenna War cell moves toward the city. Decide whether the elder is patron, prisoner, weapon, or myth before their old obligations become yours.

### Visible Situation

- The seal appears valid under current local practice; exact mechanics require owned rules.
- Three claimants possess different keys, memories, or blood ties to the haven.
- The elder's former ghoul line or retainers continue maintaining something below.
- A Sabbat or other Gehenna War cell seeks a person, not a relic.
- The court wants the coterie to open the haven under supervision.
- Sounds or signs below can be explained by machinery, retainers, or an occupant.

### Actor Configuration

- Court claimant seeking legitimate succession
- Anarch or independent claimant harmed by the elder's former rule
- Elder's childe or executor guarding the sealed instruction
- Retainer network sustaining the haven
- Gehenna War cell pursuing diablerie, recruitment, or destruction
- Mortal property holder whose building contains the access route

### Causal Chain

1. The elder never left the city; they entered voluntary torpor after learning that several factions intended to use or consume them.
2. Their executor spread a Beckoning account to make the search move elsewhere.
3. The elder prepared contradictory boons so no successor could open the haven alone.
4. A retainer recently woke or signaled the elder under a contingency whose meaning is disputed.
5. The Gehenna War cell learned that the elder remains local through a compromised childe.
6. The new instruction was sent by the executor using authority genuinely granted before torpor, not by the waking elder.
7. Opening, moving, waking, bargaining with, or destroying the elder each changes the city's balance without resolving the global Beckoning.

### Neutral Action Space

The coterie may keep the haven sealed, unite or deceive claimants, evacuate mortals, move the elder, wake them, negotiate with the executor, destroy the seal, confront the war cell, expose the false history, or refuse every inherited boon.

### Inaction Trajectory

The court forces entry, the war cell attacks during the opening, the mortal property becomes collateral, and one claimant defines the surviving account as legitimate history.

### PC Integration Prompts

- Which inherited obligation do you recognize, and why?
- What would make an elder's survival less important than the people above the haven?
- What temptation does ancient blood represent to your vampire?
- Which coterie member may veto waking the elder?

### GM-Only Hidden Truth

Hidden-truth id: `wod-vtm-frame-empty-throne-secret-001`

The elder is in voluntary torpor below the domain. The executor, not the elder, sent the fresh instruction under valid but old authority. The executor fabricated only the local Beckoning story; this frame makes no claim about the global phenomenon. The contradictory boons are a deliberate multi-party lock, while the hostile cell learned the location through a separate compromised childe.

### Reveal Requirements

Require retainer logistics, sealed-instruction provenance, and evidence from at least two claimants before locating the chamber. Determining the elder's intent after torpor may remain impossible without waking them.

### Safe Foreshadowing

- Maintenance consumption never fell after the supposed departure.
- The fresh instruction uses an old private convention.
- No claimant possesses both physical and social access.
- The hostile cell asks about a person with specific history, not a generic artifact.

### Forbidden Early Wording

Do not state that the global Beckoning is false, the elder is awake, the executor is mind-controlled, or diablerie is the only threat.

### Boundaries And Review

Discuss elder abuse, torpor, blood dependency, diablerie, cult violence, inherited coercion, body horror, harm to retainers, and Final Death. Set power level and PvP limits before selection.

### Expansion

Expand into Gehenna War only after the local elder decision resolves and the group affirmatively selects mobile, high-action metaplot play.

## Frame Materialization Rule

Materialize exactly one frame. Copy its player-facing promise, selected actors, causal chain, clock, and protected hidden truth into appropriate campaign owners. Do not copy alternate frames or their secrets. Re-name every placeholder through Session 0; a package role is not an accepted person.
