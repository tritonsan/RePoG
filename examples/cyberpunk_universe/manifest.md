## What This Package Is

This is a researched setting package for Mike Pondsmith and R. Talsorian Games' Cyberpunk universe as continued in cooperation with CD PROJEKT RED. It is not a generic cyberpunk-genre kit, a summary of one tabletop edition, or a retelling of the *Cyberpunk 2077* video game.

The package keeps four materially different periods separate:

- the corporate order associated with 2013/2020
- the Fourth Corporate War and 2023 catastrophe
- the 2045 Time of the Red
- the 2070s of *Cyberpunk: Edgerunners* and *Cyberpunk 2077*

A future campaign selects one era before choosing technology, institutions, Net access, corporate posture, named events, or rules. The package never combines all periods into a timeless inventory.

Source claims: `claim-cyberpunk-franchise-continuity`, `claim-cyberpunk-era-bridge`, `claim-cyberpunk-war-datakrash`.

## Player Promise

You play people trying to live, build, expose, transport, heal, perform, negotiate, protect, or profit in a world where bodies, information, shelter, security, and supply routes are contested. Corporations and states are powerful but not omnipotent. Gangs and security providers are not interchangeable. Neighborhoods contain workers, families, artists, patients, drivers, bartenders, medics, technicians, organizers, and countless other people whose choices matter.

The default opening is 2045: one bounded Night City district depends on fragile infrastructure; one supply chain has failed; one corporate or security claimant asserts a competing right; and an original crew must decide whom its skills serve. No famous protagonist, canonical fixer, or published legend arrives to solve the problem.

## Scope Locks

Unless Session 0 explicitly changes them:

- The setting is the official Cyberpunk fictional universe, not generic genre shorthand.
- One continuity era governs the opening.
- Night City is a valid canonical setting, but the opening district situation, crew, and active faces are original and bounded.
- The default year is 2045.
- The default crew contains three to five original player characters with complementary capabilities and a practical reason to cooperate.
- Famous characters remain background, rumor, history, or absent.
- Gameplay abstractions do not become setting physics merely because a game implements them.
- Cyberware never determines a person's worth, morality, identity, or inevitable violence.
- Player identity, body, augmentation, disability, style, history, relationships, debts, loyalties, and boundaries remain player-authored.
- Artificial-intelligence, engram, or wholly synthetic player characters are source-gated rather than assumed.
- Exact corporate portfolios, Net capabilities, cyberpsychosis explanations, political borders, and global conditions require era-appropriate sources.
- Accepted campaign history outranks later package updates.

## Four Semantic Layers

### Template-Fixed

The selected era, material inequality, contested infrastructure, institutional dependence on people and logistics, asymmetric information, body autonomy, local consequences, and the separation of setting truth from mechanics. These principles may be changed only by an explicit campaign divergence.

### Campaign-Instance

Exact year, district, route network, crew mandate, active powers, supply failure, local services, technology inventory, Net model, named faces, clocks, hidden truth, rules implementation, and famous-character proximity.

### Player-Authored

Each character's identity, body, cyberware or lack of it, disability, presentation, style, profession, skills, history, family, community, beliefs, debts, employer ties, trauma, ambitions, secrets, crew relationships, and safety boundaries.

### Era/Media-Specific

2013/2020 corporate conditions, the 2023 war and disaster, 2045 scarcity and CitiNet structures, 2070 quickhacks and institutions, published protagonists, adaptation-specific outcomes, and product mechanics. Nothing in this layer imports silently.

## Default Proposal

- Era: 2045, Time of the Red
- Locale: Night City
- Opening area: one original district-scale rebuilding network adjacent to, but not replacing, canonical geography
- Crew: three to five original people with mixed social, technical, investigative, medical, mobility, combat, or media capabilities
- Frame: `cyberpunk-frame-last-light`
- Visible pressure: a community utility dependency, a missing replacement component, and a corporate/security ownership claim
- Scale: one district, one supply route, at most one CitiNet connection layer
- Corporate posture: local claimant, employer, contractor, customer, or rival—not world-owning narrator authority
- Named characters: absent
- Net posture: 2045 CitiNet and local NET Architectures only as sourced; no automatic 2070 quickhacking
- Rules: system-neutral by default; owned *Cyberpunk RED* if mechanics are desired

This proposal is not active until accepted through [Session 0 Options](session_zero_options.md).

## Compatibility Contract

This package may inform a future campaign only when all of the following are explicit:

- selected era and continuity
- selected locale and opening scale
- accepted crew fantasy and mandate
- selected frame or custom pressure
- player-authored character scope
- rules source and mechanical implementation
- Net and cyberware model for that era
- famous-character and spoiler policy
- tone, safety, and cultural boundaries
- user approval to materialize selected content

A future materializer must prune. It must not copy every era, region, faction, frame, hidden truth, product outcome, or famous character into active memory.

## Authority And Isolation

This directory contains Markdown reference material only. It contains no active profile, state, snapshot, turn log, accepted boundary, runtime instruction, schema, registry entry, loader, resolver, or campaign command.

It does not override `AGENTS.md`, workflows, profiles, or files under `campaign/`. The only active campaign root remains `campaign/`. No file in this package may be treated as accepted state merely because it exists.

## Original Versus Canonical Content

Official-source claims are indexed in [Source Provenance](source_provenance.md). The default district canvas, comparison scores, local actor templates, six campaign frames, hidden truths, clocks, knowledge taxonomy, selection process, and materialization rules are RePoG-original design proposals. They are compatible scaffolds, not franchise canon.
