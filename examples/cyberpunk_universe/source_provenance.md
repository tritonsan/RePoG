# Cyberpunk Universe Source Provenance

## Research Snapshot

- Retrieval cutoff: 2026-07-23
- Universe focus: Mike Pondsmith/R. Talsorian Games' Cyberpunk continuity and its CD PROJEKT RED continuation
- Default era focus: 2045, Time of the Red
- Later-era focus: broad official *Cyberpunk: Edgerunners*, *Cyberpunk 2077*, and *Phantom Liberty* scope
- Attribution mode: official links, bibliographic identification, and paraphrased claim summaries
- Verbatim source text: not stored
- Fan-wiki authority: none
- Commercial-text limitation: full paid rulebooks, city books, screen works, and game databases were not independently re-read during this pass
- Confidence policy: high for explicit public descriptions and dates; medium or unresolved for fine mechanics, absent search results, global conditions, and details requiring commercial sources

## Retrieval Notes

- R. Talsorian's WordPress JSON search API was used to locate official posts and avoid relying on guessed slugs.
- Exact-title search for “Cyberpunk 2077 Campaign Book” returned the 2025 announcement and no 2026 release post; a date-filtered 2026 posts query returned an empty result at retrieval time.
- The official downloads page reported a 2026-07-20 modification and did not list the announced campaign book by that title. This supports only an unverified-release classification, not a cancellation claim.
- `https://rtalsoriangames.com/wp-sitemap.xml` returned 404 and is not cited.
- `https://www.cyberpunk.net/us/en/edgerunners` returned 404 and is not cited.
- The R. Talsorian store collection page did not yield readable content and is not cited.
- Public product and preview pages were used conservatively; no commercial rule text, stat block, item catalog, map, artwork, transcript, or long quotation was copied.
- No fan wiki or generic cyberpunk source supplied authority.
