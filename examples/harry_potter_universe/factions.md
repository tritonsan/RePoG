# Harry Potter Universe Factions

## Use Contract

These are power models, not instructions to instantiate every named organization. Select three or four opening powers. Give each a local face, mandate, constituency, capability, dependency, internal disagreement, information gap, and clock. A global institution without a bounded office or relationship is background, not an active faction.

## Power Cards

### `harry-potter-faction-ministry-civil` — Bounded Ministry Office

- **Instantiate as:** one department desk, regional liaison, records team, transport office, or interdepartmental group
- **Capabilities:** issue permissions, maintain records, inspect compliance, coordinate services, and make recommendations within mandate
- **Dependencies:** accurate information, trained staff, other departments, public cooperation, and political cover
- **Limits:** jurisdiction, backlog, incomplete records, elections, oversight, and institutional bias
- **Internal disagreement:** uniform procedure protects fairness versus local discretion prevents harm
- **PC leverage:** evidence, service expertise, constituent testimony, lawful challenge, or a workable pilot
- **Claims:** `claim-harry-potter-ministry-government`, `claim-harry-potter-institutions-fallible`

### `harry-potter-faction-law-enforcement` — Auror Or Magical-Law Unit

- **Instantiate as:** a two-to-five-person investigation unit with a named supervisor and exact jurisdiction
- **Capabilities:** investigate dark magic, apprehend dangerous practitioners, secure scenes, and coordinate detention where sourced
- **Dependencies:** admissible evidence, witnesses, warrants or authority, healer support, and public legitimacy
- **Limits:** cannot know guilt by title, cannot repair community trust through force, and may lack specialist knowledge
- **Internal disagreement:** rapid containment versus patient evidence-building
- **PC leverage:** scene access, independent expertise, witness trust, procedural challenge, or proof of a mistaken theory
- **Claims:** `claim-harry-potter-aurors`, `claim-harry-potter-wizengamot`

### `harry-potter-faction-wizengamot` — Legislative And Judicial Power

- **Instantiate as:** one committee, hearing panel, reform caucus, clerkship, or legal proceeding
- **Capabilities:** legislate, hear serious matters, establish precedent, summon testimony, and shape institutional legitimacy
- **Dependencies:** members, procedure, records, enforcement, and public acceptance
- **Limits:** combined functions can concentrate power; exact composition and process are era-specific
- **Internal disagreement:** continuity and institutional dignity versus due process and reform
- **PC leverage:** evidence, standing, counsel, public scrutiny, precedent, or coalition
- **Claims:** `claim-harry-potter-wizengamot`, `claim-harry-potter-ministry-government`

### `harry-potter-faction-school` — Hogwarts Or Selected School Authority

- **Instantiate as:** one year team, department, pastoral group, club sponsor, safeguarding panel, or exchange office
- **Capabilities:** teach, evaluate, grant access, enforce rules, supervise students, and convene school resources
- **Dependencies:** staff labor, family trust, supplies, student cooperation, and external legitimacy
- **Limits:** tradition, uneven judgment, secrecy, age power, and era-specific danger
- **Internal disagreement:** school autonomy and inherited practice versus transparency and duty of care
- **PC leverage:** student testimony, staff expertise, parent pressure, peer organizing, or a safer alternative
- **Claims:** `claim-harry-potter-hogwarts-education`, `claim-harry-potter-international-schools`

### `harry-potter-faction-student-network` — House-Crossing Peer Network

- **Instantiate as:** one club, study circle, team, mutual-aid network, publication, or elected representation experiment
- **Capabilities:** share knowledge, notice daily patterns, organize peers, create culture, and test practical solutions
- **Dependencies:** time, trust, safe rooms, adult support, and protection from retaliation
- **Limits:** minors do not own institutional safeguarding duties; rumor and peer pressure can cause harm
- **Internal disagreement:** loyalty to House or friends versus broader solidarity
- **PC leverage:** participation, listening, mentorship, resources, and consent-based leadership
- **Claims:** `claim-harry-potter-hogwarts-education`, `claim-harry-potter-daily-life`

### `harry-potter-faction-healers` — Care Network

- **Instantiate as:** one St Mungo's service, school hospital wing team, community clinic, referral network, or mobile response group
- **Capabilities:** assess, stabilize, treat, document, refer, and protect confidentiality within sourced practice
- **Dependencies:** ingredients, expertise, beds, route access, records, and patient consent
- **Limits:** not every condition is curable; care cannot erase coercion, grief, disability, or supply failure
- **Internal disagreement:** maximize throughput versus protect individualized and equitable care
- **PC leverage:** missing history, specialist skill, supply repair, patient advocacy, or consent procedure
- **Claims:** `claim-harry-potter-healing`, `claim-harry-potter-magic-not-universal-remedy`

### `harry-potter-faction-gringotts` — Goblin Banking Office

- **Instantiate as:** one branch function, account team, archive, maker-rights representative, or authorized expedition
- **Capabilities:** secure assets, authenticate custody, manage accounts, operate bank infrastructure, and interpret internal records
- **Dependencies:** goblin expertise, keys and authorization, trustworthy records, clients, and physical security
- **Limits:** wizarding law and client expectations may conflict with goblin concepts of ownership; exact contracts are source-gated
- **Internal disagreement:** preserve institutional autonomy versus negotiate sustainable public relationships
- **PC leverage:** provenance, authorization, reciprocal expertise, restitution, or a jointly written agreement
- **Claims:** `claim-harry-potter-gringotts`, `claim-harry-potter-goblin-agency`

### `harry-potter-faction-trade-network` — Magical Traders And Makers

- **Instantiate as:** four businesses sharing a delivery route, apprenticeship network, market association, or ingredient cooperative
- **Capabilities:** make, repair, stock, price, teach, extend credit, and know local demand
- **Dependencies:** suppliers, transport, bank access, skilled labor, licenses, and customer trust
- **Limits:** fragmented interests, thin margins, counterfeit risk, and unequal bargaining power
- **Internal disagreement:** protect shared access versus capture scarce supply
- **PC leverage:** repair a route, verify goods, mediate credit, train apprentices, or organize a cooperative response
- **Claims:** `claim-harry-potter-diagon-commerce`, `claim-harry-potter-daily-life`

### `harry-potter-faction-press` — Newspaper Or Independent Publication

- **Instantiate as:** one desk, editor, reporter pair, local circular, or source-protection network
- **Capabilities:** investigate, publish, frame debate, reach households, and preserve or destroy reputations
- **Dependencies:** sources, evidence, access, owl distribution, readers, and revenue
- **Limits:** sensationalism, Ministry influence, deadlines, legal pressure, and incomplete context
- **Internal disagreement:** publish quickly for public safety versus verify and protect vulnerable sources
- **PC leverage:** documents, corroboration, firsthand expertise, alternative distribution, or accountability
- **Claims:** `claim-harry-potter-press`, `claim-harry-potter-institutions-fallible`

### `harry-potter-faction-rights-coalition` — Affected-People-Led Reform Network

- **Instantiate as:** a coalition centered on Squib access, werewolf employment and privacy, house-elf freedom, goblin agreements, prison conditions, or another selected issue
- **Capabilities:** collect testimony, provide mutual aid, propose policy, organize, litigate, negotiate, and monitor implementation
- **Dependencies:** affected members' leadership, safe meeting space, resources, allies, and protection from retaliation
- **Limits:** internal diversity, burnout, surveillance, tokenization, and disagreement over goals
- **Internal disagreement:** immediate individual relief versus slower structural reform
- **PC leverage:** follow leadership, supply skills, protect evidence, transfer resources, or open a decision channel
- **Claims:** `claim-harry-potter-squib-access`, `claim-harry-potter-werewolf-stigma`, `claim-harry-potter-house-elf-bondage`, `claim-harry-potter-goblin-agency`

### `harry-potter-faction-blood-patronage` — Inherited-Status Network

- **Instantiate as:** one family alliance, donor circle, alumni network, hiring bloc, or extremist remnant; never an entire social class with one mind
- **Capabilities:** money, introductions, property, legal expertise, records, reputation, and covert influence
- **Dependencies:** deference, secrecy, institutional access, family compliance, and ideological reproduction
- **Limits:** internal dissent, public scrutiny, demographic reality, competing loyalties, and loss of legitimacy
- **Internal disagreement:** preserve privilege quietly versus restore overt supremacist rule
- **PC leverage:** expose dependency, support dissenters without centering them, challenge procedure, or build an alternative resource network
- **Claims:** `claim-harry-potter-blood-ideology`, `claim-harry-potter-institutions-fallible`

### `harry-potter-faction-international` — Delegation Or Coordinating Body

- **Instantiate as:** one school delegation, tournament committee, treaty working group, cross-border investigation, or creature-protection team
- **Capabilities:** convene jurisdictions, set event standards, share specialist knowledge, and coordinate movement
- **Dependencies:** member cooperation, translation, host logistics, secrecy compliance, and agreed mandate
- **Limits:** sovereignty, unequal influence, differing law, travel constraints, and incomplete cultural knowledge
- **Internal disagreement:** common minimum standards versus local autonomy
- **PC leverage:** interpretation, local trust, comparative evidence, logistics, or a face-saving compromise
- **Claims:** `claim-harry-potter-international-schools`, `claim-harry-potter-quidditch-international`, `claim-harry-potter-fantastic-beasts-era`

## Default Opening Power Budget

For `harry-potter-frame-mended-network`, instantiate only:

1. one local office based on `harry-potter-faction-ministry-civil`
2. one resident-and-trader network based on `harry-potter-faction-trade-network`
3. one care/referral team based on `harry-potter-faction-healers`
4. one affected-people-led access group based on `harry-potter-faction-rights-coalition`

The Daily Prophet, central Ministry leadership, Gringotts, Hogwarts, and Aurors begin off-stage unless selected during Session 0 or reached through play.

## Power Action And Clock Contract

The default `harry-potter-frame-mended-network` does not create four separate faction clocks. Actions by its four opening powers feed exactly two shared clocks: `harry-potter-clock-route-failure` and `harry-potter-clock-access-lockdown`. Each faction still records a public objective, next action, constituency, internal dissenter, external dependency, and practical limit.

A custom frame chooses one clock model before play:

- at most two shared situation clocks, with power actions feeding them; or
- at most four bounded power clocks, one per active power, with no duplicate shared clock

Every retained clock needs an owner, visible condition, next material change, advancement evidence, affected people, reversibility, and redirect path. A power does not advance merely because players ignored it. Its people must have means, information, and opportunity to act.
