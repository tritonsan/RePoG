# Harry Potter Universe Campaign Frames

## Use Contract

These six frames are RePoG-original proposals built from sourced setting constraints. They are not lost Harry Potter stories, official sequels, or summaries of published plots. Materialize exactly one frame and its matching protected truth. Alternate frames and secrets remain inert.

The player-facing sections may be shown after continuity, era, spoiler, and safety choices. Sections labeled **GM-only protected truth** must never be exposed before their reveal requirements are met.

## `harry-potter-frame-mended-network` — The Mended Network

### Player-Facing Promise

Keep a hidden community connected when a once-reliable travel service begins sending the wrong people to the wrong hearths. Investigate without abandoning patients, workers, children, elders, or residents whose records do not fit the Ministry's restored map.

### Visible Situation

In an original British magical quarter, the public Floo hearth has begun refusing some destinations and redirecting some travelers. The local Ministry liaison calls it post-war tampering. Traders want the hearth closed before an exposure incident ruins them. A clinic cannot reliably send patients onward. Residents insist the failures follow a pattern, but official records say the affected addresses do not exist.

### Active Actors

- a bounded office using `harry-potter-faction-ministry-civil`
- a resident-and-trader network using `harry-potter-faction-trade-network`
- a clinic referral team using `harry-potter-faction-healers`
- an access group using `harry-potter-faction-rights-coalition`

### Causal Chain

`restored destination register → old routing protections react → selective misdirection → missed care and deliveries → pressure for shutdown → records and residents placed at risk`

### Shared Clocks

This frame uses exactly two package-original, player-visible clocks defined in [World Operating Model](world_operating_model.md):

- `harry-potter-clock-route-failure` tracks documented service deterioration and harm to users.
- `harry-potter-clock-access-lockdown` tracks the local Ministry office's escalating restrictions.

Actions by all four active powers feed these clocks; they receive no additional faction clocks. The protected truth remains separate and never appears on either clock.

### Neutral Action Space

Audit hearth behavior, escort vulnerable travelers, compare paper and lived maps, interview route maintainers, test bounded destinations, negotiate temporary access, protect sensitive records, publish or withhold evidence, build a manual fallback, or challenge the shutdown order.

### Inaction Trajectory

1. **Route 1/4:** deliveries and referrals become reliably delayed.
2. **Access 1/4:** the Ministry adds address-verification requirements.
3. **Route 2/4 and Access 2/4:** a missed critical journey prompts destination restrictions and trader pressure for closure.
4. **Route 3/4 and Access 3/4:** a public misroute overloads fallbacks while records or equipment enter official control.
5. **Route 4/4 or Access 4/4:** if the relevant condition or office has means and evidence, the old protection collapses toward exposure or the office authorizes closure and compelled disclosure.

These are evidence-backed possibilities, not automatic scene beats; player action may redirect either clock.

### PC Prompts

- Who depends on you reaching one specific hearth safely?
- Which institution has an inaccurate record about you or someone you protect?
- What ordinary skill do you contribute when magic is not the answer?
- What kind of secrecy would you refuse to preserve?

### GM-Only Protected Truth — `harry-potter-frame-mended-network-secret-001`

During the recent war, residents and one route worker created an unauthorized rerouting charm that diverted travelers away from addresses under hostile surveillance. They protected people by removing or altering entries in the official destination register. The restored pre-war register now conflicts with that living protection. Simply destroying the charm exposes the hidden households; preserving it unchanged excludes newcomers and keeps emergency travel unreliable. A fair repair requires consent-based remapping, institutional amnesty or resistance, and a replacement safety protocol.

### Reveal Requirements

Reveal only after the PCs establish three independent elements: a repeatable routing pattern, testimony from an affected household, and a surviving maintenance or destination record. The worker's identity requires consent or direct evidence; inference alone is insufficient.

### Safe Foreshadowing

- failures cluster around records changed during the war
- the hearth redirects away from danger more often than toward it
- older residents recognize destinations absent from official maps
- a maintenance mark uses two hands or styles

### Forbidden Early Wording

Do not say the hearth is cursed, sabotaged by extremists, possessed, sentient, or trying to kill travelers. Do not identify the route worker or hidden households before evidence supports it.

### Boundaries And Expansion

No famous rescuer, universal Floo collapse, or Ministry-wide conspiracy. Expand to Diagon Alley, St Mungo's, or a Ministry hearing only if player action establishes the connection. Source anchors: `claim-harry-potter-transport`, `claim-harry-potter-secrecy`, `claim-harry-potter-institutions-fallible`.

## `harry-potter-frame-fifth-table` — The Fifth Table

### Player-Facing Promise

Build an inter-House place to learn and organize when a shared school room repeatedly closes around the students who need it most.

### Visible Situation

A House-neutral study and club room, created for one selected school year, has begun locking itself during meetings. Timetables show the room as available. Staff disagree whether the cause is student mischief, aging magic, or an unauthorized club. The students excluded most often are transfers, late entrants, and those without strong House networks.

### Active Actors

- one pastoral or curriculum team using `harry-potter-faction-school`
- one peer network using `harry-potter-faction-student-network`
- one bounded oversight office using `harry-potter-faction-ministry-civil`
- one family or alumni pressure group selected during Session 0

### Causal Chain

`record normalization → access ward reads absence as ineligibility → room closures → peer blame → staff restriction → exclusion becomes policy`

### Neutral Action Space

Observe access patterns, compare timetables and records, interview former club members, seek staff sponsorship, create alternate space, test consent-based entry, organize across Houses, appeal discipline, or redesign the room's charter.

### Inaction Trajectory

1. Meetings fragment by House.
2. Rumor labels excluded students dangerous or dishonest.
3. Staff suspend all unsponsored clubs.
4. An external review treats unequal access as proof the room should close.
5. The shared project disappears and the faulty category enters school policy.

### PC Prompts

- Which school tradition helps you, and which shuts someone else out?
- Who taught you something important outside formal lessons?
- What responsibility should adults carry in this situation?
- Would you keep the room if its rules could not be made fair?

### GM-Only Protected Truth — `harry-potter-frame-fifth-table-secret-001`

The room and its access behavior are campaign-original. A former staff sponsor built a protective ward to close when disciplinary records indicated that one House was coercing another. A recent records cleanup collapsed transfer, late-entry, accommodation, and missing data into the same exclusion flag. The ward is obeying a badly translated safeguard, not choosing worthy students. Repair requires a new charter, accountable data practice, and adult safeguarding rather than defeating the room.

### Reveal Requirements

Require a controlled reproduction, the old club charter, and testimony from someone who understands both the ward and the records change. Students may identify harm, but adults retain responsibility for institutional repair.

### Safe Foreshadowing

- closures correlate with registry fields, not House traits
- the room opens for mixed groups under older paper lists
- a faded charter emphasizes mutual protection
- no hostile magic targets a person directly

### Forbidden Early Wording

Do not call the room conscious, biased by a founder, a secret canonical chamber, or proof that a House is dangerous.

### Boundaries And Expansion

Choose exact year, staff, curriculum, and continuity first. Avoid published annual crises and named protagonists. An 1890s version requires `harry-potter-game-hogwarts-legacy` only if game-specific people or history are imported. Source anchors: `claim-harry-potter-hogwarts-education`, `claim-harry-potter-era-chronology`.

## `harry-potter-frame-quiet-ward` — The Quiet Ward

### Player-Facing Promise

Protect patients and repair a care system when a common stabilizing draught begins working inconsistently and every institution wants a quick culprit.

### Visible Situation

One St Mungo's service has seen a cluster of delayed recoveries after using an ordinary support draught created for this campaign. No patient has the same original condition. Procurement says the formula and supplier are unchanged. A Ministry inspector considers seizing the batch; Healers fear that abrupt withdrawal will harm patients who still depend on it. Families want answers without losing confidentiality.

### Active Actors

- a ward team using `harry-potter-faction-healers`
- a supplier circle using `harry-potter-faction-trade-network`
- an inspector using `harry-potter-faction-ministry-civil`
- a patient-and-family group using `harry-potter-faction-rights-coalition`

### Causal Chain

`ingredient substitution → incomplete documentation → variable brewing response → patient harm → institutional blame → supply seizure or concealment`

### Neutral Action Space

Trace lots, interview patients with consent, reproduce a batch, compare storage and wandwork, establish independent care, protect records, negotiate a recall, examine labor conditions, communicate uncertainty, or redesign procurement.

### Inaction Trajectory

1. More treatments are delayed.
2. Rumor blames one patient group or junior brewer.
3. The inspector seals the supply without a replacement.
4. The hospital suppresses uncertainty to protect confidence.
5. Patients lose both care and trust.

### PC Prompts

- Whose consent matters before you inspect a medical record?
- Which part of care do you understand that senior staff overlook?
- What harm would a perfect public explanation still fail to repair?
- When would you refuse an order to keep treatment moving?

### GM-Only Protected Truth — `harry-potter-frame-quiet-ward-secret-001`

The draught and supply chain are campaign-original. The supplier replaced one ingredient after learning that its acquisition harmed an intelligent magical being. The substitute can work safely, but the supplier concealed the change because the hospital's contract allowed no ethical review and threatened immediate cancellation. A rushed brewer then followed timing notes calibrated to the old ingredient. The crisis comes from secrecy and procurement rigidity, not malice or an inherently inferior substitute.

### Reveal Requirements

Require patient-pattern analysis, physical or documentary evidence of substitution, and testimony from both a brewer and the affected supplier or being-community representative. Do not reveal private medical facts as a shortcut.

### Safe Foreshadowing

- failures vary with brewing time rather than diagnosis
- supplier seals are genuine but batch annotations differ
- the substitute has traceable ethical provenance
- junior staff noticed a texture change before outcomes changed

### Forbidden Early Wording

Do not call the batch poisoned, the supplier fraudulent, the patients cursed, or the substitute primitive. Do not imply magic should cure disability or trauma.

### Boundaries And Expansion

Keep one ward and one supply chain. Expand to Ministry review or Diagon Alley only through evidence. Source anchors: `claim-harry-potter-healing`, `claim-harry-potter-potions-bounded`, `claim-harry-potter-magic-not-universal-remedy`.

## `harry-potter-frame-ink-before-dawn` — Ink Before Dawn

### Player-Facing Promise

Decide how to tell a dangerous truth when the evidence is real, the file is incomplete, and publication may expose the people it should protect.

### Visible Situation

A small publication receives a Ministry memorandum suggesting that a transport or registration policy will be expanded after a recent incident. The Daily Prophet is preparing a reassuring official account. The memo bears valid marks but contains contradictory dates and a missing annex. A source demands publication before dawn and refuses to explain who else appears in the file.

### Active Actors

- an independent desk using `harry-potter-faction-press`
- a public-information office using `harry-potter-faction-ministry-civil`
- an affected group using `harry-potter-faction-rights-coalition`
- a rival or partner Daily Prophet desk, bounded to this story

### Causal Chain

`policy drafts → merged file → urgent leak → competing narratives → source exposure risk → public pressure before verification`

### Neutral Action Space

Authenticate marks, find the annex, corroborate with affected people, negotiate redactions, confront the office, prepare a staged publication, protect the source, publish uncertainty, or let another outlet lead.

### Inaction Trajectory

1. The official account defines the incident.
2. The source approaches a less careful outlet.
3. A policy pilot begins without scrutiny.
4. Vulnerable names circulate through rumor.
5. Later correction cannot restore trust or privacy.

### PC Prompts

- Whose story have you previously told without enough consent?
- What evidence would make you delay a true story?
- Which institution can cut off your access?
- What would you publish even if nobody believed you?

### GM-Only Protected Truth — `harry-potter-frame-ink-before-dawn-secret-001`

The memorandum is genuine, but the source combined two official drafts so that an audit trigger would activate before a vulnerable registry could be destroyed. One draft proposes coercive expansion; the other rejects it. The missing annex identifies people whose testimony changed the later draft. Publishing the raw file proves institutional debate but exposes those witnesses and may falsely present a rejected option as settled policy.

### Reveal Requirements

Require document comparison, independent confirmation of the policy process, and consent-aware contact with at least one affected witness. Magical authenticity proves origin, not completeness or interpretation.

### Safe Foreshadowing

- ink, dates, and routing marks are internally valid but not contemporaneous
- paragraph numbering skips between drafting styles
- the source asks about audit procedure, not only headlines
- the official spokesperson avoids claiming the memo is forged

### Forbidden Early Wording

Do not call the file fake, a definitive secret law, a total Ministry conspiracy, or proof that every journalist is controlled.

### Boundaries And Expansion

Choose one policy and one publication ecosystem. A war-era version requires higher spoiler and coercion boundaries. Source anchors: `claim-harry-potter-press`, `claim-harry-potter-ministry-government`, `claim-harry-potter-institutions-fallible`.

## `harry-potter-frame-borrowed-key` — The Borrowed Key

### Player-Facing Promise

Resolve a custody dispute without turning goblins into gatekeepers or wizard heirs into automatic owners when two legitimate histories claim the same crafted object.

### Visible Situation

A small campaign-original magical instrument is due to leave a Gringotts account under a wizarding inheritance document. The bank suspends transfer because its maker record describes a return condition. The inheritor needs the instrument to keep a community service operating. A goblin craft representative says use may continue, but transfer of title has never been agreed.

### Active Actors

- a bounded office using `harry-potter-faction-gringotts`
- an inheritor and service network using `harry-potter-faction-trade-network`
- a legal committee or mediator using `harry-potter-faction-wizengamot`
- a maker-rights group using `harry-potter-faction-rights-coalition`

### Causal Chain

`death or succession → incompatible custody records → transfer suspended → service loses instrument → public blame → forced seizure or negotiated stewardship`

### Neutral Action Space

Trace provenance, translate records, inspect the instrument by consent, create a temporary-use agreement, find substitute capacity, hear maker testimony, challenge inheritance assumptions, compensate labor, or establish shared stewardship.

### Inaction Trajectory

1. The service reduces operations.
2. The inheritor mobilizes political pressure.
3. Bank staff harden security.
4. Press coverage frames one side as theft.
5. A forced transfer damages future cooperation even if the object survives.

### PC Prompts

- What have you inherited that you do not believe you fully own?
- Which kind of expertise has an institution ignored?
- Who depends on the object but has no title claim?
- What would fair stewardship require from you personally?

### GM-Only Protected Truth — `harry-potter-frame-borrowed-key-secret-001`

The instrument is package-original. A deceased wizard broker knowingly maintained two ledgers: a wizarding inheritance record and a goblin maker-return agreement. The broker expected future parties to negotiate but concealed the conflict to retain status and access. Neither current claimant created the contradiction. A third maintenance ledger shows that community users funded most repairs, giving them a material interest even though neither legal tradition names them as owner.

### Reveal Requirements

Require both primary ledgers, independent verification of the maintenance record, and direct representation from goblin makers and community users. A key opening a vault proves access, not rightful title.

### Safe Foreshadowing

- both documents have valid provenance
- repair marks differ from original craft marks
- fees were paid by an account not named in either title record
- bank staff consistently distinguish custody, use, and ownership

### Forbidden Early Wording

Do not call the bank greedy, the goblins treacherous, the heir entitled by nature, or the object stolen before the custody histories are heard.

### Boundaries And Expansion

Keep one object, one account, and one affected service. Exact Gringotts law, curse-breaking, and vault security require targeted sources. Source anchors: `claim-harry-potter-gringotts`, `claim-harry-potter-goblin-agency`.

## `harry-potter-frame-lantern-delegation` — The Lantern Delegation

### Player-Facing Promise

Host an international magical exchange while protecting migrating creatures, navigating incompatible rules, and refusing the easy story that visitors brought the danger.

### Visible Situation

A visiting school or field delegation arrives at a researched or explicitly original exchange venue. Small luminous creatures created for this campaign begin gathering around wards and luggage, disrupting travel and drawing secrecy concerns. The host authority suspects smuggling. The visitors say the creatures are unknown to them. A nearby caretaker reports that local migration patterns changed before the delegation arrived.

### Active Actors

- a host and visiting team using `harry-potter-faction-international`
- a school or field authority using `harry-potter-faction-school`
- a caretaker-and-resident network using `harry-potter-faction-rights-coalition`
- a regulator using `harry-potter-faction-ministry-civil`

### Causal Chain

`new protective wards → habitat corridor displaced → creatures follow enchanted light → delegation arrival creates false attribution → containment harms migration → diplomatic and secrecy crisis`

### Neutral Action Space

Map movement, compare ward timing, interview caretakers, establish a safe corridor, translate regulations, inspect luggage by consent, relocate events, redesign lighting, negotiate jurisdiction, or communicate uncertainty to the public.

### Inaction Trajectory

1. Creatures enter increasingly public spaces.
2. Officials quarantine the delegation.
3. Host and visitors accuse each other.
4. A forced removal damages the habitat.
5. Secrecy response and diplomatic blame outlast the event.

### PC Prompts

- Which custom do others misunderstand about your home or school?
- What creature or place has taught you to observe before acting?
- What authority are you willing to challenge as a guest?
- Who needs an interpreter even when everyone shares a spoken language?

### GM-Only Protected Truth — `harry-potter-frame-lantern-delegation-secret-001`

The creatures and ward interaction are campaign-original. A host security contractor strengthened perimeter wards before the exchange and displaced a long-standing migration corridor. The creatures follow one harmless frequency in the visiting delegation's enchanted lanterns, making the visitors appear responsible. The contractor knew the caretaker's survey was incomplete but met the written approval standard. Repair requires habitat access, ward redesign, and shared accountability rather than proving smuggling.

### Reveal Requirements

Require a pre-arrival migration observation, ward-change records, and a controlled comparison of local and visiting enchantments. Do not resolve another culture through a British expert's assumption.

### Safe Foreshadowing

- creatures were seen near the venue before the visitors arrived
- they follow light behavior, not nationality or luggage ownership
- old paths terminate at newly strengthened wards
- the contractor's approval is valid but ecologically narrow

### Forbidden Early Wording

Do not call the visitors smugglers, the creatures invasive, the host corrupt, or the behavior an attack without evidence.

### Boundaries And Expansion

Choose an exact date and source policy first. A version set on dates established by a selected Fantastic Beasts work requires `harry-potter-screen-fantastic-beasts` plus that identified film or screenplay. Any wider 1920s–1940s bridge needs separately cited book/web history or an explicit campaign-original label. An 1800s version may use `harry-potter-game-hogwarts-legacy` only for accepted details. Source anchors: `claim-harry-potter-international-schools`, `claim-harry-potter-secrecy`, `claim-harry-potter-fantastic-beasts-era`.

## Materialization Gate

Before copying a frame into active campaign files:

- [ ] continuity, era, year, and spoilers are accepted
- [ ] one theater and at most one connection layer are selected
- [ ] the group fantasy and PC authorship boundaries are accepted
- [ ] exactly one frame is selected
- [ ] only its matching secret is available to a named GM-exclusive owner; no `*-secret-*` id or protected body enters player-visible material
- [ ] the retained clock model is capped and reconciled; the default has exactly two shared clocks and no faction clocks
- [ ] visible actors have original local faces and limits
- [ ] sensitive themes have explicit lines, veils, or handling tools
- [ ] named-character proximity is recorded
- [ ] all campaign-original magic is labeled as such
- [ ] source claims survive materialization

A custom frame may replace all six. It must still define a visible need, causal chain, active powers, neutral action space, inaction trajectory, protected truth, reveal requirements, and safety boundary.
