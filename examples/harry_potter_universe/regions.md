# Harry Potter Universe Regions

## Use Contract

Select exactly one physical opening theater and at most one compatible connection layer. A region card is a bounded campaign canvas, not permission to load every named resident, room, shop, spell, or published event. Canonical places retain their sourced identity; original streets, offices, wards, businesses, and communities must be labeled campaign-instance.

## Physical Theater Cards

### `harry-potter-region-recovery-quarter` — Original Recovery Quarter

- **Status:** package-original default; not a canonical district
- **Player promise:** live where hidden shops, homes, care referrals, and travel routes make magic part of ordinary responsibility
- **Canon anchors:** British magical secrecy, regulated Floo travel, Diagon Alley commerce, St Mungo's care, owl-delivered news
- **Opening boundary:** one original market lane, two residential courts, one public Floo hearth, one clinic referral desk, and one non-magical threshold street
- **Include:** workers, families, apprentices, recent graduates, Squibs, elders, traders, patients, route staff, and accepted nonhuman participants
- **Exclude by default:** Hogwarts campus, central Ministry leadership, famous homes, unique artifacts, and published villains
- **Pressures:** route reliability, access records, reconstruction grants, rent, potion supply, secrecy incidents, and contested memory
- **Claims:** `claim-harry-potter-secrecy`, `claim-harry-potter-transport`, `claim-harry-potter-daily-life`

### `harry-potter-region-hogwarts` — Hogwarts And Grounds

- **Status:** canonical; exact staff, rooms, rules, defenses, and calendar are era-specific
- **Player promise:** education, friendship, rivalry, discovery, duty of care, and institutional tradition in a residential magical school
- **Opening boundary:** one year group or staff team, two shared learning spaces, one House-neutral need, and one edge of the grounds
- **Include:** lessons, mentoring, clubs, exams, House networks, portraits, ghosts, school care, and age-appropriate risk after sourcing
- **Exclude by default:** every secret chamber, published annual crisis, all named teachers, and unrestricted castle omniscience
- **Pressures:** access, discipline, curriculum, safeguarding, tradition, inter-House trust, and external oversight
- **Claims:** `claim-harry-potter-hogwarts-education`, `claim-harry-potter-international-schools`

### `harry-potter-region-hogsmeade` — Hogsmeade

- **Status:** canonical village; exact businesses and residents require era sources
- **Player promise:** a magical settlement where students, residents, workers, visitors, and school authority meet in public
- **Opening boundary:** one street, three establishments, one school access rule, and one surrounding path
- **Include:** hospitality, retail, deliveries, local gossip, seasonal events, and student/resident tensions
- **Exclude by default:** a complete shop catalog, secret passages, and named proprietors without sourcing
- **Pressures:** tourism, school weekends, safety, supply, privacy, and who counts as a local stakeholder
- **Claims:** `claim-harry-potter-hogsmeade`, `claim-harry-potter-daily-life`

### `harry-potter-region-diagon-alley` — Diagon Alley

- **Status:** canonical commercial district; exact shop survival and ownership are era-specific
- **Player promise:** trade, craft, banking, news, supply, and hidden access concentrated in one magical street network
- **Opening boundary:** one trade block, four businesses or services, one loading route, and one threshold to non-magical London
- **Include:** apprentices, makers, booksellers, potion suppliers, families, bank clients, journalists, and regulators
- **Exclude by default:** every canonical shop, Knockturn Alley as automatic evil shorthand, and famous-owner cameos
- **Pressures:** licensing, counterfeit goods, ingredient custody, credit, secrecy, redevelopment, and unequal access
- **Claims:** `claim-harry-potter-diagon-commerce`, `claim-harry-potter-gringotts`, `claim-harry-potter-press`

### `harry-potter-region-ministry` — Ministry Of Magic

- **Status:** canonical institution; departments and office-holders vary by era
- **Player promise:** public service, law, bureaucracy, investigation, reform, and the gap between mandate and outcome
- **Opening boundary:** one department or interdepartmental working group, one public-facing service, one records corridor, and one oversight route
- **Include:** clerks, specialists, visitors, advocates, investigators, and elected authority at a distance
- **Exclude by default:** access to every department, unrestricted classified records, and the Minister as routine patron
- **Pressures:** jurisdiction, elections, secrecy compliance, evidence, patronage, backlog, and public legitimacy
- **Claims:** `claim-harry-potter-ministry-government`, `claim-harry-potter-wizengamot`, `claim-harry-potter-aurors`

### `harry-potter-region-st-mungos` — St Mungo's Hospital

- **Status:** canonical magical hospital; exact wards, staff, and treatments require sources
- **Player promise:** care under pressure where magic helps without making injury, disability, consent, or grief trivial
- **Opening boundary:** one intake service, one specialist ward, one supply chain, and one family waiting area
- **Include:** Healers, trainees, patients, visitors, porters, ingredient suppliers, and community advocates
- **Exclude by default:** miracle cures, diagnostic omniscience, named long-term patients, and trauma as spectacle
- **Pressures:** triage, confidentiality, consent, scarce expertise, mislabelled ingredients, access, and institutional reputation
- **Claims:** `claim-harry-potter-healing`, `claim-harry-potter-magic-not-universal-remedy`

### `harry-potter-region-gringotts` — Gringotts And Its Public Interfaces

- **Status:** canonical goblin-owned and operated bank; vault and contract detail is source-gated
- **Player promise:** custody, craft, inheritance, security, and conflicting concepts of ownership
- **Opening boundary:** one public hall function, one account or object dispute, one authorized route, and one external claimant
- **Include:** goblin decision-makers, clients, translators, curse-breakers if sourced, security staff, and legal representatives
- **Exclude by default:** robbery as the assumed play loop, unrestricted vault access, and goblins as traps or obstacles
- **Pressures:** keys, authorization, maker rights, inheritance, historical records, trust, and wizarding regulation
- **Claims:** `claim-harry-potter-gringotts`, `claim-harry-potter-goblin-agency`

### `harry-potter-region-azkaban` — Azkaban

- **Status:** canonical prison; high-harm, source- and safety-gated theater
- **Player promise:** inspect detention, oversight, reform, records, and the consequences of institutional cruelty
- **Opening boundary:** one authorized visit, inspection team, records inquiry, or post-release support route; not a dungeon crawl
- **Include:** prisoners as people, advocates, rotating guards in the sourced post-war model, healers, inspectors, and families
- **Exclude by default:** torture spectacle, casual Dementor use, mass escape, and guilt inferred from imprisonment
- **Pressures:** due process, humane conditions, staff rotation, records, security claims, and accountability
- **Claims:** `claim-harry-potter-azkaban-reform`, `claim-harry-potter-aurors`

### `harry-potter-region-rural-britain` — Original Hidden Community

- **Status:** campaign-original settlement or route anchored to sourced secrecy practices
- **Player promise:** explore how magical households, non-magical neighbors, land, creatures, and public services coexist beyond major institutions
- **Opening boundary:** one village edge, three magical households, one creature habitat, and one transport link
- **Include:** farmers, caretakers, local healers, mixed families, Squibs, ward maintainers, and non-magical witnesses
- **Exclude by default:** an invented ancient super-school, sovereign micro-state, or unlimited creature reserve
- **Pressures:** concealment, land use, weather, transport, generational change, creature safety, and local memory
- **Claims:** `claim-harry-potter-secrecy`, `claim-harry-potter-transport`, `claim-harry-potter-daily-life`

### `harry-potter-region-international-school` — Exchange Campus Or Delegation

- **Status:** source-gated; select one researched school or create an explicitly original exchange venue
- **Player promise:** learn across magical traditions without treating Britain as universal or other cultures as spectacle
- **Opening boundary:** one visiting cohort, one shared project, two institutional hosts, and one translation or jurisdiction issue
- **Include:** students or staff, interpreters, families, local officials, and different educational assumptions
- **Exclude by default:** national stereotypes, unsourced curricula, and homogenized accents or magical practices
- **Pressures:** recognition of qualifications, safeguarding, travel permission, competition, diplomacy, and cultural ownership
- **Claims:** `claim-harry-potter-international-schools`, `claim-harry-potter-fantastic-beasts-era`

### `harry-potter-region-quidditch-circuit` — Quidditch Event Network

- **Status:** canonical sport with international organization; exact rules and teams require selected sources
- **Player promise:** compete, report, heal, officiate, organize, or protect a mass magical event
- **Opening boundary:** one qualifying event or tournament week, two teams, one venue, and one support network
- **Include:** athletes, coaches, officials, medics, journalists, vendors, transport staff, fans, and local residents
- **Exclude by default:** copying a published World Cup result, celebrity cameos, and treating crowd safety as disposable spectacle
- **Pressures:** secrecy, travel, officiating, protest, security, sponsorship, injury, and media narrative
- **Claims:** `claim-harry-potter-quidditch-international`, `claim-harry-potter-press`

## Connection Layers

### `harry-potter-region-floo-layer` — Regulated Hearth Network

This layer connects one selected physical theater to no more than three destinations. Record each hearth's owner, permission, users, operating hours, regulator, failure evidence, and fallback. Children, elders, and disabled travelers must not be treated as edge cases. Do not assume every Hogwarts fireplace or non-magical chimney is connected. Claims: `claim-harry-potter-transport`, `claim-harry-potter-secrecy`.

### `harry-potter-region-press-owl-layer` — News And Correspondence Network

This layer connects sources, editors, readers, owl routes, official statements, and small-circulation alternatives. Record who can publish, who pays, who is exposed, what is delayed, and what evidence survives. Moving photographs or quick delivery do not make a report unbiased. Claims: `claim-harry-potter-press`, `claim-harry-potter-daily-life`.

## Era/Media Overlays

- **1800s / Legacy:** attach `harry-potter-era-1800s` and `harry-potter-game-hogwarts-legacy`; import only sourced story facts, never gameplay density or mechanics.
- **Fantastic Beasts era:** attach `harry-potter-era-fantastic-beasts` and `harry-potter-screen-fantastic-beasts`; research the selected country and film before defining law or culture.
- **Main-series school:** attach `harry-potter-era-main-series` and choose book-core or screen-Harry-Potter; set a named-event spoiler ceiling.
- **Post-war default:** attach `harry-potter-era-postwar` and book-core; treat local reconstruction as original unless sourced.
- **Stage future:** attach `harry-potter-era-stage-future` and `harry-potter-stage-cursed-child`; protect stage-story outcomes.

## Default Opening Network

Materialize only:

- `harry-potter-region-recovery-quarter`
- optional `harry-potter-region-floo-layer`
- one market lane
- two residential courts
- one clinic referral desk
- one public hearth
- one non-magical threshold street
- three or four active powers from [Factions](factions.md)
- six to eight original faces
- `harry-potter-frame-mended-network`

Diagon Alley, St Mungo's, and the Ministry may exist as off-stage destinations or authorities. They do not become fully loaded regions until player action creates a durable connection.
