# Harry Potter Universe Source Provenance

## Research Snapshot

- Retrieval cutoff: 2026-07-23
- Universe focus: the Harry Potter fictional universe within the broader Wizarding World franchise surface
- Default narrative spine: seven original Harry Potter novels
- Default setting supplement: official author-attributed writings and official encyclopedia/fact files on HarryPotter.com
- Separate media layers: Harry Potter films, Fantastic Beasts films, *Harry Potter and the Cursed Child*, and *Hogwarts Legacy*
- Attribution mode: official links, bibliographic identification, and paraphrased claim summaries
- Verbatim source text: not stored
- Fan-wiki authority: none
- Commercial-text limitation: the seven novels, published screenplays, stage script, films, game narrative, companion books, and other paid works were not exhaustively re-read during this pass
- Confidence policy: high for explicit inspected public descriptions; medium for chronology or synthesis requiring selected works; unresolved for fine mechanics and absent public detail
