# Harry Potter Universe World Operating Model

## Purpose

This file converts researched setting propositions into causal operating rules for original play. It does not enumerate every spell, creature, lesson, artifact, family, or published event. Exact capabilities still require the selected source and, where relevant, a rules implementation.

## Core Causal Chain

1. Magic exists within and alongside non-magical society.
2. The International Statute of Secrecy makes concealment a shared legal and logistical obligation.
3. Concealment produces institutions, hidden places, regulated routes, specialized media, and unequal access.
4. Institutions depend on practitioners, records, labor, infrastructure, and public cooperation; they can be competent, biased, captured, or wrong.
5. Magic expands action but remains method-specific. Skill, materials, intent, access, counters, law, and consequences matter.
6. Family history, education, wealth, species or being-status, health, and institutional classification shape opportunity without determining worth.
7. Information travels unevenly through schools, ministries, newspapers, owls, portraits, rumor, and private records.
8. A local failure therefore becomes a social problem before it becomes a duel: somebody loses treatment, travel, livelihood, safety, standing, or voice.
9. Player choices alter custody, trust, access, evidence, and institutional response.
10. Consequences persist in relationships and services even when a spell repairs the visible damage.

Source claims: `claim-harry-potter-secrecy`, `claim-harry-potter-institutions-fallible`, `claim-harry-potter-transport`, `claim-harry-potter-press`, `claim-harry-potter-magic-not-universal-remedy`.

## Durable Truth Cards

### `harry-potter-truth-001` — Concealment Is Infrastructure

- **Assertion:** Magical secrecy is maintained through law, behavior, hidden access, travel systems, and institutional response rather than one universal veil.
- **Consequences:** A breach has witnesses, records, repair work, jurisdiction, and people affected by the cover response.
- **Limit:** Do not invent a perfect detection grid. Establish who noticed, by what channel, and with what authority.
- **Claims:** `claim-harry-potter-secrecy`, `claim-harry-potter-transport`.

### `harry-potter-truth-002` — Government Is Powerful And Fallible

- **Assertion:** Britain's Ministry governs magical affairs through departments, elections, regulation, enforcement, and contact with non-magical government, but its decisions reflect people and politics.
- **Consequences:** Procedure, patronage, evidence, public pressure, and institutional memory can matter as much as spell power.
- **Limit:** A Ministry logo is not a local actor. Instantiate an office, official, mandate, resources, and oversight route.
- **Claims:** `claim-harry-potter-ministry-government`, `claim-harry-potter-institutions-fallible`.

### `harry-potter-truth-003` — Law Has Distinct Functions

- **Assertion:** Enforcement, adjudication, legislation, imprisonment, and administration are related but not interchangeable.
- **Consequences:** Aurors do not automatically decide guilt; the Wizengamot's combined functions create both process and concentrated power; prison policy can change.
- **Limit:** Exact procedure, evidentiary standards, sentence, and appeal rules require era-specific research.
- **Claims:** `claim-harry-potter-wizengamot`, `claim-harry-potter-aurors`, `claim-harry-potter-azkaban-reform`.

### `harry-potter-truth-004` — Magic Is Method-Bound

- **Assertion:** Spells, potions, enchanted objects, creatures, plants, travel networks, and wandlore have different requirements and failure modes.
- **Consequences:** A proposed effect needs a known method or accepted invention, not the statement that magic can do anything.
- **Limit:** Do not infer exact mechanics from a game interface or a cinematic effect.
- **Claims:** `claim-harry-potter-potions-bounded`, `claim-harry-potter-wands-variable`.

### `harry-potter-truth-005` — Access Travels Through Systems

- **Assertion:** Floo connections, trains, concealed entrances, brooms, Apparition, Portkeys, owls, and other methods solve different movement or communication problems.
- **Consequences:** Permission, age, health, secrecy, destination knowledge, regulation, and route custody can create meaningful constraints.
- **Limit:** Do not treat every fireplace, person, or destination as universally connected.
- **Claims:** `claim-harry-potter-transport`, `claim-harry-potter-secrecy`.

### `harry-potter-truth-006` — Education Socializes As Well As Teaches

- **Assertion:** Hogwarts and other schools transmit magical skill, relationships, status, tradition, competition, and institutional assumptions.
- **Consequences:** Curriculum, mentorship, House networks, access needs, discipline, and student organization are campaign pressures.
- **Limit:** A House does not fix personality or morality. Exact staff and curriculum are era-specific.
- **Claims:** `claim-harry-potter-hogwarts-education`, `claim-harry-potter-international-schools`.

### `harry-potter-truth-007` — Care Has Capacity And Limits

- **Assertion:** Healers, school care, St Mungo's, potions, herbs, and charms address magical illness and injury, but magic does not erase every condition or consequence.
- **Consequences:** Triage, consent, supply, specialist knowledge, long-term care, and family access matter.
- **Limit:** Never declare a disability, identity, or trauma curable because magic exists.
- **Claims:** `claim-harry-potter-healing`, `claim-harry-potter-magic-not-universal-remedy`.

### `harry-potter-truth-008` — Economy Depends On Custody And Labor

- **Assertion:** Banks, shops, makers, couriers, household labor, healers, school suppliers, and transport workers sustain daily magical life.
- **Consequences:** Ownership customs, credit, keys, ingredients, repairs, and trusted access can generate conflict without a world-ending villain.
- **Limit:** Exact prices, wages, currency conversion, property law, and goblin contracts require sources.
- **Claims:** `claim-harry-potter-diagon-commerce`, `claim-harry-potter-gringotts`, `claim-harry-potter-daily-life`.

### `harry-potter-truth-009` — Media Shapes Public Reality

- **Assertion:** The Daily Prophet has wide reach but can sensationalize or be influenced by governing power; small publications and rumor provide partial alternatives.
- **Consequences:** Publication timing, evidence custody, retractions, access, and source protection are playable power.
- **Limit:** A newspaper report is an in-world assertion, not automatic objective truth.
- **Claims:** `claim-harry-potter-press`, `claim-harry-potter-institutions-fallible`.

### `harry-potter-truth-010` — Classification Distributes Access

- **Assertion:** Blood-status ideology, Squib exclusion, werewolf registration and stigma, and legal categories for beings shape employment, safety, credibility, and participation.
- **Consequences:** A form, registry, school rule, hiring decision, or service gate may embody conflict.
- **Limit:** Classification never determines a person's value, morality, or player capability.
- **Claims:** `claim-harry-potter-blood-ideology`, `claim-harry-potter-squib-access`, `claim-harry-potter-werewolf-stigma`.

### `harry-potter-truth-011` — Nonhuman Beings Have Agency

- **Assertion:** Goblins and house-elves have intelligence, relationships, customs, interests, and power under unequal arrangements with wizarding institutions.
- **Consequences:** Ownership, labor, freedom, consent, memory, and treaty questions require perspectives rather than monster labels.
- **Limit:** Do not invent a single species-wide opinion or make oppression decorative comedy.
- **Claims:** `claim-harry-potter-house-elf-bondage`, `claim-harry-potter-goblin-agency`.

### `harry-potter-truth-012` — Magical Society Is International

- **Assertion:** Britain is one jurisdiction within a wider world of schools, governments, travel, sport, and international coordination.
- **Consequences:** Delegations bring different laws, curricula, customs, languages, security duties, and interests.
- **Limit:** One British source cannot define another country's entire magical culture.
- **Claims:** `claim-harry-potter-international-schools`, `claim-harry-potter-quidditch-international`, `claim-harry-potter-fantastic-beasts-era`.

### `harry-potter-truth-013` — Era Changes What Is Possible

- **Assertion:** Government, transport, war status, public memory, named people, school conditions, and media presentation vary across centuries and continuities.
- **Consequences:** Every opening needs a date and source policy before capabilities are assumed.
- **Limit:** Do not use a later reform, game convenience, or stage outcome to solve an earlier problem.
- **Claims:** `claim-harry-potter-era-chronology`, `claim-harry-potter-technology-separation`.

### `harry-potter-truth-014` — Published Heroes Do Not Exhaust Agency

- **Assertion:** Institutions and communities contain students, families, clerks, healers, traders, journalists, makers, transport workers, athletes, caretakers, and organizers whose decisions matter.
- **Consequences:** Original PCs can change bounded lives and systems without replacing a published protagonist.
- **Limit:** Do not counterfeit undocumented canonical heroics; label original events.
- **Claims:** `claim-harry-potter-daily-life`, `claim-harry-potter-universe-scope`.

## Magical Action Contract

Before resolving an original magical action, establish:

1. **Purpose:** What concrete change is sought?
2. **Method:** Spell, potion, object, creature relationship, plant, ritual, route, research, or ordinary action?
3. **Capability:** Who knows how, and from what accepted source or campaign invention?
4. **Access:** Wand, ingredients, time, location, permission, target, record, or helper needed?
5. **Resistance:** Countermagic, material constraint, consent, law, secrecy, distance, or uncertainty?
6. **Evidence:** What trace, witness, record, symptom, or changed object remains?
7. **Consequence:** Who gains or loses access, trust, safety, custody, or time?
8. **Limit:** What is not solved even on success?

If an exact mechanic is unresolved, adjudicate only the fictional position or pause for targeted research. Do not invent a permanent universal law from one dramatic ruling.

## Local Operating Models

### Secrecy Incident

`exposure or anomaly → witness and trace → local containment → Ministry or community jurisdiction → explanation/repair → trust and memory consequences`

A successful concealment can still harm a witness, erase evidence, delay care, or create resentment. Use `harry-potter-truth-001` and `harry-potter-truth-002`.

### Service Failure

`dependency → missed handoff → unequal impact → competing diagnosis → temporary workaround → custody dispute → durable repair or exclusion`

Services include Floo access, owl delivery, potion supply, school accommodation, healer referral, bank access, or market licensing. Use `harry-potter-truth-005`, `harry-potter-truth-007`, and `harry-potter-truth-008`.

### Institutional Investigation

`allegation → evidence custody → authority claim → witness risk → public narrative → hearing or decision → accountability gap`

Violence cannot substitute for proof. Use `harry-potter-truth-003` and `harry-potter-truth-009`.

### School Pressure

`learning need → rule or tradition → unequal access → peer/faculty response → experiment or organizing → consequence → revised practice or entrenched harm`

Adults retain safeguarding responsibility. Use `harry-potter-truth-006` and `harry-potter-truth-010`.

### Rights And Recognition

`classification → denied voice or resource → testimony and coalition → institutional defense → negotiation or resistance → material remedy → backlash or precedent`

The affected people define their goals; PCs do not rescue a passive category. Use `harry-potter-truth-010` and `harry-potter-truth-011`.

### International Coordination

`local rule → cross-border movement → incompatible expectations → translation and jurisdiction → shared threat or opportunity → negotiated protocol → precedent`

Difference must be researched, not exoticized. Use `harry-potter-truth-012` and `harry-potter-truth-013`.

## Scale Contract

The default opening contains:

- one physical theater
- no more than one connection layer
- three to five PCs
- three or four active power centers
- six to eight named opening faces
- one service, route, institution, or public need under pressure
- exactly two shared, player-visible clocks and one separately protected truth
- no more than two named canonical figures, preferably zero

Expand only when player action creates a durable connection. Hogwarts, the Ministry, Gringotts, St Mungo's, and international bodies do not all become active merely because they exist.

## Conflict Contract

Every consequential conflict must identify:

- what each side needs rather than only whom it opposes
- authority and practical capability
- dependency and limit
- people affected but not represented
- evidence and knowledge asymmetry
- nonviolent, procedural, relational, and magical action paths
- what refusal preserves or costs
- what violence cannot repair

No faction is omniscient. No spell makes consent irrelevant. No institutional title guarantees truth.

## Clock Contract

The default `harry-potter-frame-mended-network` uses exactly two package-original, player-visible shared clocks. Actions by all four opening powers feed these clocks; the powers receive no separate clocks.

### `harry-potter-clock-route-failure` — Route Failure

- **Owner:** the shared service condition; the GM advances it only when fictional evidence shows deterioration
- **Visibility:** player-visible from the opening
- **Four segments:** repeated delay documented → critical referral or delivery missed → public misroute or fallback overload → closure or uncontrolled exposure becomes imminent
- **Evidence:** failed travel tests, delayed goods, witness accounts, clinic records, or a documented public incident
- **Affected people:** patients, children, elders, workers, traders, residents with altered records, and route staff
- **Reversibility:** early delays are reversible; later trust, spoiled supply, or exposure requires additional repair
- **Redirect paths:** manual escorts, fallback hearths, consent-based remapping, distributed supply, or a negotiated service pause

### `harry-potter-clock-access-lockdown` — Access Lockdown

- **Owner:** the bounded local Ministry office
- **Visibility:** player-visible as an announced review from the opening
- **Four segments:** extra verification imposed → destinations restricted → records or equipment placed under official control → hearth closure or compelled disclosure is authorized
- **Evidence:** written notice, staff arrival, changed permission, seized record, hearing date, or executed order
- **Affected people:** residents without clean records, service staff, traders, patients, witnesses, and the office's own dissenters
- **Reversibility:** administrative steps can be appealed or redirected; disclosure and destroyed trust cannot be silently undone
- **Redirect paths:** verified pilot, independent audit, emergency access agreement, lawful challenge, public scrutiny, or institutional amnesty

The inaction trajectory in the selected frame maps events to these segments. A segment advances only when its owner or condition has means, information, and opportunity; it never advances solely for dramatic timing. A custom frame may instead use no more than four bounded power clocks, but it must not duplicate these shared clocks.

## Ordinary Agency Test

Before introducing a famous person or unique artifact, ask:

1. Can a local worker, student, patient, trader, clerk, caretaker, journalist, or neighbor hold the relevant knowledge?
2. Can the institution act through a bounded office rather than its most famous leader?
3. Can the crisis be solved through custody, trust, labor, evidence, or coalition rather than exceptional destiny?
4. Does the PC group have a reason to matter that survives the removal of every named protagonist?

If not, reduce the premise or explicitly select a high-canon frame.
