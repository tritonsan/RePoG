# Star Wars Universe World Operating Model

## Purpose

This file converts researched setting propositions into causal rules for original play. It does not enumerate every planet, species, ship, weapon, Force ability, military unit, or published event. Exact capability and chronology still require the selected source and rules implementation.

## Core Causal Chain

1. A large, inhabited galaxy contains many worlds, systems, cultures, governments, and unequal centers of power.
2. Hyperspace makes interstellar connection possible but depends on equipment, fuel, navigation, routes, labor, and local access.
3. Political regimes use those connections to represent, tax, supply, police, occupy, resist, or abandon communities.
4. Regimes change across eras; institutions inherit records, weapons, debts, routes, personnel, and damage from predecessors.
5. The Force connects life but remains mysterious, unevenly perceived, and interpreted through different traditions.
6. Ships, droids, medicine, communications, credits, and weapons expand action without removing maintenance, scarcity, jurisdiction, consent, or consequence.
7. Species, genetics, chassis, culture, and homeworld shape context without fixing identity or morality.
8. Information moves unevenly through sensors, witnesses, couriers, comlinks, institutions, propaganda, and rumor.
9. A local route or service failure therefore affects food, medicine, work, refuge, representation, and safety before it becomes a fleet battle.
10. Player choices change custody, visibility, trust, access, logistics, and political legitimacy; consequences persist after the blaster fire ends.

Source claims: `claim-star-wars-hyperspace`, `claim-star-wars-force`, `claim-star-wars-regions`, `claim-star-wars-comms`, `claim-star-wars-economy`.

## Durable Truth Cards

### `star-wars-truth-001` — Distance Has Infrastructure

- **Assertion:** Hyperspace travel requires a ship or compatible system, functioning hyperdrive, fuel, navigation information, and a viable path.
- **Consequences:** Damaged drives, stale coordinates, blockades, route custody, fuel, and maintenance create material pressure.
- **Limit:** Never infer fixed travel time or universal lane access without a selected source.
- **Claims:** `claim-star-wars-hyperspace`, `claim-star-wars-starships`.

### `star-wars-truth-002` — Geography Is Not Destiny

- **Assertion:** Core, Mid Rim, Outer Rim, Unknown Regions, planets, and systems orient space; they do not define one culture, economy, legality, or moral type.
- **Consequences:** Every theater needs local government, livelihoods, languages, infrastructure, and history.
- **Limit:** “Outer Rim” is not shorthand for lawless, poor, rustic, or disposable.
- **Claims:** `claim-star-wars-regions`, `claim-star-wars-coruscant`, `claim-star-wars-ferrix`.

### `star-wars-truth-003` — Regimes Are Historical

- **Assertion:** Republic, Empire, rebellion, New Republic, First Order, and Resistance roles change with era and are not simultaneous equivalents.
- **Consequences:** Law, legitimacy, armed reach, public knowledge, records, and local cooperation must be dated.
- **Limit:** A familiar insignia does not define the exact office, unit, mandate, or capacity present.
- **Claims:** `claim-star-wars-republic`, `claim-star-wars-empire`, `claim-star-wars-rebellion`, `claim-star-wars-new-republic`, `claim-star-wars-first-order`, `claim-star-wars-resistance`.

### `star-wars-truth-004` — Institutions Depend On People

- **Assertion:** Senates, militaries, councils, cells, guilds, and relief networks act through bounded people, records, resources, and relationships.
- **Consequences:** Procedure, staffing, dissent, logistics, evidence, and public trust matter.
- **Limit:** No organization is omniscient or perfectly unified.
- **Claims:** `claim-star-wars-senate`, `claim-star-wars-rebellion`, `claim-star-wars-new-republic`.

### `star-wars-truth-005` — The Force Is Plural And Mysterious

- **Assertion:** The Force is life-connected, while sensitivity, belief, training, interpretation, and institutional membership remain distinct.
- **Consequences:** Jedi, Sith, Nightsisters, believers, unaffiliated sensitives, and skeptics may relate to it differently.
- **Limit:** Do not invent one universal power list or call every dark-side user Sith.
- **Claims:** `claim-star-wars-force`, `claim-star-wars-jedi`, `claim-star-wars-sith`, `claim-star-wars-force-traditions`.

### `star-wars-truth-006` — Destiny Does Not Erase Consent

- **Assertion:** Visions, prophecies, lineages, artifacts, or Force phenomena never override player authority or ordinary causal evidence.
- **Consequences:** A character may question, refuse, reinterpret, conceal, or leave a tradition.
- **Limit:** No surprise famous ancestry, chosen-one status, morality test, or forced spiritual conversion.
- **Claims:** `claim-star-wars-force`, `claim-star-wars-anti-essentialism`.

### `star-wars-truth-007` — Machines Need Labor And Care

- **Assertion:** Starships, hyperdrives, droids, sensors, weapons, and communication systems require operators, parts, power, knowledge, and repair.
- **Consequences:** Mechanics, astromechs, suppliers, salvagers, pilots, and maintainers hold consequential agency.
- **Limit:** Cinematic reliability or a game cooldown is not a universal engineering rule.
- **Claims:** `claim-star-wars-starships`, `claim-star-wars-droids`, `claim-star-wars-comms`.

### `star-wars-truth-008` — Droid Status Is Local And Contested

- **Assertion:** Droids have specialized capabilities and may hold knowledge and relationships, while universal consciousness, ownership, emancipation, and memory law are unresolved.
- **Consequences:** Chassis, programming, memory, maintenance, autonomy, and legal recognition become explicit local questions.
- **Limit:** Never treat a speaking droid as inventory by default or declare all droids identical persons by unsupported universal rule.
- **Claims:** `claim-star-wars-droids`.

### `star-wars-truth-009` — Origin Does Not Fix Identity

- **Assertion:** Species, homeworld, culture, clone genetics, droid chassis, and military manufacture do not determine morality, profession, politics, voice, or destiny.
- **Consequences:** Individuals and communities contain disagreement, change, mixed belonging, and self-definition.
- **Limit:** Avoid species-wide traits beyond sourced anatomy or environment; even those require character consent.
- **Claims:** `claim-star-wars-clones`, `claim-star-wars-mandalorians`, `claim-star-wars-anti-essentialism`.

### `star-wars-truth-010` — Economy Is Local

- **Assertion:** Credits, barter, rations, cargo, salvage, repair, debt, and black markets vary by regime and place.
- **Consequences:** Acceptance, custody, transport, labor, trust, and need matter more than a universal price list.
- **Limit:** Do not invent galaxy-wide wages, taxes, exchange rates, or scarcity from one example.
- **Claims:** `claim-star-wars-economy`, `claim-star-wars-ferrix`.

### `star-wars-truth-011` — Law Follows Jurisdiction And Power

- **Assertion:** Senates, governors, councils, militaries, guilds, syndicates, and local communities claim different authority.
- **Consequences:** A bounty, permit, seizure, arrest, blockade, or salvage claim needs a grant, jurisdiction, enforcement path, and challenge route.
- **Limit:** Guild membership or a uniform is not universal lawful authority.
- **Claims:** `claim-star-wars-senate`, `claim-star-wars-bounty`, `claim-star-wars-syndicates`.

### `star-wars-truth-012` — War Reorders Ordinary Life

- **Assertion:** War and occupation alter routes, records, labor, food, medicine, family, housing, trust, and public memory.
- **Consequences:** Refugees, veterans, civilians, clones, droids, occupied people, and former fighters need more than victory scenes.
- **Limit:** Planetary destruction, forced labor, torture, and child soldiers require explicit safety scope.
- **Claims:** `claim-star-wars-empire`, `claim-star-wars-clones`, `claim-star-wars-medicine`, `claim-star-wars-refugees`, `claim-star-wars-syndicates`.

### `star-wars-truth-013` — Information Is Uneven

- **Assertion:** Comlinks, records, sensors, testimony, propaganda, couriers, and rumor carry partial knowledge with different access and reliability.
- **Consequences:** Authentication does not prove interpretation; intercepted messages do not create universal awareness.
- **Limit:** Do not assume an instant, free, uncensored, galaxy-wide HoloNet.
- **Claims:** `claim-star-wars-comms`, `claim-star-wars-empire`, `claim-star-wars-resistance`.

### `star-wars-truth-014` — Published Heroes Do Not Exhaust Agency

- **Assertion:** Mechanics, pilots, medics, clerks, farmers, traders, diplomats, droids, clones, organizers, scouts, and neighbors can change bounded lives and systems.
- **Consequences:** Original PCs matter through responsibility, trust, evidence, repair, and coalition rather than inherited fame.
- **Limit:** Do not counterfeit undocumented Canon heroics or transfer a published victory without divergence.
- **Claims:** `claim-star-wars-franchise-scope`, `claim-star-wars-ferrix`, `claim-star-wars-refugees`.

## Action Contract

Before resolving a consequential action, establish:

1. **Purpose:** What concrete change is sought?
2. **Method:** Technical, social, political, physical, military, criminal, medical, exploratory, or Force-related?
3. **Capability:** Who knows how, from what selected source, role, training, or accepted invention?
4. **Access:** Ship, part, fuel, weapon, medicine, record, code, permission, ally, route, or time required?
5. **Jurisdiction and consent:** Who may authorize, refuse, be searched, be exposed, or be harmed?
6. **Resistance:** Distance, defense, countermeasure, uncertainty, law, trauma, environment, or competing interest?
7. **Evidence:** What trace, witness, record, sensor return, damage, debt, or changed relationship remains?
8. **Limit:** What is not solved even on success?

If an exact mechanic is unresolved, adjudicate only the established fictional position or pause for targeted research.

## Local Operating Models

### Route Failure

`dependency → stale or manipulated navigation → missed transit → unequal harm → emergency workaround → route-custody conflict → repair, isolation, or exposure`

Use `star-wars-truth-001`, `star-wars-truth-007`, and `star-wars-truth-013`.

### Political Transition

`old authority collapses → records and armed assets persist → new claim seeks legitimacy → local interests test capacity → service or security gap → negotiated rule or renewed coercion`

Use `star-wars-truth-003`, `star-wars-truth-004`, and `star-wars-truth-011`.

### Salvage And Supply

`damaged asset → competing custody claims → labor and hazard → recovered value → unequal distribution → repair, sale, seizure, or community use`

Use `star-wars-truth-007`, `star-wars-truth-010`, and `star-wars-truth-011`.

### Occupation And Resistance

`coercive demand → compliance pressure → hidden survival network → retaliation risk → contested tactic → civilian consequence → liberation, compromise, or deeper control`

Use `star-wars-truth-003`, `star-wars-truth-012`, and `star-wars-truth-013`.

### Care And Refuge

`displacement or injury → capacity limit → identity or route barrier → triage and consent → supply decision → recovery, exclusion, or relocation`

Use `star-wars-truth-008`, `star-wars-truth-009`, and `star-wars-truth-012`.

### Force Encounter

`unusual perception or event → interpretation through belief/tradition → evidence and doubt → invitation or coercion → chosen response → relational and ethical consequence`

Use `star-wars-truth-005` and `star-wars-truth-006`.

## Scale Contract

The default opening contains:

- one physical theater
- at most one connection layer
- three to five PCs
- three systems or fewer initially visible
- three or four active powers
- six to eight original opening faces
- one route, service, settlement need, or public responsibility under pressure
- exactly two shared player-visible four-segment clocks
- one separately protected truth
- no faction clocks
- no more than two named canonical figures, preferably zero

Expand only when player action creates a durable connection.

## Conflict Contract

Every conflict identifies:

- each side's need and constituency
- authority, practical means, and jurisdiction
- logistical dependency and limit
- affected people not represented
- evidence and knowledge asymmetry
- technical, social, procedural, relational, defensive, and violent paths
- what refusal preserves or costs
- what violence cannot repair

No faction is omniscient. No Force ability makes consent irrelevant. No weapon resolves legitimacy.

## Default Clock Contract

`star-wars-frame-broken-beacon` uses exactly two package-original shared clocks. Both are player-visible from the opening. Actions by all four opening powers feed these clocks; no opening power receives a separate faction clock.

### `star-wars-clock-corridor-isolation` — Corridor Isolation

- **Owner:** shared route condition; the GM advances only on evidence of material deterioration
- **Visibility:** player-visible
- **Four segments:** delivery delay documented → medical or food transit missed → one settlement loses reliable passage → corridor closure or dangerous unsurveyed travel becomes imminent
- **Evidence:** nav logs, beacon telemetry, failed arrivals, fuel loss, damaged cargo, patient records, or witness reports
- **Affected people:** residents, refugees, crews, patients, traders, droids, maintainers, and isolated settlements
- **Reversibility:** early delays can be repaired; exposure, loss of life, route abandonment, or destroyed trust need further remedy
- **Redirect paths:** escorted passage, local nav sharing, beacon repair, alternate supply, consent-based remapping, or negotiated pause

### `star-wars-clock-emergency-jurisdiction` — Emergency Jurisdiction

- **Type:** shared situation clock; not a faction clock
- **Owner:** the corridor-wide emergency-governance condition and public review state
- **Current advancing actor:** the bounded New Republic reconstruction mission administers the review, while evidence, demands, resistance, and alternatives from all four opening powers can advance, pause, or redirect it
- **Visibility:** player-visible as an announced emergency review
- **Four segments:** ad hoc verification disrupts access → contested authority restricts routes → records or equipment are consolidated under emergency control → compulsory mapping, seizure, or armed closure is authorized
- **Evidence:** public notice, inspection, permit denial, council demand, failed safeguard, record request, patrol deployment, seizure order, or hearing date
- **Affected people:** undocumented settlements, refugees, traders, crews, local officials, droids, aid users, and mission dissenters
- **Reversibility:** procedure can be challenged; exposed refuge routes and coerced legitimacy cannot be silently undone
- **Redirect paths:** independent audit, protected registry, temporary corridor charter, amnesty, civilian oversight, lawful challenge, mutual-defense agreement, or withdrawal of emergency authority

The inaction trajectory in the selected frame maps evidence to these segments. A clock advances only when its owner or condition has means, information, and opportunity, never solely for pacing.

## Ordinary Agency Test

Before introducing a famous person, superweapon, unique artifact, or fleet:

1. Can a local pilot, mechanic, medic, courier, droid, clone, trader, official, refugee, or neighbor hold the relevant knowledge?
2. Can a government or movement act through one bounded office, unit, or relationship?
3. Can custody, labor, evidence, trust, navigation, or coalition resolve the core need?
4. Does the group still matter if every named protagonist is removed?

If not, reduce the premise or explicitly select a high-Canon frame.
