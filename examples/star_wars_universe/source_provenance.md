# Star Wars Universe Source Provenance

## Research Snapshot

- Retrieval cutoff: 2026-07-23
- Universe focus: the official Star Wars fictional universe across eras and media, without reducing it to one trilogy, series, game, family, or protagonist
- Default continuity: current Star Wars Canon
- Separate layers: Legends; selected *Star Wars: Visions* works; selected LEGO alternate works; promotional/crossover works; fixed game narrative; player-variable outcomes; gameplay and presentation
- Attribution mode: official public links and paraphrased claim summaries
- Verbatim source text: not stored
- Fan-wiki authority: none
- Commercial-work limitation: films, series, novels, comics, games, and other paid works were not exhaustively replayed or reread during this pass
- Confidence policy: high for explicit inspected official descriptions; medium for official-policy synthesis or details requiring a selected work; unresolved for unsupported universal mechanics and daily-life rules
