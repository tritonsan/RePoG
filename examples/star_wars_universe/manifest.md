## What This Package Is

This is a researched setting package for the official Star Wars fictional universe across its eras and media. It is not generic space opera, a Skywalker Saga replay, an Original Trilogy campaign, a Clone Wars unit simulator, a Mandalorian conversion, a Jedi-only game, a licensed-game rules port, or an attempt to merge every published work into one timeline.

The package distinguishes:

- current Star Wars Canon
- the pre-2014 Expanded Universe retained as Legends
- the nine official qualitative eras
- selected *Star Wars: Visions* works as alternate or continuity-independent anthology layers
- selected LEGO alternate works
- promotional and crossover works
- fixed narrative in a selected game
- player-variable game outcomes
- gameplay, interface, and presentation abstractions
- package-original and campaign-divergent content

A Canon work may reintroduce a name or concept associated with Legends. That makes only the newly established Canon proposition authoritative in Canon; it does not import the older work's complete biography, chronology, powers, or outcome. Source claims: `claim-star-wars-franchise-scope`, `claim-star-wars-canon-legends`, `claim-star-wars-eras`.

## Player Promise

You play original people whose decisions matter within a vast, uneven galaxy: pilots, mechanics, traders, medics, diplomats, scouts, investigators, defenders, refugees, droids, clones, mystics, organizers, couriers, salvagers, or neighbors. Starships and the Force can expand what people attempt, but they do not erase distance, maintenance, evidence, consent, logistics, law, trauma, political power, or consequence.

The proposed default begins during `star-wars-era-new-republic`, after Imperial defeat has opened political space but before reconstruction has made every route safe or every remnant harmless. One original frontier corridor links a few communities to medicine, repair, food, trade, and representation. Its navigation beacons are failing selectively. A three-to-five-person crew with a practical service mandate must keep people connected while deciding who controls maps, whose settlements become visible, and what legitimate government owes communities that survived by hiding.

The corridor, communities, exact interval, crew, beacon failure, active powers, and hidden cause are RePoG-original proposals. They are not events from *The Mandalorian*, *Ahsoka*, a novel, comic, or game.

## Selection Locks

Nothing becomes active merely because this directory exists. Session 0 must affirmatively accept the package, continuity, era, media policy, group fantasy, theater, frame, rules posture, and safeguards. Unselected proposals remain inert.

- The setting is the Star Wars fictional universe, not a generic galaxy with familiar names pasted onto it.
- Canon and Legends do not form one frictionless history.
- One continuity policy, one chronology policy, and one spoiler ceiling govern the opening; one official era is required only when the chronology policy maps to it.
- Era, continuity, medium, selected-work scope, and gameplay status are separate metadata.
- Exact BBY/ABY dates require selected-work support; the default imposes none.
- The default group has three to five original characters, a practical shared responsibility, and meaningful refusal and exit rights.
- Force sensitivity, Jedi or Sith affiliation, prophecy, famous ancestry, and unique destiny are never assigned by default.
- Published heroes, villains, leaders, and legacy families remain history, reports, distant actors, or absent unless proximity is accepted.
- Film composition, editing, sound, production design, animation style, LEGO logic, game HUDs, inventory, respawn, fast travel, map compression, progression, encounter density, and player-variable outcomes are not automatic setting physics.
- Hyperspace requires equipment, navigation, resources, and risk; it is not consequence-free teleportation.
- A region label describes geography, not culture, wealth, morality, legality, or technological level.
- Species, genetics, homeworld, clone origin, droid chassis, Force sensitivity, and culture do not determine personality, worth, profession, allegiance, or morality.
- Universal droid personhood, ownership, memory, bounty, refugee, medical, currency, wage, housing, or HoloNet rules are not invented from silence.
- Player identity, species, culture, language, body, disability, cybernetics, droid or clone status, Force relationship, beliefs, history, ties, trauma, private facts, and boundaries remain player-authored.
- Slavery, forced labor, occupation, genocide, planetary destruction, child soldiers, memory wipes, torture, mind influence, religious coercion, war trauma, and harm to refugees are explicit opt-in themes with handling agreements.
- Accepted campaign history outranks later package updates.

## Four Semantic Layers

### Template-Fixed

Once selected, fictional invariants preserve continuity and chronology labels, plural Force traditions, changing political regimes, costly distance and infrastructure, local rather than universal law/economy, ordinary people's agency, anti-essentialism, and media/mechanics separation. A campaign may change a fictional invariant only through a recorded divergence.

### Campaign-Instance

Exact date, system, worlds, route map, polity, law, economy, settlement, ship, crew mandate, rules implementation, mechanically available capabilities, Force access, factions, faces, clocks, hidden truth, famous-character proximity, and consequences.

### Player-Authored

Name, pronouns, identity, species, culture, language, home, body, disability, cybernetics, clone or droid relationship, Force sensitivity or belief, tradition, profession concept, desired competency direction, history, ties, private knowledge, fears, ambitions, trauma, and boundaries. Rules-compatible implementation of capability is shared campaign work and cannot override the player's character concept or veto.

### Era/Media-Specific

The chronology policy; official era when applicable; selected-work chronology when not; Canon or Legends; selected film, series, prose, comic, game, anthology, LEGO, or crossover work; fixed narrative; branching outcome; presentation; and mechanics. These fields never silently collapse.

## Cross-Cutting Process Safeguards

Affirmative selection, player authority over authored and private fields, GM/player knowledge separation, consent, accessibility, and safety boundaries apply across all four layers. They are not fictional propositions and cannot be waived by an in-world divergence. Only an affected participant may grant narrow, explicit permission over their own field.

## Default Selection Rationale

[Session 0 Options](session_zero_options.md) compares exactly sixteen player fantasies. The frontier community crew is the recommended onboarding default because it:

- supports civilians, veterans, droids, clones, Force believers, nonbelievers, and varied professions without requiring any one identity
- connects aid, trade, navigation, repair, investigation, diplomacy, and local defense
- uses the New Republic's documented reconstruction/remnant pressure without copying a series premise
- keeps a few settlements and one route legible
- lets Imperial, Republic, underworld, and local power matter without making any logo omniscient
- creates action without making combat or Force power the only meaningful contribution
- permits restorative, procedural, social, technical, exploratory, and defensive solutions
- leaves exact date and named-canon proximity open for source-aware Session 0 selection

This ranking is package-original design judgment, not an official franchise preference.

## Compatibility Contract

A future campaign must explicitly record:

- selected continuity, chronology policy, official era when applicable, date policy, media, works, and spoiler ceiling
- whether Legends, Visions, LEGO, crossover, or game material is excluded or selected by work
- one physical theater and at most one connection layer
- group fantasy, mandate, size, authority, refusal, and exit rights
- rules source and fiction-versus-mechanics policy
- Force sensitivity and tradition posture
- famous-character and famous-lineage proximity
- droid, clone, species, culture, war, occupation, slavery, coercion, and minor safeguards
- approval to materialize a pruned subset

A future materializer must not copy every era, planet, faction, frame, secret, power, technology, or character into active memory.

## Authority And Isolation

This directory contains Markdown reference material only. It contains no active world state, profile, snapshot, turn log, accepted boundary, runtime instruction, schema, registry entry, loader, resolver, or campaign command.

It does not override `AGENTS.md`, workflows, profiles, or any file under `campaign/`. The only active campaign root remains `campaign/`. Package existence never implies user selection or readiness.
