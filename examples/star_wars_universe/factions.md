# Star Wars Universe Factions

## Use Contract

These are power models, not instructions to instantiate every galactic organization. Select three or four opening powers. Give each a local face, mandate, constituency, capability, dependency, internal disagreement, information gap, and next action. A Republic, Empire, alliance, order, guild, syndicate, or culture without a bounded office, unit, cell, chapter, crew, or relationship remains background.

## Power Cards

### `star-wars-faction-reconstruction-mission` — Bounded New Republic Mission

- **Instantiate as:** one civilian route office, survey team, relief liaison, registry mission, or intersystem working group
- **Capabilities:** recognize local delegates, issue limited permits, coordinate aid, audit records, request patrol support, and propose charters
- **Dependencies:** accurate information, local cooperation, ships, funding, political cover, and trust in the New Republic
- **Limits:** thin staffing, demilitarization, remnant threats, inherited records, jurisdiction, and distant oversight
- **Internal disagreement:** standardize quickly to restore service versus negotiate slowly to avoid reproducing Imperial control
- **PC leverage:** local evidence, route expertise, public testimony, a workable pilot, independent oversight, or lawful challenge
- **Claims:** `claim-star-wars-new-republic`, `claim-star-wars-senate`.

### `star-wars-faction-authoritarian-command` — Imperial Or Successor Command

- **Instantiate as:** one garrison, remnant flotilla, security bureau, governor's office, or First Order unit appropriate to era
- **Capabilities:** armed coercion, surveillance, seizure, permits, detention, propaganda, and control of infrastructure
- **Dependencies:** supply, intelligence, discipline, technicians, compliant intermediaries, and fear
- **Limits:** contested legitimacy, internal rivalry, maintenance, desertion, local ignorance, and finite force
- **Internal disagreement:** preserve personnel through pragmatic retreat versus restore domination through exemplary violence
- **PC leverage:** logistics, dissenters, evidence, evacuation, misinformation with consequences, coalition, or negotiated surrender
- **Claims:** `claim-star-wars-empire`, `claim-star-wars-first-order`, `claim-star-wars-stormtroopers`.

### `star-wars-faction-rebel-cell` — Local Rebel Or Resistance Cell

- **Instantiate as:** three to twelve people with one territory, target, support network, and command relationship
- **Capabilities:** observation, sabotage, evacuation, courier work, recruitment, and coordination with a wider movement when sourced
- **Dependencies:** secrecy, civilians, safe houses, transport, intelligence, and moral legitimacy
- **Limits:** scarcity, compartmentalization, trauma, reprisal risk, tactical disagreement, and incomplete strategic knowledge
- **Internal disagreement:** strike now to disrupt coercion versus protect people and build durable capacity
- **PC leverage:** trustworthy intelligence, transport, technical skill, mediation, civilian accountability, or an alternative tactic
- **Claims:** `claim-star-wars-rebellion`, `claim-star-wars-resistance`.

### `star-wars-faction-local-council` — Settlement Or Corridor Council

- **Instantiate as:** elected council, rotating assembly, clan compact, worker forum, village leadership, or original mixed body
- **Capabilities:** allocate local services, represent residents, maintain norms, negotiate access, and mobilize labor
- **Dependencies:** participation, legitimacy, communication, revenue or shared resources, and recognition by neighbors
- **Limits:** factional disagreement, exclusion, thin capacity, old grievances, and outside coercion
- **Internal disagreement:** protect autonomy and hidden practices versus seek recognition, trade, and aid
- **PC leverage:** representation, facilitation, evidence, service, translation, or a consent-based agreement
- **Claims:** `claim-star-wars-regions`, `claim-star-wars-refugees`, `claim-star-wars-anti-essentialism`.

### `star-wars-faction-patrol` — Bounded Defense Or Patrol Unit

- **Instantiate as:** one local defense flight, convoy escort, Republic patrol pair, watch crew, or era-appropriate military detachment
- **Capabilities:** escort, deterrence, search and rescue, inspection within mandate, reconnaissance, and limited force
- **Dependencies:** fuel, ships, parts, crews, intelligence, maintenance, public cooperation, and legitimate rules of engagement
- **Limits:** response time, jurisdiction, escalation, civilian risk, fatigue, and political oversight
- **Internal disagreement:** visible security deters attack versus militarization reproduces occupation
- **PC leverage:** route knowledge, repair, witness trust, oversight, de-escalation, or proof of a mistaken threat model
- **Claims:** `claim-star-wars-starships`, `claim-star-wars-new-republic`, `claim-star-wars-stormtroopers`.

### `star-wars-faction-freight-cooperative` — Freighters And Route Workers

- **Instantiate as:** three ships, one dock crew, a convoy association, courier line, or mixed transport cooperative
- **Capabilities:** move cargo and passengers, maintain schedules, share navigation, extend credit, and improvise routes
- **Dependencies:** fuel, hyperdrives, nav data, ports, repairs, crews, cargo, and accepted payment
- **Limits:** capacity, breakdown, customs, piracy, debt, weather or space hazards, and unequal route access
- **Internal disagreement:** preserve universal minimum service versus prioritize contracts that keep the fleet solvent
- **PC leverage:** crew labor, navigation, mediation, repair, cargo verification, or cooperative governance
- **Claims:** `claim-star-wars-hyperspace`, `claim-star-wars-starships`, `claim-star-wars-economy`.

### `star-wars-faction-salvage-network` — Salvagers, Mechanics, And Yards

- **Instantiate as:** one yard cluster, repair guild, wreck-recovery team, neighborhood shop network, or worker cooperative
- **Capabilities:** assess damage, recover parts, repair systems, preserve technical memory, and convert wreckage into local capacity
- **Dependencies:** safe access, tools, droids, skilled labor, storage, custody agreements, and buyers or users
- **Limits:** hazardous sites, contamination, competing claims, scarce parts, extraction pressure, and thin margins
- **Internal disagreement:** reserve recovered systems for community repair versus sell high-value parts to fund survival
- **PC leverage:** provenance, technical skill, safety practice, fair allocation, alternative supply, or defense of worker control
- **Claims:** `claim-star-wars-ferrix`, `claim-star-wars-droids`, `claim-star-wars-economy`.

### `star-wars-faction-aid-network` — Medical And Refuge Network

- **Instantiate as:** one clinic, mobile medical ship, refugee host network, food cooperative, evacuation team, or rebuilding council
- **Capabilities:** triage, stabilize, shelter, document needs, coordinate passage, distribute supplies, and protect confidentiality
- **Dependencies:** medicine, food, transport, trained people, hosts, language access, power, and consent
- **Limits:** capacity, exposure, burnout, uncertain law, supply interruptions, and unequal political support
- **Internal disagreement:** prioritize immediate life-saving movement versus invest in durable local belonging and autonomy
- **PC leverage:** transport, medical skill, privacy protection, supply repair, advocacy, or a host-community agreement
- **Claims:** `claim-star-wars-medicine`, `claim-star-wars-refugees`, `claim-star-wars-economy`.

### `star-wars-faction-droid-network` — Droid Maintenance And Mutual-Aid Network

- **Instantiate as:** one repair bay, encrypted memory circle, shipboard droid group, emancipation network, or mixed-organic maintenance collective
- **Capabilities:** machine access, diagnostics, stored experience, technical labor, communication, and mutual repair
- **Dependencies:** parts, power, safe backups, trusted maintainers, physical access, and locally negotiated status
- **Limits:** incompatible chassis, restraining devices, memory wipes, ownership claims, prejudice, and uncertain law
- **Internal disagreement:** remain hidden to preserve memories versus seek public recognition and risk seizure
- **PC leverage:** consent-respecting repair, legal or social advocacy, secure storage, access, or recognition of individual preferences
- **Claims:** `claim-star-wars-droids`, `claim-star-wars-comms`, `claim-star-wars-anti-essentialism`.

### `star-wars-faction-bounty-guild` — Brokered Bounty Network

- **Instantiate as:** one broker, three hunters, one client pool, and one local contract custom
- **Capabilities:** match contracts, verify limited credentials, share leads, hold payment, and enforce reputation
- **Dependencies:** credible targets, payment, information, local access, and participants who honor terms
- **Limits:** jurisdiction, false claims, collateral harm, rival brokers, client deception, and community resistance
- **Internal disagreement:** complete any accepted contract versus require evidence, limits, and lawful standing
- **PC leverage:** investigate the claim, negotiate surrender, expose a client, protect a target, or rewrite contract terms
- **Claims:** `claim-star-wars-bounty`, `claim-star-wars-syndicates`.

### `star-wars-faction-syndicate` — Bounded Criminal Organization

- **Instantiate as:** one crew, refinery office, route broker, protection network, mining interest, or coalition representative
- **Capabilities:** capital, illicit transport, coercion, bribery, intelligence, armed labor, and access to prohibited goods
- **Dependencies:** profit, secrecy, workers, fear, political cover, customers, and reliable routes
- **Limits:** rival interests, unstable coalitions, resistance, internal ambition, supply, and exposure
- **Internal disagreement:** preserve predictable extraction and local deals versus seize control through escalating violence
- **PC leverage:** disrupt dependency, organize workers, expose accounts, build alternative supply, negotiate passage, or split a coalition
- **Claims:** `claim-star-wars-syndicates`, `claim-star-wars-economy`.

### `star-wars-faction-force-community` — Force Tradition Or Stewardship Circle

- **Instantiate as:** one teacher-student lineage, shrine community, archive, pilgrim network, unaffiliated sensitive circle, or selected sourced tradition
- **Capabilities:** disciplined practice, interpretation, mediation, site knowledge, community memory, and limited sourced Force abilities
- **Dependencies:** trust, continuity, teachers, safe practice, site or archive access, and willing participants
- **Limits:** persecution, internal disagreement, incomplete knowledge, temptation, trauma, and the Force's mystery
- **Internal disagreement:** share knowledge to protect people versus conceal it to prevent capture or misuse
- **PC leverage:** respectful study, protection, testimony, refusal of coercion, site stewardship, or dialogue among traditions
- **Claims:** `claim-star-wars-force`, `claim-star-wars-jedi`, `claim-star-wars-sith`, `claim-star-wars-force-traditions`.

## Era Matrix For Named Powers

| Named power | Stable public anchor | Question before use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Galactic Republic | Senate-based union | selected era, emergency powers, local reach, war status, and corruption |
| Galactic Senate | member-world representation | capital, membership, procedure, practical authority, and current regime |
| Galactic Empire | authoritarian military state | governor or unit, supply, occupation method, local collaborators, and timeline |
| Rebel Alliance | coalition resisting Empire | cell, command link, civilian relationship, secrecy, and named-event collision |
| New Republic | restored Senate and reconstruction government | exact interval, capital, demilitarization, remnant policy, and local capacity |
| First Order | Imperial successor movement | secrecy, territorial reach, public knowledge, and selected sequel-era date |
| Resistance | small force distinct from New Republic | support network, resources, public legitimacy, and spoiler ceiling |
| Jedi Order | historical Force institution | era, survival status, doctrine, membership, named masters, and public knowledge |
| Sith | distinct dark-side order | era, secrecy, lineage, active membership, and exact selected source |
| Bounty Hunters Guild | brokered contract ecosystem | local broker, jurisdiction, evidence, payment, and enforcement |
| Pyke or other syndicate | selected criminal organization | local branch, commodity, workers, coalition, coercion, and era |
| Mandalorian polity or clan | diverse historical communities | clan, creed, government, diaspora, occupation, and selected work |

A stable anchor never makes all details timeless.

## Default Opening Power Budget

For `star-wars-frame-broken-beacon`, instantiate only:

1. `star-wars-faction-reconstruction-mission`
2. `star-wars-faction-local-council`
3. `star-wars-faction-freight-cooperative`
4. `star-wars-faction-aid-network`

An Imperial remnant is an off-stage pressure until evidence or Session 0 selection makes a bounded command active. Jedi, Sith, famous Mandalorians, central New Republic leadership, and major syndicates begin off-stage.

## Power Action And Clock Contract

The default frame creates no faction clocks. The four opening powers' actions feed exactly two shared clocks: `star-wars-clock-corridor-isolation` and `star-wars-clock-emergency-jurisdiction`.

Each power still records a visible objective, next action, constituency, internal dissenter, external dependency, practical limit, and information gap. A custom frame chooses one clock model before play:

- at most two shared situation clocks, with power actions feeding them; or
- at most four bounded power clocks, one per active power, with no duplicate shared clock

Every clock needs an owner, visible condition, four or fewer segments, advancement evidence, affected people, reversibility, and redirect path. No power advances merely because players ignored it; its people need means, information, and opportunity.
